## Imagery

- If the tool supports image placement: use photos of **real Le Wagon students, teachers, and alumni** in learning/working settings. Warm, positive, diverse.
- No stock photography.
- Rounded corners 16–24px if images are used.
- No borders, strokes, or drop shadows on images.

## Brand guardrails

- Only the updated squared logo + three-window minibus. No old circular logo or four-window minibus.
- No gradients (the predefined social gradient is acceptable as an alternative to solid backgrounds, but keep it simple).
- Do not mix too many purple variants in one slide.
- No fake metrics or fabricated claims in any added text.
- Tone: direct, confident, practical, lifestyle-aspirational (not tourist-y), community-focused, evidence-based.
