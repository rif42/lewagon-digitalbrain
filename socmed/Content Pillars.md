# Le Wagon Content Pillars

Three core content pillars for the Le Wagon Bali data science bootcamp social media strategy. Each pillar maps to a funnel objective: convert skeptics, build credibility, or create belonging.

## 1. Why a Data Science Bootcamp Is Needed Now

**Objective:** Convert skeptics and justify the investment.

**Core message:** AI is changing work fast. A focused, project-based bootcamp is the fastest way to become employable in data — without a CS degree.

**Key arguments:**
- AI is automating tasks but creating demand for data-literate professionals.
- Companies collect more data than ever but lack people who can extract value from it.
- University programs teach theory; bootcamps teach shipping real projects in weeks.
- Data science is one of the fastest-growing career paths in Asia and globally.
- Career switchers can enter tech without starting from zero.

## 2. Day in the Life / Teaching Process

**Objective:** Build credibility and reduce uncertainty about the program.

**Core message:** The bootcamp is intense, structured, and hands-on. Students leave with real projects and a portfolio.

**Key arguments:**
- Daily rhythm: lectures, pair programming, challenges, project work.
- Curriculum is designed for beginners but produces job-ready output.
- Instructors are practitioners, not just teachers.
- Final projects simulate real client work.
- Demo Day is the public proof of what students can build.

## 3. Bali as the Place to Do It

**Objective:** Create community and belonging through lifestyle aspiration.

**Core message:** Bali offers the best environment to focus, learn, and reset — surrounded by nature, food, beaches, and a global remote-work community.

**Key arguments:**
- Lower cost of living lets students focus on learning instead of finances.
- Bali has a strong tech and digital nomad community.
- Natural environment supports focus and mental health during an intense bootcamp.
- Food, beaches, and weekend trips make the experience memorable.
- Cohort bonding is stronger when people live and explore together.
