# Le Wagon Post Ideas

Ready-to-use post ideas organized by the three content pillars for the Le Wagon Bali data science bootcamp.

## Pillar 1: Why a Data Science Bootcamp Is Needed Now

**Carousel: "5 signs you should consider a data bootcamp"**
1. You feel stuck in a non-tech role.
2. You want to work with AI, not be replaced by it.
3. You prefer building projects over reading textbooks.
4. You want a faster path than a second degree.
5. You keep seeing data roles but don't know how to qualify.

**Reel: "AI won't replace you — but someone who uses AI might"**
- Hook: AI is reshaping work.
- Body: The people who thrive are the ones who can ask the right questions, clean data, and build with AI tools.
- CTA: A data bootcamp teaches exactly that.

**Static post: "Companies are data-rich, insight-poor"**
- Visual: simple chart showing data growth vs. skilled worker gap.
- Caption: explain the opportunity gap and how bootcamps fill it.

**Quote card: Myth vs. reality**
- Myth: You need a math PhD to work in data.
- Reality: You need curiosity, consistency, and structured practice.

**Carousel: "What you actually learn in 9 weeks"**
- Python, SQL, data visualization, machine learning, AI APIs, final project.

## Pillar 2: Day in the Life / Teaching Process

**Reel: "A day at the Bali bootcamp"**
- Morning: lecture + live coding.
- Midday: healthy lunch break.
- Afternoon: pair programming on challenges.
- Evening: project work or beach wind-down.

**Carousel: "From zero to Demo Day"**
- Week 1-2: foundations.
- Week 3-5: projects and APIs.
- Week 6-7: machine learning.
- Week 8-9: final project + Demo Day prep.

**Story series: "Meet the instructor"**
- Short Q&A with a lead teacher about their background and teaching style.

**Reel: "This is what students build"**
- Screen recordings of final projects with quick explanations.
- Examples: Fake Data Detector, PlayerLens, Human Resources Analytics.

**Carousel: "What makes our teaching different"**
- Project-based learning.
- Peer collaboration.
- Real datasets.
- Code reviews.
- Career support.

## Pillar 3: Bali as the Place to Do It

**Reel: "Study in the morning, surf in the afternoon"**
- Campus shots in the morning, beach/surf clips in the afternoon.

**Carousel: "Bali food heaven for students"**
- Warung eats, smoothie bowls, local coffee, night markets.
- Caption: great food that doesn't break the student budget.

**Static post: "Cost of living breakdown: Bali vs. major cities"**
- Rent, food, transport, coworking comparison.
- Show how much further a bootcamp budget goes.

**Reel: "Weekend in Bali"**
- Beach trips, waterfalls, temples, sunset spots.
- Position bootcamp as a life experience, not just classes.

**Story series: "Bali coworking and tech meetups"**
- Highlight spaces like Hubud, Outpost, Dojo Bali.
- Show the remote-work and startup community.

**UGC prompt: "Show us your Bali study setup"**
- Repost student photos from cafes, villas, or campus.

**Carousel: "Why our students choose Bali"**
- Focus.
- Community.
- Nature.
- Affordable living.
- Memorable experience.
