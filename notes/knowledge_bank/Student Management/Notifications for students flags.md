# Notifications for students flags
## Summary
The student flags feature seems to lack notification functionality. When a flag is created (e.g., image rights refused, attendance issues). there is no pop-up or alert to notify relevant team members. This results in missed information and manual communication overhead. Also, BM don’t have acces to the page before kick-off days on the menu, they need to type the URL.
## Business Impact
- Program Managers and Batch Managers must manually check flag page to identify new flags
        -
- some infos can be discovered late
        -
## Context & Problem
**Exemple of current process:**
1. Kevin or Florine creates a student flag (e.g., "Image rights refused")
        1.
1. No notification is sent to relevant team members (PM/BM)
        1.
1. PM/BM must manually navigate to student profile to see the flag
        1.
1. OR Florine must manually message the team on Slack to inform them
        1.
1. Information can be missed entirely if no one checks or communicates
        1.
**Proposed solution:**
Implement notification system that:
- 1 week before the bootcamp: send a notification on manager slack channel on the batch with all flags
        -
- During the bootcamp: Sends a slack notification when a flag is created on the manager batch channel
        -
