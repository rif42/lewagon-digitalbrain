# Expose student performance Data to the students
### Release Note 🚀​
[[Expose challenge completion to students|🏁Expose challenge completion to students]]
---
### Problem description
It is a requirement for students to have completed **60% of the flashcards and 60% of the challenges** to receive a certificate of completion. In order to track their own progress with these goals, students have to ask Batch Managers for an update on the status. As a result, Batch Managers are flooded with requests for updates, responsible students feel stress over whether they are hitting the requirements, and less motivated students lose track of work and either try to cram all the work into the last couple of days or don’t get a certificate. We had people who were genuinely surprised to learn they had not hit the targets and, therefore, were not released a certificate.
> [!note] 
> **Feature:** to show flashcards and challenges completion percentages of a student on the student’s profile on Kitt or otherwise automate the process of students getting their current progress stats (for example through Slack messages)
### How it will help
- No need for BMs to constantly feedback the updates to the students - saves time and energy at 1-on-1s and beyond - efficiency ⬆️​
        -
- Responsible students will feel on track, and less motivated students will have a constant reminder of the need to work on their progress - potentially better bootcamp experience for both categories.
        -
- Less work put into chasing completion of those percentages and giving info about progress for the Programs team - efficiency ⬆️
        -
> [!note] 
> **Research** suggests that providing students with information on how well they are doing can change educational behaviour and stimulate self-regulation in learning. Attaching one example of such research ⬇️​
In Q4 2023 we experimented with having **BMs weekly send out messages to each student individually with their current level of %s** through the duration of the entire batch.
**Benefits we observed:**
- less admin work for the Ops team as we don’t have to chase students to release certificates - all the needed percentages were in place on day 1 of the career week for immediate certificate release
        -
- increased student motivation to complete challenges - us and BMs heard students refer to their percentages both in the context of keeping it up and sharing with others
        -
- less admin for BMs, as they used to get messages from students asking how their progress looked all the time
        -
- students didn’t have to cram work into a few last days or career week to get a certificate, and overall progress looks evenly distributed
        -
### Future potential
The feature can be improved by:
- adding to a mere reflection of student current percentages, a suggestion on what the percentage of a successful student looks like at a given point of a bootcamp (based on prior data from exemplary students).
        -
- adding info on how a given student compares to the rest of the batch to expand benchmarking information and add elements of competition that might drive performance.
        -
- adding feedback on how much a student engaged with materials this week as compared to the previous weeks to stimulate an even distribution of engagement.
