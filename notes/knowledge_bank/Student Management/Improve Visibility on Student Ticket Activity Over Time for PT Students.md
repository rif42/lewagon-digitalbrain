# Improve Visibility on Student Ticket Activity Over Time for PT Students
## Release note 🚀​
[[Enhance student tickets activity dashboard for PT students|📈Enhance student tickets activity dashboard for PT students]]
## Summary
> [!note] 
> TODO: Explain the problem in one sentence. ***Don’t think/talk about a solution ❌***​
Current ticket activity tracking in Kitt only shows the average number of tickets created by a student over the last five session, making it difficult to assess real engagement in PT & PT Flex batches were they have 2 to 3 ticket sessions per unit.

## Context & Problem
> [!note] 
> TODO: Explain in great details the context surrounding this issue
<details><summary>**Suggested Checklist**</summary>
</details>
In the current setup:
- Kitt shows an average number of tickets over the last 5 session only.
        -
- This metric is not the best for PT & PT Flex students, who have more session per unit than FT student.
        -
- There’s no view to see the total number of tickets submitted by a student during a unit, or over the full course.
        -
- Teachers, BMs and PMs struggle to spot students who haven't submitted tickets at all, making it harder to monitor progress and identify those who need support.
        -
Example:
- A student might submit 1 ticket every 2 weeks, but this is invisible due to the 5-day view limit.
        -
- Without historical ticket data or an overall count per unit, it’s impossible to distinguish between low, medium, and high engagement students.
        -
- For example, batch PT Flex 2045, it looks like no one is doing ticket even though they are:
        -
![](https://app.notion.com/image/attachment%3A0a354cd0-ad49-47d3-ae19-590ee511f1a7%3AScreenshot_2025-05-28_at_18.19.47.png?table=block&id=2018924a-f014-8042-91ff-c6b7839e939c&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
This makes the current ticket view misleading and inefficient, particularly in Flex formats. A more comprehensive view (e.g., total tickets per unit or all-time) would significantly improve intervention and support strategies.
