1. Scrape Linkedin alumni profiles to enrich Kitt’s database as you browse Linkedin

## Instructions
1. Please download the latest release 👆 of the extension on your computer.
Make sure to download it in your Documents folder and not in your Downloads folder to be sure to not remove it by mistake.
    ![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)
1. Unzip the file you downloaded by double clicking on it.
1. Go to your chrome extensions tab by typing chrome://extensions in your chrome browser address bar.
1. Please ensure that the Developer mode is **activated** on the right top corner of the chrome extensions tab
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F046f64a4-7a9c-4730-be53-8154e35370f7%2FGroup_3.png?table=block&id=e90efa7d-75e4-45ba-82d8-f5295110060d&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1330&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
1. Click on the Load unpacked button on the top left corner of the chrome extensions tab
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fe0b24c75-9dd2-4fad-9dc8-b2f98c433ac0%2FScreenshot_2022-01-04_at_16.46.13.png?table=block&id=a5e40c1a-57e4-4adc-8cfb-7d8f3a5eb1ab&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1330&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
1. Navigate to the folder where you have downloaded the extension, select the extension folder and click on Select.
    ![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)
1. For the extension to work properly (like scraping dates), you need to set Linkedin language to **English** 👉 <https://www.linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/settings/language>
1. That’s it! The extension is now ready to work 👌​
