# I. Onboarding
## 1. To onboard a new MiniJobber
The process goes as follows:
- We create the MiniJobber via Kitt
- He/She receives a form to fill out for the onboarding process (replacing the previous Google Spreadsheet). The link is sent via Slack from the current user who created the MiniJobber via Kitt.
***⚠️ At this point, no Slack notification is sent. You have to go on the conversation between you and the MiniJobber to see the message.***
Video of the creation of a MiniJobber:
> [!video] https://www.notion.com/help/images-files-and-media
- When he/she fills out the form, a Slack notification will be sent to #germany-teaching. The status is also displayed on the MiniJobber's profile page (kitt.lewagon.com/alumni/<github_username>/mini_jobber).
Video of the form & the Slack notification received in #germany-teaching:
> [!video] 
- We can then preview the contract and send it to the MiniJobber for docusigning.
    ![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)
- We will be notified when he/she signs the contract.
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fc9c03e33-a2bb-4103-8b49-2840745cd384%2FCapture_decran_2021-03-31_a_10.39.55.png?table=block&id=ae6a2711-dc0f-49fb-87b5-3d2c23317bd1&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1330&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)

*For existing contracts, we won't have the necessary information. This means at least email, fullname and address. To solve this, you can either edit it yourself or send the onboarding form. *

Once the necessary info are filled, we can create an extension:

# II. Voiding
To void a contract (only possible if the contract is sent but not yet signed):
- Click on the button
![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)
- Enter the reason for voiding the contract (max. 200 characters):
    ![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)
- You will be asked to confirm:
    ![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)
The MiniJobber will receive an email confirming the voiding of her contract.
