# Onboarding students on new batches
[No access](https://app.notion.com/p/47d1068031ba40bd95c460821b1abb8a?pvs=25)
## Demo
> [!video] https://www.notion.com/help/images-files-and-media
This new feature fixes a couple of issues
## How to send invitation before the start of a batch?
- Go on your batch's ATS and select 'GO' in the pipe
- Applicants who have received an invitation already will be marked as yellow while the ones who received the invitation and signed up on Kitt will be marked as green
- Click on Actions and select Send onboarding invitations
![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)
- A modal will pop-up and will allow you to select who you will want to send the onboarding invitation to. All the applications who are currently in DEPOSIT and GO are eligible to receive the invitation
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F3de39a82-2894-44d3-8c33-d0d0e93a13a6%2FScreenshot_2020-12-16_at_17.48.31.png?table=block&id=dbef4791-558c-42e8-a382-d8ace55e5040&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1330&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
- By default everyone who wasn't invited yet is selected. You can change the subject and body of the email manually or update the template in your ATS settings.
- The applicants will receive an email similar to the one below which will ask them to sign up on Kitt.
    ![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)
- When the student signs up Kitt, city managers will receive a slack notification on the admissions channel of their city. There is no need to approve them anymore. The student will be automatically invited to slack and the slack channel of their batch.
    ![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)
## Keeping the original flow
At the moment, during setup day, our students go through the whole setup of their computer and are then asked to sign up on kitt by going to <https://kitt.lewagon.com/onboarding>. The message below will be slightly modified and will ask them to make sure they haven't received an invitation from Le Wagon to sign up on Kitt.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fc1298ae1-72fd-4865-9bdf-cc2a4edf593f%2FScreenshot_2020-12-16_at_18.09.43.png?table=block&id=aa2b4472-8c1f-4345-95b5-aaa1ffe63edc&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
If city managers do not wish to adopt the new flow and send the onboarding invitation before hand that is perfectly fine. The old flow still works with some modifications.
When a student fills the form on Kitt to onboard themselves, city managers can receive one of three different messages
