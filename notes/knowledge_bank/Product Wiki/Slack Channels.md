# Slack Channels
---
Slack is an essential tool we use to communicate efficiently with our students, and to help teachers communicate with each other to ensure a great learning experience for everyone 👌​
We have two different channels for every bootcamp, and as a teacher you will be in both:
### Batch Channel
Student channels are called #️⃣batch-####-emil and their members include the batch’s students **and **teaching crew.
> [!note] 
> The batch channel is where you communicate with the whole class and share relevant information / resources during and around sessions.
In this channel, students will receive automatic notifications to remind them of things like:
- A new teacher joining the crew

- The time their class starts and the Zoom link

- The link to their daily quiz

### Teachers Channel
Teacher channels are called 🔒batch-####-teachers and their members include the batch’s teaching crew **only. **
> [!note] 
> The teacher channel is where the teaching crew can communicate with each other and share relevant information / resources during and around sessions.
In this channel, teachers will receive automatic notifications to remind them of things like:
- A new teacher joining the crew
- A link to fill out a **teacher debrief** with the teachers’ feedback about the session
