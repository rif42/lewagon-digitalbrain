### Build agents for your own needs
---
The idea is for you to build the best agents that can help you either:
- Get rid of repetitive tasks by automating them.
- Have a partner to develop your ideas and turn them into real and solid projects.
- Guide you through technical tasks if you need help or lack some knowledge.
> [!note] 
> Building an agent can take a bit of time. This is **time investment**, is in your best interest to take the time to build something solid and useful.
### ✌️ **Two tips? **
---
- [ ] Framing the instructions for your agent
- [ ] Updating your agent after a while
