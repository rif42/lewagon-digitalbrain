# Generic Flyer
> [!note] 
> If you need to make any alterations for local adaptations, please reach out to the Brand & Content team for support.
## About
A print-first flyer presenting Le Wagon's offerings, aimed at providing a high-level overview of bootcamps and other training programs. The goal is to balance key information with an engaging design to encourage potential leads to scan a QR code for more detailed info.
⚠️ **Please note:** Print in small quantities only (we all know how fast things can change at Le Wagon)
