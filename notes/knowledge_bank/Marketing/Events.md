# Other platforms
In www admin panel, you can browse to “Local Events” and create a new one.
url: <https://www.lewagon.com/v4/admin/resources/local_events/new>
Here you can fill out the event’s details, which, if published, will be published on your city’s page.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fd2317690-e7d2-4bdf-84cc-1a721dfc783a%2FUntitled.png?table=block&id=09391bcb-6ae7-42c3-9917-d4e16f6b000d&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
If you want to use Meetup, Livestorm or any other platforms, and you want to display them on your city’s page, simply create your event in the admin’s panel, and add the event url (meetup’s for instance) in the Event url field. That’s it.
