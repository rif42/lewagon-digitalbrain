****A. Q4 delays hit us so hard 🔨 and our processes are not made to launch fast
**
We **struggled in Q1** under the pressure of:
- delays in product launches
- delay in our own roadmap (due to product launch but not only) creating a big backlog to catch up on in Q1 for Q2
- accumulated problems with CRM (with no owner) and more complexities with more products
- people not joining on time or not being present 100% of the time (double work for some)
- central le wagon launch process and marketing processes not adapted to fast launches (with uncertainty)
**B. 🌤️ Silver lining: we’ve delivered so many important project this quarter and the results are JUST a LITTLE LATE
We managed to **👏🏽 🟢
- Launch Skill courses with 96% Atteignment in FR
- [Global OKRs](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1e5aC3Ds0cqsZ6OiJl9y_JHMHjASPCcEpyxz5d34BG0c/edit#gid=1896475753) show we’re late to date in applies for web dev, DA, DS by a little vs H1 time spent (missing roughly 1 mo of work) **
**- Keep our CRM afloat without an owner and with a limited amount of mistakes made 👏🏽 (major shoutout)
- Launch an A+ B2B website 👏🏽
- Launch an A+ NY campaign with an unseen level of reach & Traffic impact 👏🏽
- Launch a new social media strategy with record engagement so far (doubled) 👏🏽
- Launch A+ brand assets like syllabus, financing doc, new videos ready to be posted 👏🏽
- understand how to better serve regions with better trackers 👏🏽
- Launch ground breaking new channels like Referral, Free content and more manager oriented tests that are the future 👏🏽
- Recognize the need to create a testing machine by persona x product leveraging past content & AI 👏🏽
- Finally pausing on the website & channels to get a clearer view of what works and what doesn’t (must be finalized)👏🏽
**In this organic evolving situation, we haven’t been able to **🔴**
**- focus on what matters in the noise aka the quality of data and reporting (which have not been prioritized high enough ⚠️)
- Isolating what we can control and what we can’t (important to focus on what can be moved vs what needs to be escalated)
- understanding the reality of our markets (need to diversify persona in crisis) and balance brand and brand <> acquisition
**💡: Conclusion
**- We can’t pilot our business without data
- We can’t launch a product and figure out how to market it if we don’t have robust testing capabilities
Both should be prioritized high.
