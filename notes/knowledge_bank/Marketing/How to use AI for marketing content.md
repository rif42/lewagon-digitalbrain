# How to use AI for marketing content
> [!note] 
> As Marketers, AI is our new best colleague and we should all leverage it to:
- **Understand** our environment, define our persona, gain insights
- **Brainstorm** and find great ideas for content, partnerships, events
- **Improve** our copy, optimize our SEO strategy
This playbook aims to guide the utilization of AI in three key areas: persona and strategy development, content ideation, and copy creation.
Have a question? @Camille Hemet
## 1. **Persona and Strategy Development**
---
### a. **Market Research:**
> [!note] 
> Use AI-powered tools to analyze your market, your competitors, and your key selling point.
**Example of a prompt: **
1. *List the 5 main competitors of Le Wagon in the UK*
1. *Conduct a full analysis of [competitor company name] and identify the competitive advantages and disadvantages of their product.*
1. *Conduct a full analysis of Le Wagon and identify the competitive advantages and disadvantages of their product.*
1. *Based on these 2 analyses (Le Wagon and General Assembly), what's the key selling point of Le Wagon to convince people living in the UK to join this bootcamp rather than General Assembly's one?*

### b. **Customer Persona Creation:**
> [!note] 
> Identify target audience segments, blockers, insights, motivations.
Collect demographic and psychographic data to create detailed customer profiles.
**Example of a prompt: **
*Act as a marketing expert. Create 4 personas who could be interested in joining a Le Wagon tech bootcamp. For each persona, include:*
- *Behaviors*
- *Motivations*
- *Blockers and no-go reasons*

## 2. Content ideation

### a. Leverage the existing
> [!note] 
> Find ideas on how to leverage existing content and test AI tool to transform/adapt your piece of content
**Example of a prompt:**
You’re a content Manager at Le Wagon and you wrote [this blog article](https://blog.lewagon.com/career/software-engineer/). How can you leverage this piece of content?

> [!note] 
> Optimize your SEO positioning on keywords by getting ideas for blog posts and content.

Cluster this list of keywords according to funnel stages whether they are top of the funnel, middle of the funnel or bottom of the funnel keywords.

### b. Blog post for SEO
**Example of a prompt for a content brief:**
*Please create a detailed SEO-focused content brief to target the keyword {keyword} with the blog post name -> {blog post name}. Include objective or a goal action for readers, target audience, target keyword and related keywords, title, meta description, content format, tone, outline (headers and sub-headers, including word count), ballpark length, and internal and external link opportunities for the article. Also, include other relevant SEO tips from both sources to be implemented.*
*Please provide the brief in a code block without any HTML styling or formatting for easy copying.*
