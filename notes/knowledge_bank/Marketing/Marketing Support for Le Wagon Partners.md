# Marketing Support for Le Wagon Partners
# Overview
Welcome to the marketing support overview for Le Wagon partners. This presentation outlines the comprehensive marketing support provided by the headquarters to our partners worldwide. Our goal is to empower you with the tools and resources needed to successfully market your operations locally while maintaining a consistent global brand.
---
## Marketing Hub in Notion
### Access and Purpose
- **Access**: Fully accessible to everyone.
- **Purpose**: Documentation of processes, best practices, campaigns, events, CRM, product marketing, content, marketing calendar, and marketing team directory.
- **Goal**: Maximize visibility on central marketing initiatives and enable franchises to replicate marketing efforts autonomously.
- **Release**: Expected by the end of the quarter.
---
## Support Slack Channel
### #marketing-ops
- **Purpose**: A dedicated channel for marketing operations questions.
- **Benefits**:
        - Collective intelligence through shared questions and answers.
        - Increased reactivity and collaboration.

## Blog (Wordpress)
### Content Creation and Management
- **Access**: Create content on [blog.lewagon.com](http://blog.lewagon.com/).
- **Central Team Responsibilities**:
        - New template creation.
        - Content deletion for SEO, performance, brand alignment, or legal reasons.

## Other Media
### Mini Websites and Lead Attribution
- **Management**: Central team manages mini websites (e.g., [start.lewagon.com](http://start.lewagon.com/)).
- **Usage**: Franchises can use these in their marketing strategy.
- **Lead Attribution**: Qualified leads based on country or interested campus.

## Content Utilization
### Central and Localized Content
- **Central Content**: Free to use in marketing strategies.
- **Localization**: Franchises responsible for localizing content; not integrated into automated CRM workflows.
