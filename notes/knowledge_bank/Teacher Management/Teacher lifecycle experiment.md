# Teacher lifecycle experiment
## 💡 Overview - Solution ideation
*Describe what is the problem and what potential solutions we might have to solve it.*
### 🔍 Assumptions
*Write all the assumptions we might have towards this solution. Try to find the riskiest solutions first, the ones that would make the experiment fail for sure.*
---
- Assumption 1: *ei → Teachers need this tool to do that or Our teachers/batch managers/etc… have x,y,z problem*
- Our batch managers and program managers don’t have a clear idea of the lifecycle of a teacher at Le Wagon
- TA’s need a 5 step training to become lead lecturer. (Basic training / rehearsal / Livecode / lecture)
- Potential TA need at least one ticket role play to start working as TA’s
- Teachers need a certificate of training for bootcamp accreditations
- Teachers in the professional growth stage need follow up evaluations and 1:1 retrospectives
---
### 🥼 Hypothesis
*Build hypothesis around the above assumptions. In other words, turn the assumptions into sentences by following the examples in *[[Growth through experiments|this guide.]]
---
1. We believe that Batch managers and Program managers don’t know when to train a teacher because there is no clear process which will in a long term make any guidelines or tools ineffective. If we clearly test and validate a teacher lifecycle, program management processes will improve and consequently, teaching quality mentions in the NPS feedbacks.
1. We also believe that teachers quality is not consistent because teams don’t have a clear process to turn TA’s into lead lecturers. If we test and validate a process, teacher quality mentions in NPS will improve.
1. Moreover, we believe that bootcamps can’t get government accreditations because they need a teacher certification in its lifecycle. If we create an official certificate at the end of teacher’s trainings, Government accreditations will improve.
1. Finally, we believe that teacher’s growth is inexistent because bootcamps don’t the tools to work on and track teacher’s professional growth. if we build and test a teacher growth process with evaluations, retrospectives and workshops we believe that teachers turnover will decrease and teaching quality will increase.
