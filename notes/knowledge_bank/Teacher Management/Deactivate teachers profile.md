# Deactivate teachers profile
## Release Note 🚀​
It was decided to not build a flag system however we improve the workflow and experience to deactivate a teacher profiles.
[[Deactivate teacher profiles|👨‍🏫Deactivate teacher profiles]]
---
## Summary
> [!note] 
> TODO: Explain the problem in one sentence. ***Don’t think/talk about a solution ❌***​
Teachers sometimes display toxic behavior towards Le Wagon staff or are not recommendable. With our B2C, B2U, B2B, and Online programs, it can be difficult to pass on this information before they get staffed elsewhere. Additionally, new staff members or others may not be aware of a teacher’s history and might contact them for projects, leading to a poor experience.
## Business Impact
> [!note] 
> TODO : Explain business impact of your issue
<details><summary>**Suggested Checklist**</summary>
</details>
- Teachers are often staffed on a training, as certification jurors, or for workshops without knowing their history. This can result in a bad experience for staff and clients, sometimes even turning them into detractors.
        -
- It improves the overall image and boosts NPS. Beyond that, it streamlines processes by working only with motivated teachers and fosters a more positive environment.
        -
- For leads, a bad workshop experience can make us lose potential future students, which happens more often than we think. It also wastes ops teams' time managing difficult teachers who tend to be demanding and raise many concerns. In B2C, this kind of teacher can significantly lower NPS. For example, in DA Paris, since removing certain people from the crew, we've reached a record NPS for the program. Additionally, it can demoralize an entire teaching team—other teachers and TAs may lose motivation or even become detractors themselves in the worst-case scenario.
        -
## Context & Problem
> [!note] 
> TODO: Explain in great details the context surrounding this issue
<details><summary>**Suggested Checklist**</summary>
</details>
From my experience, I tried to remove detractor teachers from Le Wagon, but they were still being staffed elsewhere and continued to take up a lot of time for the concerned ops team. Events that should have been simple to organize became stressful and problematic because of these individuals. The goal is to share this information to prevent such issues.
