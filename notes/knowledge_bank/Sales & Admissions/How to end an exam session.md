# How to end an exam session?
Now that all candidates have been assessed, it is time to generate all your jury minutes and send to candidates their results before closing the exam session.
> [!note] 
> **If your candidates are not assessed yet, please** [[How to assess candidates|go back to the previous step]].
## Summary
---
- [[How to end an exam session|Generate jury minutes]]
- [[How to end an exam session|Generate certification documents]]
- [[How to end an exam session|Send documents]]
- [[How to end an exam session|Close and archived the exam session]]
> [!note] 
> **All the following actions can be taken in the Candidates tab.**
## Generate jury minutes
---
Start by generating the juries minutes either individually by clicking on the **Generate minutes** action in the jury dropdown (1), or all of them at once clicking the dedicated **batch action** (2).
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F4dc40d5a-0cf3-4b7b-8e51-65f45e124f74%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_16.00.04.png?table=block&id=4f19f137-c120-4914-bf19-66eb6e9da928&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F1dc3d3f5-b0eb-4a35-b5a2-f9022b96a8da%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_16.02.21.png?table=block&id=3c66524d-486a-463d-aaab-d9029074a1bd&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Once the minutes has been generated, you can open it and access the **.pdf **you must save and store. The generated document will still be available even after closing the exam session.

## Generate certification documents
---
Certification documents are a bundle of two legal documents:
- The **Validated blocks document**
- The** Certification document**
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fab7e8278-b2fb-42b4-aed4-a02fb1164f44%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_16.29.35.png?table=block&id=5abf37b7-23c9-4abf-9ec9-3a737e4151da&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=640&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
---
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F4e318a18-f790-4f23-b0a4-9663422262ea%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_16.32.54.png?table=block&id=bf6bd90c-b60b-42c1-8f97-53914fdbc4ac&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=640&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
> [!note] 
> **If a candidate gets its certification**, both documents are generated.
**In the opposite case**, only the *validated blocks document* will be produced mentioning explicitly that the candidate failed the exam.
You can generate documents individually using the main action at the end of each candidate row. The action is automatically updated based on the candidate results.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F6d4a8224-228c-45d7-80ba-6e42c3fc23cb%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_16.49.58.png?table=block&id=b9903e94-46a7-4593-bbcf-5c729532f2a9&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Once the documents have been generated, you can access them via the main action. In case of a certified student, the validated block document link is accessible in the dropdown.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F0199c027-fe7f-45d5-83d0-b777c42fb397%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_16.48.51.png?table=block&id=0c1d92a0-54d5-43dd-a43e-7bedc98b4627&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
As for the minutes you can also use the dedicated **batch action **to generate all the certification documents at once.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F8ed05355-fe65-4d0d-98d4-e6bba9d615a5%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_16.42.52.png?table=block&id=93561657-e559-4bf8-a469-bb8aab8828ac&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
## Send documents
---
Once again, you can send the certification documents individually to each candidates or use the batch action dropdown to send everything in one click.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Ffe2f53ea-0188-48ca-a806-9ef591cedeed%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_16.54.14.png?table=block&id=ae234499-c0fd-44b3-8aae-78c520b9aa1e&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
- **Certified candidates** will receive the following email with both generated documents attached.
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Ff2e69673-561e-4bcb-b9b1-4fe49c23d3bd%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_16.55.05.png?table=block&id=292e14a4-e5e7-455c-974b-6480d842e8b2&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1330&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
- **Candidates **whose failed the exam will receive the following email with only the block validated document attached.
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fc07ae708-a55f-4d12-8b8c-2920e3cf9e14%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_16.55.29.png?table=block&id=3241001f-760e-4807-ad73-2fbb6414d134&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1330&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
> [!note] 
> You won’t be able to send the candidate’s result twice.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fbfa7d726-6054-42f6-a1a4-c54f539f20b4%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_17.00.41.png?table=block&id=a29ae074-6efd-40cf-92a5-0b8876d2ff59&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
## Close and archived the exam session
---
After** generating juries minutes**, **generating certification documents** and **send them**, you can finally **close** the exam session.
