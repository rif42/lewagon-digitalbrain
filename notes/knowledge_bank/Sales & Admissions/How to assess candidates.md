# How to assess candidates?
This documentation describe the assessment process for exam session jurors.
# Before assessment step
After being onboarded, a juror will land on the following page.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F627a089d-dd4b-4c53-b97f-9b70b09475fa%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_15.18.14.png?table=block&id=48f4a4c7-f957-45fa-92b3-1fdd7d563062&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
At this stage, the juror don’t have anything to do except:
- Sign the certificate of experience sent by DocuSign (only if you have chosen one during [[How to create an exam session|the session creation]])
- Complete their company, job title and signature (only if you are running an RNCP exam - these information are mandatory for [[How to create an exam session|the jury’s minute]])
If one of this information is missing, the juror will see these alerts on each page:
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fe834c7e7-5b93-404c-ad4b-22d8ffb42c2a%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_15.20.52.png?table=block&id=5cd33fc3-2209-4288-9cea-20f3187254bb&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=640&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)

# During assessment step
Right after closing the last challenge, the assessment step will be released and jurors can start to assess candidates assigned to their jury.
By going back on the platform, they will see this new screen:
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fbcb6150a-7377-496c-8933-afe69ad5e454%2FScreenshot_2022-03-25_at_18.03.12.png?table=block&id=8b982366-cc56-415c-a52c-f88a08d423f0&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
From now, each juror will have only a subset of the candidates to assess (and each candidates will be assess by only one juror).
The candidates a juror as to assess appear on the top of the list and are highlighted with a light grey background.
## Request access to candidates repos
If the challenges to assess contain work realized on GitHub, the juror will need to first Request access to candidates repos.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F7fb2b3dc-d3bd-4dde-b98f-258cad520a2c%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_15.30.59.png?table=block&id=32bd6e0e-7e50-4f45-9875-ca154e3c05f0&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
As soon as the juror will be invited to candidats repos, they will be able to click on the Grade link on the right of each candidate’s row.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fef833b93-e83b-4a3c-89d8-f2a72eb80571%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_15.45.31.png?table=block&id=d74ee6df-2d3d-43bf-9a35-cb01ac16f6ab&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
# Grade a candidate
By clicking on the Grade button, a juror will access to the page to grade a candidate. Here is how it looks like:
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F27b5384d-091b-47ba-a34a-68bc3523c2f4%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_17.22.28.png?table=block&id=3de0d34f-3b26-4fb7-9e65-b7114f2b3075&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
1. Name of the candidate being assessed
1. Button to open the notes panel
1. The subject assigned to the candidate (if exam has subject)
1. The first item to assess
1. The candidate submissions
## Grading system
For each skill, the juror will need to choose between Denied, In process of acquisition and Acquired.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F6075d575-960a-4983-80b3-925973ce7e3a%2FScreenshot_2022-01-26_at_11.53.43.png?table=block&id=bb86f89b-82ed-4988-ab90-29842e366260&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Each juror should choose between these 3 options for each skill to assess.
## Notes
During the assessment process, each juror can take notes about the candidate. Just click on the Notes button on the top right of the page to open the side panel to write down your notes.
![](https://file.notion.com/f/f/2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f/4af5739c-6124-4c3b-972c-20faad0e1c8f/Kapture_2022-01-26_at_12.02.40.gif?table=block&id=094cecc9-7c03-414c-b44b-3b8429af2d31&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&expirationTimestamp=1784851200000&signature=qOMrwVf-4prW8lEOYwuZa3KDQw3Rb40dB2sc9NHQRA4)
> [!note] 
> This notes will never be show to the candidate. There are only here to help the president and the jurors during the deliberations.
As the deliberations process can occur few days after the grading it allows the juror to keep in mind their reflexions about candidate’s work.
# Deliberations
When jurors have finish to rate each candidate of their jury, they need to set a meeting in order to review candidates grades and to deliver or not the certification.
**Only the president of a jury will be able to open the deliberations** by clicking on the button on the top right of their screen:
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fd5e5dea3-208c-4d35-b28f-4e8a95cb3d6f%2FScreenshot_2022-01-26_at_12.20.09.png?table=block&id=0920e912-a2d4-479a-8d29-d34c2520701c&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
## President role
During the deliberations, **only the president** will be able to access to the deliberations page of each candidate.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F46dff9fe-8ef5-45c6-ac2d-0cab83a4c8a8%2FScreenshot_2022-01-26_at_12.23.42.png?table=block&id=c193c8aa-7ab5-49d8-8120-807b4032be51&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
For this reason, we recommend to the president to share their screen to other jurors, or to conduct deliberations during an in person meeting.
## Conduct of the deliberations
When the president click on the Deliberation link of a candidate, they will fall on this page:
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fd6d0b961-f801-4194-b5fc-1e86b116fbd3%2FScreenshot_2022-03-25_at_18.07.20.png?table=block&id=dbb33ae2-5c41-47a5-becf-ee79c74dc8d2&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Here is the steps to follow to deliver certification or not to a candidate.
### 1. Grant blocks
For each block, jurors have to debate in order to decide if a candidate as acquired the block or not. After the debates, the president grant the block or not.
Jurors can base their judgement on the validation threshold minimum value indicator.
![](https://file.notion.com/f/f/2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f/c0c6052f-c678-4c7b-9641-9ff024931aca/Kapture_2022-01-26_at_12.34.20.gif?table=block&id=d164fb00-6e2e-44ce-8987-09b037161e9c&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&expirationTimestamp=1784851200000&signature=O5YGvZ1P1_oomHNzg8wN-IfgU8Sjl3NSmgPLe97wvNs)
![](https://file.notion.com/f/f/2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f/0aa64c4a-9b18-4ec5-a814-3cd5bd4db83c/Kapture_2022-01-26_at_12.41.13.gif?table=block&id=81900876-b526-4af0-8459-272498bf1f2c&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&expirationTimestamp=1784851200000&signature=vIRAVNLc3SoNAegXmBzbj2e3I3-DrCteLioxt7FQtW8)
> [!note] 
> **Update candidate’s grade**
The president is now able to update each candidate's grade during the deliberation in order to harmonize the certification process.
To to it, they just has to click on Update candidate’s grades above the candidate’s results.
### 2. Add a message to candidate
After reviewing each block, the president (with the help of the members of their jury) will have to leave a general assessment for the candidate.
It could be congrats, incentives or advices but also explanations if the candidate didn't obtain the certification.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F3c9bca99-9b63-4fa6-b42a-dd508e9b3cca%2FScreenshot_2022-01-26_at_12.46.14.png?table=block&id=0ad3ceec-6a35-4314-9f70-20ef72fa9cb5&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
> [!note] 
> The general assessment left to the candidate will be displayed on the certification document.
### 3. Deliver certification
The last step is to deliver or not the certification to the candidate by checking on the Grant certification to candidate checkbox and to Submit the deliberation.
![](https://file.notion.com/f/f/2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f/da3c014f-50e2-4d84-9894-9a4de575967d/Kapture_2022-01-26_at_12.51.48.gif?table=block&id=02e77ff3-748b-4e3b-8220-2b63dcbde3ca&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&expirationTimestamp=1784851200000&signature=iWiGnmZCCQLV7HxN-FFW4ViHb2Iz8Z-0DJvGHe8PgaI)
## Close the deliberations
When all the deliberations have been done for each candidate, the president should return on the candidate list and click on Close deliberations button on the top right of the screen.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fb72c8f2d-1f9b-4190-bc45-d77bbe34893a%2FScreenshot_2022-01-26_at_12.54.11.png?table=block&id=731fa490-fe3f-42f8-8b89-e579a6471785&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
