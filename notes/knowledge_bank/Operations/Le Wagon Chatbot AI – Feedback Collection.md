## 🎙️ How to Interact with the Chatbot
- Visit the website or use the iframe above
- Ask any questions a prospective student or curious visitor might have.
- Explore different flows and try using casual or varied wording.
- If something seems off or unclear—report it!
---
## 💬 How to Share Feedback
Please use the following structure to keep feedback actionable and easy to review:
➤ What did you test?
*(e.g., “Scholarship flow”, “What is the length of the Data Analytics bootcamp?”, “Where is the Tokyo campus?”)*
➤ What was the issue?
*(e.g., “The response was outdated”, “It didn’t understand my question”, “Too generic”)*
➤ Your suggestion (if any):
*(e.g., “Include details about part-time options”, “Add a clarification for financing options in France”)*
➤ Screenshot(s):
*(Optional but helpful—screenshots can show exact messages or bugs.)*
