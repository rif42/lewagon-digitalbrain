# Best practices for filming locations / B-roll
## **🌆 Angles, framing, lighting, settings for environment shots.
**
### **What to capture:**
- Classroom shots (students working, coding on laptops).
- Campus environment (hallways, coffee breaks).
- City/location vibes (landmarks, streets near campus).
- Event moments (applause, speaker on stage, networking).
### **Angles:**
- Mix **wide shots (establishing scene)** + **close-ups (details)**.
- Add movement (pan left/right, tilt up/down, walking shots).
- Keep clips short (3–5 seconds each).
### **Lighting:**
- Film at **golden hour** outdoors if possible.
- Indoors: use natural light or well-lit spaces.
