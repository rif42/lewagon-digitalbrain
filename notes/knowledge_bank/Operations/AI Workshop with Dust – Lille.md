# About the project
### Context & Problem
As AI tools become increasingly central to business operations, professionals in the Lille region lack accessible, hands-on opportunities to discover and experiment with these technologies in a local setting. Le Wagon Lille, in partnership with Dust, aims to bridge this gap by hosting a community evening event that brings together real-world experience sharing and direct practice — making AI concretely useful and approachable for local professionals and decision-makers.
### The project
Organize a 2-hour evening event in Lille, building on the format successfully deployed in Paris. The event will blend:
- **1 hour of presentation**: concrete AI use cases, company experience-sharing (with a potential Dust client as testimonial), and a presentation of the Dust platform
- **1 hour of hands-on workshop**: guided by a Dust expert, participants will build their first AI agent
Le Wagon handles event logistics and organization. Dust provides the expert facilitator and potentially a client speaker.
