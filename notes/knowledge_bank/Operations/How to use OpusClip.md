# How to use OpusClip
## ⁉️ What is OpusClip?
OpusClip is an AI video editing tool that helps to streamline the video editing process. You can use this tool to:
- convert long videos to short ones
- reformat the size
- use pre-set templates
- add Le Wagon logos, intros, outros, CTAs, overlays, subtitles and other animations
- clean the audio (remove pauses, background noises)
- add AI audio voiceovers
You can then either export your videos, or post directly from OpusClip onto social channels.

## **🏁 Quickstart guide:**
1. Upload your raw video file.
1. Select **aspect ratio** (vertical for socials).
1. Choose a template (below).
1. Adjust captions, branding, overlays.
1. Export and save to shared drive/Notion card.
## **🎥 Available templates (recommended):**
- **Talking Head (with captions)** → for testimonials, tips, tutorials.
- **Dynamic Zoom Cuts** → for social ads, short announcements.
- **Subtitled Storytelling** → for case studies & longer clips.
- **Split Screen** → side-by-side comparisons or before/after videos.
