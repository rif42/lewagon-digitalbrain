# About the project
## Context & Problem
The market is saturated with AI content, but there is a lack of **practical and actionable formats** tailored to Go-To-Market professionals (Marketing, Sales, Customer Success, RevOps). Existing masterclasses tend to be too theoretical and don't allow participants to actually build tools during the session.
Le Wagon and Dust also have **complementary communities** (tech professionals, growth, ops, marketers) that have not yet been activated together through a live co-hosted format. There is a clear opportunity to create a recurring series that combines Le Wagon's pedagogical expertise with Dust's product depth.
## The Project
A **series of 4 monthly masterclasses** co-hosted by Le Wagon and Dust, focused on building AI agents applied to business functions. The format is interactive and "follow-along": participants build workflows in real time alongside the presenter.

**Platform:** Registration and streaming on Learn (Dust's side). Dust shares the participant list afterwards.
