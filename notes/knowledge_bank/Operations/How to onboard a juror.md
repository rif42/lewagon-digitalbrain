# How to onboard a juror?
Now you have created at least one jury, let’s see how to onboard a juror.
If you haven’t created a jury yet, please go back to the previous step by following link bellow.
[How to create a jury?](https://app.notion.com/p/lewagon/How-to-create-a-jury-25988183c304477d959ccc653d3b0896?pvs=25)
# Onboard a juror
## Onboard a juror from a previous session
On a specific jury, click on Add juror to Jury’s name to make the onboarding form appears.
![](https://file.notion.com/f/f/2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f/abf1206c-95c8-4200-ac96-33e189d2fcff/Kapture_2022-01-24_at_18.03.40.gif?table=block&id=50f1582d-9626-4299-8703-434cf46e941f&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&expirationTimestamp=1784851200000&signature=zX2ZA2RM0RTCOp6yq0VcjF5FtPcoHvZZNomoKYcEOGA)
Then click on the dropdown and start typing the name of the juror you want to onboard. When you have selected the juror you want to onboard, click on Add juror.
![](https://file.notion.com/f/f/2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f/5e8772b6-ea41-4aca-b21c-b245dc6e85ed/Kapture_2022-01-24_at_18.01.00.gif?table=block&id=d11b52e1-1fc9-433e-a243-a6bf8d7349c8&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&expirationTimestamp=1784851200000&signature=VSji3371uXWXfQxRh1-srkqsaXrmWHrpiLQE2IJZg4g)
## Onboard a brand new juror
Once again, start by clicking on Add juror to <Jury’s name>.
Then instead of using the dropdown to select an existing juror, click on onboard a new juror.
Enter they email, first name and last name and click on Add juror.
![](https://file.notion.com/f/f/2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f/67c230ef-350c-41ab-8c8e-adaa3949c467/Kapture_2022-01-25_at_11.39.02.gif?table=block&id=68d37e60-7058-4bd8-8c8e-bba229a44ea4&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&expirationTimestamp=1784851200000&signature=QWOF7GJSGIGRR76po5HPyt1WatLE46mPmnMI1X8l7jg)
> [!note] 
> An email will be send to any new juror you onboard to invite them to the platform.
An Invited badge will be display next to the juror name until they connect for the first time.
# Choose a president for each jury
After onboarding your juror, you may have noticed this warning message on top of each jury (only if you activated the juries during the [[How to create an exam session|creation of your session]]).
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F4b7f3593-b231-4349-9519-3e94d4afb2ae%2FScreenshot_2022-01-24_at_18.08.35.png?table=block&id=1721720a-5a3b-4664-b7e3-cb50bcfdf0ea&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
This means that you haven’t choose any president for your jury.
To choose a president, click on the 3 dots on the right of a juror row and select Set as president.
![](https://file.notion.com/f/f/2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f/8e842413-1a3f-4f75-9405-a3a78caa4c78/Kapture_2022-01-25_at_11.57.11.gif?table=block&id=215cb505-bfb4-4fa7-b665-15a09a37e68b&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&expirationTimestamp=1784851200000&signature=G8nGtKbWjPpPe8SyHOEvkFk9W4TXJ6mwKKnJ7B4-uR8)
> [!note] 
> President has a specific role during the assessment process. They is the one who will have access to the button to grant certification to the candidates.
# Exempt a juror of grading
For some specific use case, you might give access to candidates submissions to a juror without asking them to rate the candidates.
In that case, you can exempt the juror from the grading process. To do that, click on the 3 dots on the right of a juror row and select Exempted from grading.
![](https://file.notion.com/f/f/2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f/5635a887-abc3-4578-a283-857909a3c2df/Kapture_2022-01-25_at_12.14.23.gif?table=block&id=67ad2fb1-87ca-43e3-a457-33c48d92ce08&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&expirationTimestamp=1784851200000&signature=SCuMUoAYs4wG7q3JomyKAXKH-kV_G7EZFeT9G5_juiI)
# Remove a juror
If you need to remove a juror, click on the 3 dots on the right of a juror row and select Remove.
![](https://file.notion.com/f/f/2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f/b61bf197-f06c-45db-9efe-9d1187eb5d52/Kapture_2022-01-25_at_12.18.47.gif?table=block&id=a77d795a-90f6-489e-9da2-6d428d55baff&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&expirationTimestamp=1784851200000&signature=LXumbrqd9XhZZSYasDi4wjX073ss7bctPRDoOJoa2tk)
