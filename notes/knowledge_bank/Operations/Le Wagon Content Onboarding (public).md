**Le Wagon** is a global leader in immersive tech training.
**Our mission is to train people from all backgrounds in web development, data and no-code, give them the best learning experience of their life, and help them accelerate in their career.**
Founded in Paris in 2013, Le Wagon now has campuses in +**40 cities across 25 countries**, including global tech hubs such as **Berlin, London, Tokyo, Singapore, and Sao Paulo**. Most importantly, Le Wagon is a community of **29,000+ graduates** - from all walks of life, including many entrepreneurs (who created 200+ tech startups and raised $1B+ globally), career changers, and digital nomads.
**Co-Founding team**
Le Wagon was created in 2013 by Boris Paillard, Romain Paillard and Sébastien Saunier with the aim to build a program to teach people the skills and tools to bring their ideas to life as they felt that traditional degrees’ courses were failing to provide students with these new tech skills.

## Editorial strategy overview
---
**Your role and responsibilities**
As an expert content creator, your role is to leverage your specialized knowledge to craft insightful and engaging content that aligns with our brand’s editorial strategy. You’ll be responsible for:
> [!note] 
> **Proposing relevant topics:** Continuously suggesting article subjects that resonate with our evergreen tech themes and audience interests.
---
> [!note] 
> **Creating high-quality content:** Writing well-researched, original articles that reflect your expertise, with a focus on delivering value to our readers.
> [!note] 
> **Collaborating with the marketing team:** Working closely with our Marketing and Content teams to ensure your articles align with our overall strategy and meet our quality standards.
---
> [!note] 
> **Meeting deadlines:** Managing your time effectively to produce content within the agreed timelines, recognizing that this work often complements your primary professional commitments.
