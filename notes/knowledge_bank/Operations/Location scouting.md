# Location scouting
## **📍 Planning to shoot indoors or outdoors? Things to consider & ideas on where to shoot your videos.
**
### **🏫 Indoors:**
- Pros: Controlled environment, no weather risk, easier sound.
- Consider: Lighting (near windows, avoid harsh shadows), background (clean, uncluttered, with Le Wagon branding if possible).
- Ideas: Classrooms, common areas, event spaces, coworking-style setups.
### **🏙️ Outdoors:**
- Pros: Natural light, dynamic backgrounds, city vibe.
- Consider: Noise (traffic, wind), permits if filming in public, changing light.
- Ideas: Near the school’s building, outdoor cafés, rooftops, iconic city spots.
