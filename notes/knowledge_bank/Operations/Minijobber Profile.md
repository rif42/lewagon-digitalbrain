# Minijobber Profile
## Context
In Germany, the complex computation of Mini Jobber payslips generates a lot of questions from the teaching staff about the amount they receive on a given month. The current payslip view is only visible to the city staff and not the teacher.
## Solution
We’ve created a new Mini Jobber tab in the alumnus profile displaying simplified information about Mini Jobber contracts and the overview of a teacher’s payslips with the possibility to review their details.

In case there are multiple contracts, you will see them in different rows.
Under the Payslips section, you’ll be able to see the details that went into the calculation of the total due Amount from the payslip.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fc544a2b7-bd0b-4f27-beec-355d7a6c95db%2FScreenshot_2022-03-02_at_5.49.29_PM.png?table=block&id=c343fa47-bd70-4ce0-815d-acdbd281e094&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
If you click on See details, you can see a Payslip recap which splits the earnings into two:
1. earnings from **teaching days** scheduled in the calendars (teaching)
1. **additional assignments** added manually by the staff (extra)
On the bottom of the page, there is a detailed recap of the calculation previously seen under the Payslips section in the teacher’s profile 👇​
