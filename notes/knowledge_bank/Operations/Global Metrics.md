# Global Metrics
> [!note] 
> This document serves as a comprehensive overview of the key performance indicators and dashboards used by the CRM & Ops squad to track, analyze, and optimize global performance. It consolidates essential metrics, including email campaign effectiveness, lead-to-applicant conversion rates, and overarching campaign results, into one centralized resource. By providing quick access to the most relevant data points and tools, this document supports data-driven decision-making and ensures alignment across teams for achieving operational excellence
