### Context & Problem
***Mandatory to create the idea.**** What is the challenge or pain point this project comes to solve?*
> [!note] 
> Le Wagon has 31,000 alumni with compelling upskilling and career-switch success stories, but this organic advocacy potential is currently underutilized. Our 100 employees could be powerful authentic brand ambassadors, yet there's no structured program to coordinate their social sharing. We're missing out on a significant acquisition and brand awareness channel that would be more authentic and cost-effective than traditional advertising.
### The project
***Mandatory to move in backlog.**** What does the project look like? What is the expected outcome?*
> [!note] 
> Launch a structured Employee Ambassador Program that activates 70+ Le Wagon employees to share 4-5 pre-written, UTM-tracked social posts monthly. The program will synchronize content distribution across all employee networks on designated 'Wave Days,' amplifying alumni success stories and driving measurable lead acquisition. This includes building the complete infrastructure: content calendar, UTM tracking system, Slack-based distribution mechanism, eventually incentive structure, and monthly performance reporting.
