# Equipment & phone guidelines
## **📱 The best phones to use & video settings for optimal output. Additional nice-to-have equipment.**
### **Best phones to use:**
- iPhone 12 and above, or any recent iPhone (Pro versions are even better).
- Google Pixel 6 and above.
- Samsung Galaxy S21 and above.
- Most recent flagship devices = good enough (just ensure storage + battery).
### **Phone settings:**
- Resolution: **1080p** (social media) or **4K** (if editing/heavy cropping).
- Frame rate: **30fps** (default) or **60fps** (for smooth movement/B-roll).
- Orientation: **Vertical for social**, **horizontal if required**.
- Lock focus & exposure (tap and hold on subject).
### **Nice-to-have equipment:**
- Tripod (to avoid shaky footage).
- Ring light or small LED panel (for consistent lighting).
- [Clip-on lavalier microphone](https://www.amazon.fr/Microphone-t%C3%A9l%C3%A9phone-automatique-r%C3%A9verb%C3%A9ration-enregistrement/dp/B0DQTXPQRR?dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.BE5IlIkQ5NyG3AsnX3UXMVbAvpdUb6h7Pyz1jA__k7plBEAY9T5F459L66P0LEtHK-HwLWXEjXdx10zjHcoOWCw8Sz17qHKoueoI9OYIjJC5ksD-ICxE2s-1DXuE_TOsiNIcr8OOewXlbjARAUUY8hwJowDedMU2Si3gw2l1YEsr2ud-_blq6uGd4QT8SF2OVmvLKsJlevkAK-i4WI4yaJJcDKPxOO4WaULFYNsLHnyGRWyi4OlkvxRBFJXo4KlA5c7Vtshj9Jig2aVihJrY7WRsYUKjdfqTKYPqY2Vkeso.TL-vL8ZhTvuIfxU_aSy5ZhczAoLk2MtJkwTY_DlYUdc&dib_tag=se&keywords=Lavalier%2BMic&qid=1759321670&sr=8-6&th=1) (better audio quality).
