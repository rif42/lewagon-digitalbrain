## ❓ FAQ: Common questions about Kitt
- **Is Kitt like a typical LMS?**
No. While Kitt includes LMS functionalities, it’s built specifically for Le Wagon’s bootcamp experience. It combines structured content delivery, automated testing, and hands-on project tracking into a single platform.
- **Can students access content after the bootcamp?**
Yes. Alumni retain access to materials like lecture notes, recorded sessions, and challenges, helping them review or upskill anytime.
- **How does Kitt help teachers?**
It reduces grading workload, centralises resources, and provides real-time insights into student engagement, allowing instructors to focus on mentoring.
- **What data does Kitt track?**
Kitt tracks attendance, challenge completions, lecture views, and more. This helps identify students who may need support early.
- **Can we show Kitt to prospective students?**
Yes, but only using a demo environment with anonymised/fake data, in compliance with GDPR. A visual library is available for marketing use.
- **How do new instructors learn to use Kitt?**
They are onboarded with internal documentation and templates, and benefit from its intuitive, structured interface.
- **Why is Kitt important for global consistency?**
Kitt ensures all students receive the same learning experience regardless of location or instructor, a major strength of Le Wagon’s pedagogy.
- **What makes Kitt uniquely bootcamp-ready?**
It includes bespoke features like Setup Day, buddy pairing, voting systems, and real-world coding challenges—designed specifically for immersive learning.
