# Crm & Ops’s missions & Structure
## Overview
> [!note] 
> The CRM & Ops team at Le Wagon plays a pivotal role in the growth and success of the organization. This document outlines the team’s missions, structure, and key responsibilities, focusing on campaign management, CRM operations, conversion, and innovation projects.
# ᳩ Main Missions
1. **Develop and Implement CRM Strategies:**
        - Drive engagement, conversion, and retention across all customer lifecycle stages.
        - Ensure consistency and quality of CRM data for all teams.
        - Optimize and innovate CRM & Third-party tools and processes.
        - Collaborate with S&A team about leads management
1. **Support Campaigns and Automation:**
        - Design, execute, and optimize re-engagement, activation, and transactional campaigns.
        - Collaborate with regional teams to align messaging and segmentation.
1. **Maintain and Optimize CRM Operations:**
        - Guarantee the proper use of HubSpot and maintain data accuracy.
        - Provide training, documentation, and support for teams.
1. **Drive Growth and Innovation:**
        - Identify and test new acquisition channels.
        - Implement creative projects to enhance customer experience and conversions.

# **𖧑 Team Structure**
*4 people composed the CRM & Ops Squad
*
**Baptiste – Head of CRM & Ops**
- Reports to the Global Head of Growth.
- Responsible for the development and management of the CRM strategy for the main channels (emailing, notifications, messaging, etc.), with a focus on user engagement, activity, and conversion.
