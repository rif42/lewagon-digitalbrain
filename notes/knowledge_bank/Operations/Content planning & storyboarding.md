# Content planning & storyboarding
## **📝 Templates, best practices & AI prompts to plan your video scene by scene if needed.
**
### **Why storyboard?**
- Saves time during filming.
- Helps align on message & visuals before recording.
### **Simple template (for short videos):**
1. **Hook (0–3s):** Attention grabber → “Did you know…?”
1. **Content (4–20s):** Main message (tips, list, explanation).
1. **Call-to-action (20–30s):** CTA → “Join us at Le Wagon!”
### **Best practices:**
- Keep visuals moving every 3–5s (change angle, add text overlay).
- Mix A-roll (person speaking) + B-roll (classroom, campus, city shots).
- Use branded colors & fonts for captions.
