## 🤔 How to know which type of List I do need ?
> Choose the correct list type needed following this logic
**Static Lists**
- Quick/One time views/actions
- Filters can’t be edited
---
**Dynamic Lists**
- Long term actions/views
- Daily needs
- Limited number
---
**Views**
- Long term views
- Daily needs
- No analysis
- Only one object focused

Every time that you add a filter it will go inside a Group, every filter within the same Group will act as AND
If you want to add an OR condition, you will need to click to “Add filter group”
*FE* in my picture my list will display :
- All contacts that have their Campus set to Barcelona and got their Follow-up notes to fake
- OR all the contacts that have their lifecycle set as SQL

### 🎤 Specific cases for audiences
> If you are creating your list for some segmentation you **must **use any of the following proprieties
- **Campus **= Will get you all full lead list associated to your campus
- **Region Owner**** **= Will get you get a full lead list associated to your region
- **Campus ****=**** ****Online** **AND Country ****=**** ****your country, ** to your Country and that are Online
- **Country **= your country **AND Campus** = Unknown **AND Region Owner** = Your region, Will get you all full lead list associated to your country
> ⚠️ Before saving your list double check if you have used the correct proprieties and that you have included the mandatory ones :
