# Notion Tutorial
This is a simple Notion training for the team. Here we use a grey text to quickly describe our page.
You can introduce any block typing the / shortcut in Notion.
# Start with a "Heading 2" block
A cool thing you can do is to list all tasks to complete at the beginning of the document and assign them within the team referencing people with @:
- [ ] Finish writing [[Notion shortcuts|⌨Notion shortcuts]] assigned to @Rebecca Menat
- [ ] Add a cool banner assigned to @Anouk Hajage
