## Team 2025 OKRs
---
### 🎓 Objective 1: Deliver Best-in-Class In-Person and Online Student Experience
**Key Results:**
- **+90%** response rate on the mid-feedback form by **Q2 2025**
- **+80%** response rate on the end-of-feedback form by **Q3 2025**
- Conduct **quarterly NPS analysis** per program, campus, and region to monitor student satisfaction by the **end of each quarter**
- Reach **50+ NPS** for Growth Marketing by **EOY 2025**
- Increase **On-Campus NPS by 10 points** (from 56 to 66) and **Online NPS by 5 points** (from 68 to 73) by **EOY 2025**
### 💰 Objective 2: Optimize Training Delivery for Improved Financial Performance
**Key Results:**
- Design and implement a **Best Pedagogical Margin Matrix** to guide Ops staffing decisions by **Q2 2025**
- Collaborate with Finance to **audit and streamline payment collection**, reducing delays by **X%** by **Q3 2025**
- Create a **teacher payment policy** aligned with local regulations by **Q2 2025**
- Implement a **refund tracking system** and **reduce refund requests by 10%** by **EOY 2025**
### 🧑‍💼 Objective 3: Build & Develop Strong Operational Team Members
**Key Results:**
- Recruit a **Bootcamp Ops Manager** by the end of **Q1 2025**
- Audit and update the **operational documentation** to ensure it's **current, standardized, and accessible** by **Q2 2025**
- Develop and standardize **onboarding for key Ops roles**, with **100% completion** for new hires by **Q3 2025**
- Implement a **meeting structure & knowledge-sharing process** to foster **cross-functional collaboration** by **Q2 2025**
### 👨‍🏫 Objective 4: Establish Teacher Recruitment Process & Grow Teachers Community
**Key Results:**
- Design and implement a **structured teacher recruitment process** by **Q3 2025**
- Centralize **40% of external teacher recruitment** through the central team by **EOY 2025**
- Review, update, and roll out a full **teacher lifecycle process** to improve engagement & satisfaction by **EOY 2025**
- Partner with Education to host **2 teacher meetings** (Q2 & Q4) to present the **Education Roadmap** and encourage community engagement
