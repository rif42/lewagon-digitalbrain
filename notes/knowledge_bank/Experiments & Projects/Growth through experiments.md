# Growth through experiments
> [!note] 
> Questions? ask @Daniel Rodriguez on slack.
> [!video] 
### **Overview**
In program management, we believe in developing new tools and processes using the lean methodology. This methodology was first introduced by Eric Ries in his book "The Lean Startup."
In the book, the author discusses the concept of Minimum Viable Product (MVP), which he defines as:
> A Minimum Viable Product is that version of a new product which allows a team to collect the maximum amount of validated learning about customers with the least effort.
### **Our Doctrine?**
1. Fail fast
1. Work around acquired learning on top of learning
1. Experiment and learn from experiments.

**1. Identifying assumptions**
What do we think we already know?
*Here are some examples of assumptions we could use:*
> *In order for this idea to be successful we need this assumption to be true: …. (assumptions)*
> *Our teachers/batch managers/etc… have x,y,z problem*
> *______ matters to my teachers*
> *______ will attend or use this tool*
> *no satisfactory existing solutions*
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F5799c4dd-b9e9-453c-bad7-f1e93e2daf18%2FScreenshot_2023-04-21_at_17.03.29.png?table=block&id=8d1d2374-10d2-4d48-8144-c0b731215254&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
1. **Hypothesis build**
Hypothesis in contrast with assumptions are actionable, it must have the following:
> if we [Action], we believe [Subject/target] will [Predicted/action] because [reason]
With metrics, more specific:
> We believe [Subject/target] has a [Problem] because [reason]will [Predicted/action] because [reason]. If we [action], this [metric] metric will improve.
1. **Minimum criteria for success**
Make sure when deciding which experiment to start with or do, that you compare cost vs reward, this will also give us the Metric for success as that will be the reward as outcome of the experiment.
