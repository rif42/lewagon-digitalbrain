## 💡 Overview - Solution ideation
Our vision is to:
> 1 -** Design a full lifecycle for delivering new products and program improvements in collaboration with the content team**
> 2 - **Set the grounds for a smooth collaboration between content & Program Management**.
By doing so, we expect several benefits, including but not limited to:
- Improved communication between departments
- Better prioritisation of tasks
- Increased transparency
        - Better alignment of content and PM teams towards OKR’s solution ideations
- A new system for assigning tasks and experiments across the Program Management & Content team
To achieve our objectives we will trial a new project management tool “Asana”. Asana as a tool is currently approved and utilised by both the Content Management and B2B teams. We will also test and approve in collaboration with the content team, a full product delivery and improvement lifecycle.
### 👣 Current pain points
---
### Efficiency of Work
- As member of the program management team, I don’t have a way to scale experiments to local teams and track its progress effectively
- As a member of the program management team I often find out that another department already worked on the same initiative as me, I don’t have a way to effectively avoid this duplicated work.
- As a member of the program management team it is hard to keep track of my productivity
### Shared Processes and Ownership
- As a program manager I don’t have a clear way to suggest an improvement (Where is this improvement recorded?)
- As a member of the program management team, I struggle to ensure that all my current tasks are aligned with OKRs
- As a member of the content team, I don’t share ownership with the PMs on the product OKRs and I cannot track their achievement.
- As a member of the content team, I receive an unmanageable number of direct requests from local stakeholders about the product
### Transparency and Cross-departmental Visibility
- As a program manager I don’t have a way to find the information if I want to communicate to my team which initiatives have been launched, are in process or won’t be worked on at this moment
- As a member of the content team, I have limited visibility over the progress of work of the PM team despite sharing the same product.
- As a member of the content team, I am missing an opportunity to harness the value from local networks in searching content freelancers
