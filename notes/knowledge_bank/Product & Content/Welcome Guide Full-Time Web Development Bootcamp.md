### **Key Dates for your Diary **🗓️​
These are the key dates of the Bootcamp. Please add them to your diary - you will also find them in your batch Google Calendar! (see the [Things to do before the first day](https://lewagon.notion.site/p/072d3fe55c204463a0beb8e1e92241d5))
**Monday 17th April ****→** **Set Up & Welcome** 👋** **
**Monday 15th May** (5PM - 6PM) **→ Pitch Night** 🎤** **
**Friday 19th May** (5PM - 6PM) **→** **Product Design Sprint **🏃‍♀️** **
**Monday 29th May ****→**** ****Airbnb Projects begin** 🏡** **
**Monday 5th June**** →** **Final Projects begin**
**Friday 9th June** (5PM - 6PM) **→** **Halfway Demos**🤞​
**Thursday 15th June ****→** **Dress Rehearsal **😎​
**Friday 16th June (**Keep your night free!)** → Demo Day** 🏁
This is your biggest opportunity to demonstrate what you have been building over the course of the Bootcamp. You'll be showing off a full user journey of web apps, having learnt all of the hard and soft skills necessary to pitch and build a product in teams. Have a look at previous[ ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B9YUhH2ggAQ&t=15s)[Demo Days](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B9YUhH2ggAQ&t=15s)[ ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B9YUhH2ggAQ&t=15s)to get an idea of how it looks like 😎​
**Monday 19th June - Friday 23rd June ****→**** ****Career Week** 🎓
The aim of Career Week is to help Le Wagon Web Development and Data Science Bootcamp graduates take the next steps in their careers through a tailored series of workshops, buddy reviews, assignments, hiring events and video tutorials.

### **Final Tuition Fee Payments **
**Standard Payment**
You will have already transferred the deposit to secure your seat on the Bootcamp.
The remaining balance is due 1 week before the start date on **Monday 10th April 2023. **
You can use the exact same payment details and invoice reference number as you did for the deposit payment to make the transfer. You are also welcome to begin your final balance transfer as of now.
**Instalment Payment Plan with Alma **
If you have opted for the instalment payment plan with our partner Alma, you will receive an email from Alma at least a week before the start of the bootcamp to setup your payment.
**Make sure you take action on this email to validate your payment for the bootcamp**
