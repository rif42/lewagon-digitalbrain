## How to create new vertical pages
---
1. **To create a new vertical page, you can click on **[this link](https://www.lewagon.com/admin/resources/vertical_courses)**.**
1. **Click on ****Create new vertical course**
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fprod-files-secure.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2F2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f%2F2f05ffbd-dd27-4cb3-8f6b-7895a68aa39e%2FCapture_decran_2024-09-18_a_16.01.17.png?table=block&id=1058924a-f014-800b-8567-e88555d2b663&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1330&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
1. **Add all the details about the page:**
        1. Slug: it’s the URL of the page - Use only lowercase characters separated by underscore (e.g. finance_managers)
        1. Name the page
        1. Add a picture in the header
        1. Choose the number of bullet points you want to see in the first section *(*see this image*)*
        1. Add the query string of the session module:
                    1. Go to the [next sessions page](https://www.lewagon.com/sessions) and filter the sessions based on your needs using the available filters.
                    1. Once you’ve applied the filters, the URL will automatically update.
                    1. Copy everything in the URL that comes after www.lewagon.com/sessions?.
                    1. Use this query string to pre-filter the sessions in the session module of the new page.
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fprod-files-secure.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2F2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f%2F85c4b612-54c8-47a8-ad7e-cc07a98664f5%2FCapture_decran_2024-09-18_a_16.01.26.png?table=block&id=1058924a-f014-8085-89d0-d51f6ad484fe&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1330&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
1. **Save the page**
1. **Visit the newly created page: ****https://www.lewagon.dev/courses/{SLUG}**
    *If your slug contains an underscore, replace it by a dash in the URL.
    For instance: ****finance_managers**** ➡️ **https://www.lewagon.dev/courses/****finance-managers***
1. **Update the content of the page (title, intro paragraph, figures…)**
    You can refer to the [Not found](https://app.notion.com/p/025db61b2e734968bb581569528b59c5?pvs=24#025db61b2e734968bb581569528b59c5) for assistance with editing your page.
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fprod-files-secure.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2F2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f%2Fdb4e374f-6ec7-4809-90ce-cafaf80d836b%2FCapture_decran_2024-09-18_a_16.37.54.png?table=block&id=1058924a-f014-8014-8690-f7d7f88b56e8&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1330&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
