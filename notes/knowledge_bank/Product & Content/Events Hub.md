At Le Wagon, **events are a core part of our marketing strategy.**
They’re not just about filling seats — they help us build brand awareness, engage our audience, and drive conversions at every stage of the funnel.
### **Overview **
---
Events are any live or online activities organized for an audience of potential students, alumni, or partner, and represent an important source of lead generation.
They can be internal (run by our own team) or external (with partners or guest speakers), and they come in many formats.
They matter because they help us achieve two critical marketing goals:
1. **Bring in new leads **→ Reach people who don’t yet know Le Wagon or aren’t in our CRM.
1. **Convert leads into candidates **→ Build trust, answer questions, and help them take the next step toward applying.
> 🔎 Well-planned events = more visibility, better engagement, and higher conversion rates.
> [!note] 
> Not all events have the same purpose. Each one should be tied to a specific funnel stage
