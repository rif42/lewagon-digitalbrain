# Running a workshop
# Preparation
Congratulations, you were selected to run a workshop for Le Wagon!
You should have received an invitation to join a batch on [Learn](http://learn.lewagon.com/), the platform that hosts our workshops. If it's not the case, ask your contact to invite you again.
When you join the batch on Learn, you should land on the **Instructions**:
![](https://lewagon.notion.site/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fa7e70afa-c87e-4528-b29f-af88c7eece70%2FScreenshot_2020-04-07_at_22.12.30.png?table=block&id=c490f810-ed05-4e84-9298-26da5fa14cdc&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1380&userId=&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
## Watch the video
The workshop is split into a 1h15 lecture and 45 minutes of practice where you act as a TA. Start by watching the replay of the lecture with a good set of headphones, in a quiet place, where you won't be interrupted, and take notes actively.
![](https://lewagon.notion.site/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Ff75c2ae2-0956-4d89-9194-d84863b124cc%2FScreenshot_2020-04-07_at_20.49.03.png?table=block&id=1c27180f-e6c9-45ff-b88d-b175f3a014d1&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1380&userId=&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
## Repeat the livecodes / demo
Make sure you are able to perform all the livecodes and demos shown in the video. Practice as much as you need to make sure you will be able to **run the lecture in 1h15** maximum. Whatever the time it takes for you to give the lecture, it's key to stop 1h15 after the start of the event, for attendees to have enough time to do the challenges. It takes practice to find the right pace!
## Read the takeaways
The *transcript* of the lecture is available in the **Takeaways** section. Make sure you read it thoroughly and don't hesitate to enrich your notes with examples and conclusions taken from there. It's key for the teacher to know the takeaways by heart and to be able to share them clearly and concisely.
![](https://lewagon.notion.site/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fc2e9cdff-cd17-472f-a107-65c1f8b00919%2FScreenshot_2020-04-07_at_20.50.48.png?table=block&id=cee1ffc1-f4d2-402e-86dc-7f6e182029a9&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1380&userId=&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
## Do the challenges
In the 2nd part of the workshops, attendees will sign in on Learn and do the challenges by themselves. During this part, you are expected to act as a TA, so make sure you did the challenges at least once to be able to debug the attendees efficiently.
![](https://lewagon.notion.site/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F30cc85a2-eed4-4c92-bc15-02fa3ca863fb%2FScreenshot_2020-04-07_at_20.51.31.png?table=block&id=dc4d5ed6-98ba-4684-9be8-7954e333130a&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1380&userId=&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
# Running the workshop
Before the start, make sure you **open the Slides in a tab of your browser** (be careful, some decks may take time to load), and test the resolution of your computer with the projector you will use. We recommend a setup with a Mirror Display scaled in 720p:
![](https://lewagon.notion.site/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F325f2d29-d782-4cc2-95ce-c30c7919243d%2FScreenshot_2020-04-07_at_20.14.05.png?table=block&id=a3b9d4c4-fd77-4d61-857f-f67e3ef725cd&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1380&userId=&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Make sure you have the token or the onboarding url of the batch for the 2nd part of the workshop. If you don't have it, ask the event manager to give it to you.
> [!note] 
> Ask your driver / event manager for the **onboarding url** or **token** before the event
Open the slides in a new tab (some slide decks take a long time to load) and we recommend displaying them full-screen in a separate desktop.
When everyone is ready and the event manager introduced Le Wagon to the attendees, it's your turn to enter the scene.
## Intro
Start by introducing yourself, your background, why did you get interested in coding? Then, explain the format of the workshop:
- 1h15 of lecture during which they should close their laptops and listen carefully
- after the lecture they will be invited to join Learn and have access to all the materials used during the 1st part
- challenges on Learn with the assistance of the teacher (you) + TAs for large audiences
Start the workshop and enjoy!
> [!note] 
> **Important**: don't onboard the attendees on Learn before the end of the lecture
## Lecture
Run your lecture, find the right pace, ask questions to the audience to make the course interactive, and pause between each section to take questions. Keep an eye on the watch, 1h15 is short!
At the end of the lecture, if the workshop has a **Quiz** in the **Takeaways** section, you can take it with the audience and turn it into a game! It's a fun way to end the lecture and transition to the 2nd part that takes place on the platform.
![](https://lewagon.notion.site/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fda06ac20-f6bf-4427-9e99-a78f2772903e%2FScreenshot_2020-04-07_at_20.52.34.png?table=block&id=fc84ded9-16a2-49b7-a49e-3c86c768d65c&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1380&userId=&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
## Onboarding
It's time to onboard the attendees on the batch on Learn.
### With a url
If you have a way to share them the url and have them onboard in 1 click, share them the url that has the following format: <https://learn.lewagon.com/onbarding?secure_token=XXXaosijdpajdXXX> (ask your event manager).
### With a token
If you don't have a way to send them the url with the encrypted token, ask them to go to <https://learn.lewagon.com/welcome>, and display the 6-character **token** of the batch on your screen (ask your event manager).
## Challenges
Once they are signed in, ask them to go to the **Your Turn** section where they will find the instructions of the challenges. Ask them to raise their hands if they have a question and assist them like during *classic *tickets.
## Feedback
10 minutes before the end, share with them the url of the Feedback form (you will find it in the **Thank You** section) and ask them to fill it before they leave. You can remind them they will keep their account on the platform and can finish the challenges at home!
