# Reboot on Rails
## 💡 Overview - Solution ideation
*Describe what is the problem and what potential solutions we might have to solve it.*
- Objective 1: Support struggling students by offering them a second reboot
- Objective 2: Decreasing the level of frustration of students during this Unit
- Solution: Offering a second Reboot session (1 hour), this time focused on Rails. The sessions will be held on:
        - **Thursday, 7th March 2pm - 3pm CET** → Week 6 - Day 4 Rails Assets
        - **Friday, 8th Match 2pm - 3pm CET** → Week 6 - Day 5 Hosting & Image Upload
    Through the 2 sessions, teachers will go through the challenges of the day from scratch, guiding the students step by step. Students will be able to start the challenges in the morning and after lunch break, they will be able to go over them together with a teacher from 2-3pm. After Reboot, they will be able to keep on progressing until the Livecode. These sessions will be mandatory for struggling students and optional for all.
