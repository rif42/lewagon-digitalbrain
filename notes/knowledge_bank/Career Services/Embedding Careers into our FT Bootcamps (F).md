# Embedding Careers into our FT Bootcamps (F)
> [!note] 
> Based on pilot feedback from Berlin and London, we are making key improvements to career awareness and engagement throughout the bootcamp journey.
### **Week 1: Kick-off / Career Form**
*No changes*
- Career Form is sent to all students on the first Wednesday of each bootcamp.
- The local Ops team will ensure there are slides with info about Le Wagon career support within the kick-off presentation. ([[At kick-off (F)|🤝🏽At kick-off (F)]])
### **Week 2: Detailed Info Session**
*No changes / *✨*New in some regions*✨​
- The regional Student Success Manager will deliver a 15-30 min Career Info Session to all students. This can be delivered in person or virtually.
### **Week 3: Focus Hour**
✨*New*✨​
- A dedicated session (ie. replacing a live code session) where students have TA/Student Success Manager support to start the Job Seeker Program, including:
        - Registering for Huntr (job tracking tool)
        - Opening and exploring the Job Seeker Program on Learn, and finding the Career Workbook
        - Identifying their ‘Career Advisor’
### **Weeks 4-7: Targeted Career Messages**
✨*New*✨​
- Regular career messages will be sent to remind students of key actions, provide relevant resources, and encourage engagement with career services.
        - These will mainly be sent by the regional Student Success Manager, using the bulk Slack message feature for all students on the Job Seeker Program
### **Weeks 8-11: Open Career Hour**
✨*New*✨​
- At a time that makes sense for each batch, the regional Student Success Manager will arrange and communicate an open session where students can drop in with career-related questions, seek feedback, and discuss job search strategies with the career team.
