# Career Support | Bootcamp Guide
---
> [!note] 
> **What career support does Le Wagon provide to Graduates?**
Our program is specifically designed to support students in their next steps after the bootcamp, whether they want to find a new job, build a company, or kick off their freelancer career.
Career support is self-paced and consists of three main programs
- Job Seeker Program
- Founders Program
- Freelancers Program
Any student is welcome to join more than one program.

### How to start using **Job Seeker Program 💼**​
Once you submitted Career Form in week 1 of your bootcamp
- We have sent you an **email with access content on Learn**
- We have sent you an **email with Huntr activation link**
Next steps:
1. Go to our Learn platform and watch introduction video and copy your Workbook.
1. Based on instructions, submit 5 mandatory assignments to your Career Advisor.
1. You will be then invited to Live Workshops, which we run every week.
1. Once you attended 4 mandatory Live Workshops you will get a link to book 1:1 with your career advisor and you will be visible to our hiring partners in Talent Pool.

> **What do Founder and Freelancer Programs cover?**
Founder and Freelancer Programs consists of
- Online and on-campus events such as workshops, Q&As and talks
- Video content library with masterclasses
- Perks such as coupons and discounts
- Networking opportunities
> [!note] 
> **Career Services for BGS students**
If your bootcamp is sponsored by **Bildungsgutschein **in Germany. You are obligated to use our services following the bootcamp. This is to increase your chance of success in the job market and make sure that our Team can best support you 🙌🏽
This requirement is mentioned in your contract and has been communicated to you on Slack at the beginning of training.
Specifically, in the week following the bootcamp, you will have to attend some online events and dedicate time to individual work. You will have to confirm it by signing Edusign attendance sheet. We will be in touch with more info during your bootcamp.
