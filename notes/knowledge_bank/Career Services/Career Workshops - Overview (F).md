# Career Workshops - Overview (F)
The Job Seeker Program includes 2 types of live workshops. Students gain access to these in Level 2, via the [#career-job-seeker-program](https://lewagon-alumni.slack.com/archives/C079UJHKVM4) Slack channel.
> [!note] 
> **Core Workshops**
> [!note] 
> **Non-Core Workshops**
### Helpful Information
- The event schedule is coordinated by @LuceliaM + @Milly Dyer each month, focusing on EU timezones. Additional events are also run on a local level (CMs are encouraged to organise Q&As each quarter)
- We use Calendly for workshop registrations. This is coordinated by @LuceliaM and @Leonor Boavida and shared with students in the #career-job-seeker-program Slack channel
- The majority of core and non-core workshops are delivered by the European Careers Team.
