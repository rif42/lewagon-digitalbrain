# Live Workshop Plan - Elevator pitch
# 🎯 Workshop goal
> This workshop helps get comfortable with introducing oneself at the interview
---
# What can students expect?
### ☑ What we’ll do
- Review key tactics and framework
- Practice 3 different introductions
- Receive feedback from peers
---
### ❌ What we won’t do
- Listen to a lecture on introductions
- Offer 1:1 coaching
- Go through all possible questions
