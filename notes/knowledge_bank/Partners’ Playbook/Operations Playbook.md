## BOOTCAMP OPERATIONS
---
### How will we work together?
You’ll have a single person from our team who will guide you through the setup and support process. They'll provide you with access to all essential documentation, tools, and resources needed to effectively run your bootcamps.
You'll need to assign a dedicated **Operations Contact** for your city. This person will oversee key aspects of your franchise's day-to-day operations, including:
- **Onboarding students**: Ensuring a smooth and correct process for welcoming new students.
- **Efficient training sessions**: Making sure that each session runs as planned without issues.
- **Staffing teachers**: Hiring and scheduling experienced teachers for each bootcamp.
- **Profitability management**: Monitoring the financial health of each batch of students.
- **Learning experience**: Ensuring students have a top-tier learning experience.
### Bootcamp lifecycle
Bootcamps are intensive, and we understand the importance of creating the best possible environment for students. Here’s what sets us apart:
- **Top-notch content:** We offer carefully curated and high-quality training materials designed to provide maximum value and relevance.
- **Experienced teachers & TAs:** Our teaching staff and teaching assistants come from the local community, bringing practical experience and deep knowledge to guide students.
- **Team-building activities:** We integrate activities to foster collaboration, engagement, and a sense of camaraderie, helping students support each other throughout their journey.
Student care and satisfaction are key priorities. We want students to feel not just like they're attending a course, but like they’re becoming part of a bigger family—a community where they belong and can thrive. This sense of belonging will help them stay motivated and achieve their goals.

**Bootcamp format**
Whether they prefer an intensive full-time option or a part-time format, our courses are tailored to their needs.
- 9 week intensive full-time format bootcamp, monday-friday from 9am - 6pm.
        - **Lectures 09:00AM - 10:30AM
        **Each morning, students join an interactive and dynamic lesson where the teacher explains the concepts of the day before they dive into practice.
        - **Challenges 10:30AM - 05:00PM
        **Then, they pair up with their buddy and work on a series of programming challenges with the support of our teaching staff, who will help them solve problems whenever they need.
        - **Live sessions 05:00PM - 06:00PM
        **At the end of the day, they review the day’s challenges in a live session where their teacher will walk them through the correction of important coding challenges
- For busy professionals, we offer a 26 weeks part-time bootcamp allowing them to keep their professional and personal commitments while learning with us.
        - **Self-study** : Student watch 2 video-recorded lectures per week
        - **Live session**: 2 evenings and on Saturday, they meet with their teachers and buddy to practice what they’ve learning with challenges.

## TEACHERS
### Teachers community
Joining Le Wagon, you’ll benefit for a worldwide community of teachers of over 3,000 professionals, each with a dedicated profile page on Kitt for easy reference. Teachers can be easily reached and communicated with through our Slack channels, ensuring smooth collaboration and engagement across the network.
All new teachers needs to **sign a NDA and complete an onboarding track **before working at Le Wagon. This ensure they have a good understanding what it means to teach at Le Wagon.
- If a franchise wishes to **hire a teacher who has worked in a core city within the last six months**, a**pproval from the core city management is required** before proceeding with the staffing decision.
- Each franchise **is responsible of sourcing, onboarding and training** their pedagogical crew. Core team will share best practices.
**Pedagogical crew**
Our pedagogical team consists of a Lead Teacher and Teacher Assistants.
The Lead Teacher is responsible for delivering lectures, leading the teaching team for the day, conducting recap sessions, overseeing project management, and fostering growth through mentoring and self-improvement.
Based on the number of students per batch, we recommend staffing as many Teacher Assistants as needed. We suggest maintaining a ratio of **8-10 students per teacher,** adjusting based on the day's difficulty level.
A Teacher Assistant serves as a guide for students, helping them navigate concepts and best practices. They simplify complex ideas, break down information into manageable parts, and provide step-by-step guidance to ensure students grasp key concepts.
**Pedagogical team rate **
At a city level, you can set default teaching rates which would make sense for your region/city.

## CAREER SERVICES
---
### What career services do we provide?
At the beginning of their bootcamp, they can choose to participate in one or more of our three career programs. Each career path is tailored to their needs to ensure they are ready to launch their project after the course: Job seekers, Freelancer and Founder Programs.
- If a student does not choose any program, they will not have access to career services
- If a student selects the Freelance or Founder program, they will be added to the relevant Slack channels, provided access to learning tracks, and can participate in any core events organised for these programs.
**Focus Job Seeker program**
> The job seeker programs (most chosen one) consist of 3 levels unlocking each time a new service.
> Level 1 : Complete self-paced Job seeker program assignments
> Level 2 : Attend Live workshops
> Level 3: Join talent pool and have your 1:1 with your career coach
> This ensure that only commited students/alumni book a session with your career coach.
- Each franchise need a dedicated **career point of contact** (POC) with an advisor account on Huntr. They will coach their alumni and promote them to L2 and L3 following the same criteria as the core team.
- Franchises have **full access to Huntr**, meaning they can view all job seekers, not just those assigned to them. ### Outcomes forms & job reports
Each year, Le Wagon publishes a job report for each city, providing detailed insights into our students' outcomes. This report helps prospective students:
- Understand what jobs they can land after our courses, the sectors that hire our students the most, and the salaries they can expect.
- Learn about the average employment rate after the bootcamp.
- Discover which companies hire our alumni.
This report is a valuable resource for anyone considering a career change or looking to gauge the impact of our bootcamp on career prospects.
- Each core city and franchise is responsible for collecting their outcome forms to meet the **75% target **and publish their job reports.
- Once the target is achieved, the franchise should request @Marta Bas to extract the form responses from Metabase into a Google Sheet, which will be used to generate the KPIs for the job report.
- The core team will also provide job report templates to the franchises via Canva.
- Once the job report is updated and finalised, franchises can upload it to HubSpot B2C and share the link with @Marta Bas , so she can update the central page published on the website.
### Hiring Partners
Since 2013, Le Wagon has built an extensive network of hiring partners, with over **1,000 companies **actively recruiting our graduates.
As a student, they gain exclusive access to our comprehensive database, allowing them to explore exactly where our alumni are working and discover how many graduates each company has hired. This gives them a clear advantage in targeting top employers and leveraging our strong industry connections to land your dream job.
