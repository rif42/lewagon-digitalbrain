# How We Work Together
### We use modern tools to connect and collaborate across different countries and timezones.
### Our teach platforms & tools
By partnering with us, you'll gain access to our exclusive learning materials, cutting-edge education platforms, and a suite of additional tools designed to simplify your daily operations and ensure consistently high levels of student satisfaction.
**Kitt **- it serves as both the students' learning platform and the administration management tool, ensuring smooth coordination of their educational journey and operational tasks.
**Learn** - is the dedicated platform for hosting workshops and providing students with prepwork materials, which they are required to complete prior to starting the bootcamp.
Both **Kitt** and **Learn** were built in-house by our dedicated engineering team, ensuring they are tailored specifically to our needs. These platforms are continuously maintained and updated to stay ahead, offering a seamless experience that empowers our franchises to deliver top-tier education with ease.
**Slack** - our primary communication platform for students and alumni. It is the exclusive tool we use to interact with students during their training, facilitating communication and support.
**Zoom **- is our video-conferencing tool used for delivering online training sessions, ensuring high-quality virtual classroom experiences.
**Huntr** - is our job tracking platform that helps students and job seekers organize and manage their job search process. Through this platform, you’ll be able to track students job search, post job offers and share relevant profiles to hiring partners
**Hubspot Careers** - is our CRM for hiring partners. You’ll be able to view and maintain an updated list of your local partners.
