# Email and tools onboarding
> [!note] 
> This doc contains the information and steps to onboard a franchise into core processes.
## **Email account and tools access**
---
1. Fill in the Employee Onboarding and the Metabase access request forms on [🔒drivers](https://teamwagon.slack.com/archives/G3FPKARL4) to be onboarded on email, slack, www, etc. by Engineering team.
    ![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)
1. Once the email account is activated, create a ticket on [help.lewagon.com](http://help.lewagon.com/) to request access to the following tools, and assign it to the respective person.
    Please, include:

        - First Name
        - Email
        - Campus
