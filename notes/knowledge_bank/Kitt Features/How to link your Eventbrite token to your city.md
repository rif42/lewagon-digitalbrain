# How to link your Eventbrite token to your city
---
> [!note] 
> www needs your eventbrite token to be able to retrieve your event’s attendees and push their information to Hubspot.
Here is how to retrieve your token and add it to your city infos on www.
# Set up your Eventbrite account
### Create** an Eventbrite account**
If you do not have an Eventbrite acccount for your city yet, you can create one 👉 [here](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/signin/?referrer=%2F)
> [!note] 
> Do not use your personal Account.
### Eventbrite Api Key
Once you have an account ready, dedicated to your city:
1. Navigate to: ***Account Settings*** 👉 ***Developer Links**** *👉 [Api Keys](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/account-settings/apps)
1. Create a new **Api Key**
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fae536695-4d01-46b7-b80d-48192db23ef6%2FScreenshot_2021-02-28_at_14.25.06.png?table=block&id=aaca22a1-fb10-4172-bdb4-bd3797e0131c&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1330&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
1. You can fill out the fields like so👇​
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F9eae97d1-550a-417c-aa1a-8299d8dfa023%2FUntitled.png?table=block&id=50c6f442-b65a-41b0-9f19-b789c3e3e695&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1330&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
1. Then you can **copy** the key called ***Private Token***
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fedd08676-df80-43ea-b118-74009c6e8da6%2FScreenshot_2021-02-28_at_14.42.22.png?table=block&id=8756c65c-9763-4f85-8b95-4e20c92e684f&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1330&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
# Le Wagon Admin Setup
Now head over to [your city admin page](https://www.lewagon.com/v4/admin/resources/cities) on www
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fc5e99389-aa56-4030-83fb-6f191e4adadb%2FUntitled.png?table=block&id=ad19f558-4026-4d55-8990-08a0ac238e57&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Click on Edit and open the Eventbrite tab.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fbeb5e125-14da-4840-b7b0-83072f4fc810%2FUntitled.png?table=block&id=7cddac7b-8999-44b8-80b8-3a25462cc309&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Paste your token in the Eventbrite access token field and scroll to Eventbrite Organization name to pick your organisation name and click on Save 👇​
> [!video] 
If there are several organisation names linked to this account, select the right one and click on Save 👇​
> [!video]
