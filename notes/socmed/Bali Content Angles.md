# Le Wagon Bali Content Angles

Detailed content angles for selling Bali as the location for the Le Wagon data science bootcamp. Use these to build community and belonging.

## Bali Is a Food Heaven

**Angles:**
- Budget-friendly warungs vs. trendy cafes: students can eat well every day.
- Healthy options support focus: smoothie bowls, fresh juices, vegan/vegetarian menus.
- Local food culture as a bonding activity: group dinners, night market tours.
- "Best post-coding meal spots near campus."
- Food Reels: "What $10 gets you in Bali for lunch."

**Visual ideas:**
- Overhead shots of nasi campur, smoothie bowls, local coffee.
- Student interviews about favorite warungs.
- Before/after class meal routines.

## Bali Is Cheap to Live In

**Angles:**
- Compare cost of living to Tokyo, Singapore, Sydney, London, San Francisco.
- Show monthly budget: accommodation, food, transport, coworking.
- Lower living costs mean students can focus on learning instead of side jobs.
- Affordable villas with pools make cohort living fun and productive.
- Long-stay visas make Bali practical for a 9-24 week bootcamp.

**Visual ideas:**
- Infographic budget breakdown.
- Tour of a student villa or shared house.
- "What I spend in a week as a bootcamp student in Bali."

## Bali Has a Strong Remote-Work Community

**Angles:**
- Coworking spaces filled with developers, founders, and digital nomads.
- Easy to find accountability, mentors, and networking events.
- Tech meetups, startup nights, and founder talks happening regularly.
- Students join a global community, not just a local class.
- Alumni can stay and work remotely after graduation.

**Visual ideas:**
- Coworking space tours.
- Event clips from startup meetups.
- Interviews with nomads who became students.

## Bali Supports Focus and Well-Being

**Angles:**
- Nature, yoga, and slower pace help prevent burnout during bootcamp.
- Cohort bonding is stronger in a relaxed environment.
- Students report better retention when they feel balanced.
- Bali offers space to reset while upskilling.
- Combine intensity with intention: work hard, recover well.

**Visual ideas:**
- Yoga or morning routine clips.
- Villa study sessions with pool views.
- Student testimonials about focus and mental clarity.
