# Carousel Copy: Data > AI

**Topic:** Learning data is more important than learning AI because AI is created from data.  
**Audience:** Mid-career professionals aged 25–40 (sweet spot 28–33), university-educated, English-speaking, from high-income countries with savings or remote income. Primary segments: Career Pivoter and Remote Upskiller.  
**Tone:** Direct, confident, practical, lifestyle-aspirational but not tourist-y, community-focused, evidence-based.  
**Format:** Instagram carousel (6 slides), 4:5 aspect ratio (1080×1350px).  
**Goal:** Sell Le Wagon Bali's data bootcamp and Bali as a focused, affordable destination.

## Visual Notes

- Format: 6 slides, 4:5 aspect ratio (1080×1350px), 60px margins.
- Background: default Purple `#5315B8`; approved alternatives Dark Purple `#371373`, Purple 05 `#F5F2FB`, Cream `#FCF3EE`, or White `#FFFFFF`.
- Typography: headings in Circular Bold / Medium; body / labels / buttons in Inter Bold / Regular. No italics.
- Colour accents: Le Wagon Red `#E60F05` for emphasis; Tonic Purple `#670BFF` for primary CTAs. No Bali sunset tones. No black or red backgrounds.
- Imagery: real Le Wagon students, teachers, drivers, and alumni from the internal image bank. Rounded corners 16–24px. No borders, strokes, or drop shadows.
- One key idea per slide.
- Final slide: updated Le Wagon Bali branding (squared logo, three-window minibus) + clear CTA.
- No emojis except in Demo Day visuals.

## Copy Principles

- No invented metrics or fabricated claims.
- Every statement is either research-backed or clearly framed as opinion / CTA.
- Direct, confident tone. No generic "AI is everywhere" openers.
- CTA matches motivation: change trajectory, land a data role, or build with AI.
