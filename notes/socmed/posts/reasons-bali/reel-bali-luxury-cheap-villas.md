# Reel: Bali Is Cheap to Live In

> **Sibling of [[reel-bali-bootcamp-reasons]]** — same series, different spine. That reel enumerates "3 reasons Bali is the best classroom"; this one leads with the **brand**: Le Wagon Bali intro → why-Bali thesis → **affordable living + the villa (the main focus, merged into one proof)** → **world-class food at a real meal price** → **freedom (money stops being the reason you can't)** → **the reward (code by day, pool by sunset)** → close. No cohort-villa beat (redundant with the villa value) and no visa angle (client direction). "Cheap" is used once, in the tagline. Single AI-VO track with subtitles; stock-footage friendly — no faces, no location shoot required.

**Format:** Instagram Reel (9:16 vertical, 1080x1920)
**Style:** AI voiceover + subtitle captions over real/stock B-roll. No talking head.
**Duration:** ~38 seconds
**Topic:** Le Wagon Bali is the best place to study — and the proof is the price. Living in Bali costs a fraction of back home, and the villa is the value: 5–10M IDR/month (≈ a week of groceries back home) buys pool, rice fields and a desk with a view. The food is world-class at about 50K IDR a meal (≈ $3). And because living costs so little, **money stops being the reason you can't** — so the reward is real: code by day, pool by sunset. Destination-sell for the **Le Wagon Bali offline bootcamp** — Bali is the star, the villa is the hero asset.
**Tone:** Calm, factual, "the math works" — not travel-brochure. The price is the story, not the beach.
**Goal:** Drive applications to the next Le Wagon Bali batch. Beat 1 plants the brand; the merged affordability/villa proof (`0:08`) carries the reel; the food beat (`0:17`) adds a real price; the freedom payoff (`0:24`) lands the "so what"; the reward (`0:30`) and end card convert.
