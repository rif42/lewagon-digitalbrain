# Lectures
To give a remote lecture in a batch hosted on Learn, you will use Zoom as video-conferencing tool. This means you need to make sure to have Zoom installed on your computer. If it's not the case, you can refer to [this paragraph](https://github.com/lewagon/setup/blob/master/macOS.md#zoom). It's free to install zoom.
In order to host a meeting with 2+ participants for 40+ minutes, Zoom requires a paying account. We will provide one for you. Two possibilities:
- [[Learn Remote Courses|You already are a teacher on Kitt]]
- [[Learn Remote Courses|You are not a teacher on Kitt]]
## Kitt teachers
If you already are a teacher on Kitt, you can use Kitt's [zoom meetings feature](https://kitt.lewagon.com/zoom_meetings) to create and host your lectures on Learn.
### Create Zoom Meeting on Kitt
- Go to <http://kitt.lewagon.org/zoom_meetings/new>
- Enter a name, the start time and end time of the lecture
> [!note] 
> There is a 30 minutes margin around the scheduled window where the zoom account stays available. The meeting does not close unless another person starts a meeting with the same account. **No need to overbook.**
- No need to enter a batch or a recording option
- Create your meeting:
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Ff8a67ce8-fd06-4bf8-a2f2-f3d52280fd4f%2FScreenshot_2020-06-01_at_08.55.58.png?table=block&id=1a386465-2fc5-4488-9404-b8091142e413&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
### Open meeting on Zoom

![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F5b985cce-708d-4d3b-b9be-45ddb16ac3c8%2FScreenshot_2020-06-01_at_09.00.27.png?table=block&id=bcf2800f-7cdd-4896-b41d-3c671e061ac4&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)

## Non Kitt teachers
Ask someone from the Le Wagon team to create the zoom meeting with a paying account (they can use a Kitt teacher account and refer to the 1st section of this guide if they don't have a paying license on Zoom) for you. Make sure you get:
- the **zoom credentials** to host the meeting
- the **url** to share to students
> [!note] 
> Use one meeting for one lecture only (to release the Zoom account)
## Starting the lecture
At this point, you should have the **Zoom account's credentials** and the **meeting's url** to share to students.
Sign out from Zoom and sign in with the right credentials. You should see the scheduled meeting.
### Set virtual room on Learn
Then, sign in on Learn and open your batch. You should see an **Open Virtual Room** CTA in the navbar. **Click** it.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fbb1cf48a-d620-42b0-b991-ad75c40b45b0%2FScreenshot_2020-06-01_at_09.09.00.png?table=block&id=a4a592e2-b760-4d97-aa1c-e1204ba18382&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)

Grab a :coffee: and[join me on Zoom](https://zoom.us/j/95456107368?pwd=cUtlc3R***09)!​

1. **Sign out** from Zoom (it's important to release the Zoom account)
1. **Reset** the batch's zoom url on Learn:

# Tickets
We won't use Zoom for tickets, but our own [Jitsi](https://jitsi.org/) server. You can see Learn's ticketing feature as a lightweight version of Kitt's ticketing system.
The main differences to have in mind:
- a teacher / member of the staff has to **Open** the tickets at the end of the lecture and **Close** it at the end of the day (or for lunch break)
- there is no need to set yourself on / off duty
- tickets are never assigned to a teacher automatically
- just **Take** tickets from the list in the index as they flow in
When you take a ticket, it opens a Jitsi 1:1 video call on a different tab, wait for the student to connect and do the remote ticket normally (ask the student to share her/his screen, share snippets or urls in the chat, etc...).
At the end of the ticket:
- close the tab
- mark the ticket as solved

## Troubleshooting
If the ticket does not display properly on Jitsi, consider the following:
- Open the ticket in a private navigation window
- Disable all your browser's extension
- Make sure your browser allowed access to the Camera & Microphone on meet.lewagon.co
- Try changing networks (consider tethering if user's mobile has 4G)
- Uninstall Avast antivirus
