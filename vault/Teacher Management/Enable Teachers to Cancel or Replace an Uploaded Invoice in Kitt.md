## Context & Problem
> [!note] 
> TODO: Explain in great details the context surrounding this issue
<details><summary>**Suggested Checklist**</summary>
</details>
When a teacher uploads an incorrect invoice (wrong date, amount, or file), it happens they notice the mistake. However, they are unable to delete or replace it themselves in Kitt. Instead, they must message the Ops team to reject the invoice.
This adds unnecessary delays and increases manual workload on both sides.
Example:
- Teacher uploads incorrect invoice
        -
- Realizes mistake
        -
- Sends Slack message to Ops
        -
- Ops member manually rejects the invoice
        -
- Teacher re-uploads the correct version
        -
This repetitive and avoidable task could be streamlined by allowing teachers to cancel or replace the invoice directly before it’s approved.
