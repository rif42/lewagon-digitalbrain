# Teacher flag / Red flag
## Summary
> [!note] 
> TODO: Explain the problem in one sentence. ***Don’t think/talk about a solution ❌***​
Teachers flags/comments visible to the internal team to follow-up on teachers and their performance.
## Business Impact
> [!note] 
> TODO : Explain business impact of your issue
<details><summary>**Suggested Checklist**</summary>
</details>
We spend quite a lot of time to talk back and forth between city managers/program managers to have feedback from teachers and know if they are suitable for position.
Sometimes, a teacher can be staffed but had previously bad comments or feedback that we missed, so this impacts directly the NPS and the student experience.
## Context & Problem
> [!note] 
> TODO: Explain in great details the context surrounding this issue
<details><summary>**Suggested Checklist**</summary>
</details>
As teachers can be teaching over diffrent campus and/or online, it’s sometimes hard to follow-up on their “performance”. It would be good to be able to flag when needed or having a comment part only open and visible to the internal staff.
For example when I want to staff a teacher but I don’t know her/him, I could go check on the profile of the teacher and see the previous comments from city managers/program managers.
It is also good to follow up if they have been warned for something (behavior, lateness, absence without notice, etc). It could allow us to take action if some wrong behaviors are too repetitives.
