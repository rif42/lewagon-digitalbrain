# AI Product Builder
*📋 This document outlines all important information & documentation about the AI Product Builder course. *
## 👀 **AI Product Builder Course overview**
[![](https://info.lewagon.com/hubfs/Icon_Red-1.svg)Ship AI Products 10x Faster | AI Product Builder | Le Wagon](https://info.lewagon.com/ai-product-building-course)​
Not everyone has the time to put their career on hold for 9 weeks to do a training. The AI Product Builder track is designed with this in mind. It's a **5-day, full-time programme** (40 hours, Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm Paris time) that fits into a professional's life.
- **Short description:** Use AI and low-code tools to build and validate AI-powered product ideas and features.
- **Landing Page** : <https://info.lewagon.com/ai-product-building-course>
- **Syllabus English** (view) : <https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bAN_0M6hgytTuHfLW7kkyZfLEaxyHel8/view>
- **Length:** 5 days (full-time)
- **Format:** Online
- **Schedule:** Weekdays, immersive & hands-on
- **Price:** 1600€ or 1000€ (alumni)
