# Welcome! (README)
Welcome to Le Wagon! You’ve just joined a global network of 27,000 and counting tech and data professionals around the world.
Since 2013, we have taught people the skills they need to land their dream job in tech, launch their startup, or change careers. Through immersive bootcamps in web development and data, we offer a life-changing learning experience!
As a franchise, you’ll benefit from cutting-edge trainings regularly maintained by our education team. You’re authorized to market, sell, and deliver established in-person bootcamps, with a 6-month preparation period for new courses to allow for content development. To build a strong presence, you'll begin with one or two programs and later expand to others from our top offerings.
### **We aren’t your typical startup.**
We successfully bootstrapped for 7 years before we decided to raise funds to expand our programs and campuses. We have scaled steadily, and we constantly iterate on our products, curriculum and services to guarantee the best experience for our students and partners.
We are deeply connected with the local tech ecosystems in all of our locations, through hiring partners and relationships with tech communities, universities, VCs, and startup incubators.
No matter your role within the company, **our #1 responsibility is to our students**: ensuring they have the best learning experience with us and reach their post-learning goals.
### What makes Le Wagon unique.
Le Wagon pedagogy is based on immersive and intensive training that will allow you to acquire all the soft skills and techniques.
- 90% of practice on real-world tech projects with peers
- Get real-time answers to never be stuck by experienced professionals
- Lifetime access to Le Wagon’s learning platform
- Product-driven
- Unique Tech Community with +27K alumni and +3K teachers
### Why “Le Wagon”?
Our founders felt that traditional university degree courses were failing to provide students with the right tech skills — that **“higher education has missed the train”**. This was the first time they thought of the word “wagon” for our brand name.
After giving it some more thought, it seemed like a cool name for different reasons. We didn’t want a name using words like “hack”, “code”, “tech” or “app” — for us, **code is more than that**, it’s a means to develop products, solve problems and reach goals. And a wagon is a vehicle that takes a group of people (like a class of students) **on a journey to a destination**.
