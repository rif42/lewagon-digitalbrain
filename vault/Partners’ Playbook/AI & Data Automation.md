# AI & Data Automation
*📋 This document outlines all important information & documentation about the AI & Data Automation Course*
## 👀 **AI & Data Automation overview**
[AI & Data Automation | Le WagonGo from manual processes to automated workflows in 5 days. Learn APIs, AI agents & data pipelines. Built for professionals ready to automate. Le Wagon.https://info.lewagon.com/ai-data-automation-course](https://info.lewagon.com/ai-data-automation-course)
Not everyone has the time to put their career on hold for 9 weeks to do a training. The AI & Data Automation track is designed with this in mind. It's a **5-day, full-time programme** (40 hours, Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm Paris time) that fits into a professional's life.
- **Short description:** Automate your workflow and boost your productivity with AI. From connecting your first API to deploying autonomous AI agents.
- **Landing page **: <https://info.lewagon.com/ai-data-automation-course>
- **Syllabus** **English** (view) : <https://drive.google.com/file/d/1djPGpF0XVJFpOjXNfDKIxI_-fNpcjkXo/view?usp=sharing>
- **Length:** 5 days (full-time)
- **Format:** Online
- **Schedule:** Weekdays, immersive & hands-on
- **Price:** 1600€ or 1000€ (alumni)
