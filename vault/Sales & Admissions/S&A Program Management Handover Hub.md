## 1. Golden Rule & Guideline for Task Separation ⚔️​
---
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F67a5e9fd-3aaa-420d-9a08-c2ea2dbf9d02%2FUntitled.png?table=block&id=710899fd-d557-43ef-8bcb-b5508107442a&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
- **Admissions**: Responsible for admissions process; access to prep-work; payment questions until tuition is received*; compiling yellow-flags and reporting to batch managers;
- **Program** **Management****: Responsible for keeping track of prep-work completion; answering prep-work related questions; sending relevant onboarding and local information; answering city-sensitive information for students in Go;
*Depending on city-size and financial system
**Program Management responsibilities* can by city, **program or batch managers - *[depending on city size](https://app.notion.com/p/4cdaafdc54894e2f8591e39b6e0cff39)
