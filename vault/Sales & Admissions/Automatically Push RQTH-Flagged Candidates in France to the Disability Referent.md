## Context & Problem
> [!note] 
> TODO: Explain in great details the context surrounding this issue
<details><summary>**Suggested Checklist**</summary>
</details>
Florine Humbert is the designated disability referent for France and must personally follow up with each candidate who declares an RQTH status. This includes:
- Sending a dedicated information email
        -
- Offering a 1:1 call to discuss potential needs
        -
- Ensuring Le Wagon’s compliance with inclusion standards
        -
Currently, these candidates are not automatically surfaced or notified to Florine. They are identified via manual checks in the system based on tags or flags, which can easily be missed, especially during periods of high admissions activity.
To remain compliant and proactive, all RQTH-tagged or flagged applicants in France need to be automatically pushed to Florine Humbert so that she can take timely action.
