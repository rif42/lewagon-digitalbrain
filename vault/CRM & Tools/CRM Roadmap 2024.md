# 2024
2024 is a great number and looks set to be a thrilling one., our CRM strategy will pivot to not only accommodate the integration of our expanding product suite, including key additions like the Growth Marketing Bootcamp and Advanced Skill Courses, but also to refine and optimize every aspect of our lead management process. This year will be pivotal in leveraging our growing database, currently at 425K contacts, to maximize the conversion rate from leads to applications.
Our strategic CRM priorities are crafted to ensure a holistic and effective approach:
1. **Revamping Event Management (Q1 2024):** Recognizing the importance of events in our lead generation and engagement strategy, we will collaborate closely with the engineering team to enhance event management processes, ensuring a smooth transition from event registration to CRM integration.
1. **Last Post-CRM Revamp Cleaning (Q1-Q2 2024):** A major focus will be on maintaining a pristine CRM database. This means rigorous cleaning, data quality control, and GDPR compliance, setting a strong foundation for all our CRM activities.
1. **Enhanced Lead Qualification (Q1-Q2 2024):** We aim to refine our lead qualification process, focusing on improving the SQL to MQL ratio. This includes creating regional dashboard metrics, revising the qualification form to be more engaging and interactive, implementing a lead scoring system, and developing an automated re-qualification flow.
1. **Integration of Skill Courses & New Products (Q2-Q3-Q4 2024):** We are setting our sights on seamlessly incorporating our new offerings into the automated lead journey flows. This will involve targeted strategies for different segments such as No Go, Old Leads, Free Courses, and Students/Alumni, ensuring each group receives tailored communication that resonates with their unique needs and interests.
1. **Developing Templates & Guidelines for New Campaigns (Q2 2024):** As we launch new campaigns, such as scholarships and UK skills bootcamps, standardized templates and guidelines will be crucial for consistency and efficiency in our messaging.
1. **Boosting Engagement & Email Strategy (Q3 2024):** Our strategy will involve analyzing and optimizing email frequency and diversifying communication channels, including bots, SMS, WhatsApp, and emails, tailored to regional preferences.
1. **Lead Nurturing Enhancement (Q3-Q4 2024):** We will focus on improving collaboration with regional teams, sharing best practices, and analyzing regional performance to strengthen our lead nurturing efforts.
Each of these strategic priorities is designed to not only anticipate but proactively address the needs and opportunities presented by our expanding product line and growing contact base. Through these focused efforts, we aim to elevate our CRM effectiveness, driving stronger lead-to-application conversion rates and building lasting relationships with our prospects.
