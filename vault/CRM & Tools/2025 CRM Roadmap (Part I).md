# 👁️‍🗨️ 2024 - A Year of Milestones and Growth**
**
2024 was a year rich in projects and accomplishments! Taking a step back, we’re proud to say we adhered closely to our roadmap, tackling key initiatives such as:
1. **Revamping Event Management (Q1 2024)**
1. **Final Post-CRM Revamp Cleaning (Q1–Q2 2024)**
1. **Enhanced Lead Qualification (Q1–Q2 2024)**
1. **Integration of Skill Courses & New Products (Q2–Q4 2024)**
1. **Developing Templates & Guidelines for New Campaigns (Q2 2024)**
1. **Boosting Engagement & Email Strategy (Q3 2024)**
1. **Lead Nurturing Enhancement (Q3–Q4 2024)**
Beyond the roadmap, we collaborated with various teams, including Hariett, to deliver projects like the UK Gov Bod and Women in Tech initiatives, while also deepening our partnership with the S&A team. To better align with other teams' priorities, we introduced **CRM Framing Sessions**—a valuable initiative that enhanced cross-team planning. Though there’s room to refine the timing of these sessions, they remain an essential part of our strategy moving forward.
### Performance Metrics
While 2024 was a productive year, we fell short of achieving some of our key conversion rate (CVR) goals:
- **SQL/MQL CVR**: Target was 52%, but we reached **41%** across the core region (**48%** in Europe).
- **Applicant/SQL CVR**: Target was 27%, but we achieved **24.7%** across the core region (**26.4%** in Europe).
These results highlight areas for improvement as we refine our strategies and execution to close the gaps in 2025.
### Team Growth and Evolution
The CRM team also grew in 2024 with Edouard joining us, expanding our scope to become the **CRM & Ops Squad.** Diana’s arrival further strengthened our ability to deliver global campaigns while bringing invaluable expertise to the team.
2024 was a year of learning and growth. While we’ve made progress, there’s always room to improve—particularly in **internal communication** and **collaboration**, such as with the Acquisition team. These challenges are part of the journey, and we’re ready to address them head-on.
### Looking Ahead to 2025
The upcoming year will bring fresh objectives as we continue to build on our skills. Our focus will include:
- Leveraging data to better assess project and campaign impacts.
- Optimizing processes for project documentation and delivery.
- Highlighting our achievements to the broader team more effectively.
2024 set a strong foundation, and 2025 promises to be another exciting chapter in our journey!
