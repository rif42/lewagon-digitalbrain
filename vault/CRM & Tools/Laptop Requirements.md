> [!note] 
> It is absolutely crucial to have a **fully functional and recent laptop** for you to make the most of the bootcamp! Please make sure to go through this document and check your computer specs **before joining the Bootcamp**.
If you don’t have a computer with the following specs, you might not be able to follow the bootcamp.

## Internet connection for Online students 🌐​
---
If you’re following one of our Online courses, it is absolutely crucial that you have a **good internet connection** throughout the bootcamp. You will be working in a remote setting, so being able to connect online without any problems is **absolutely essential**.
Before joining the bootcamp, make sure to **test you Wi-Fi connection and speed** ⚡​
