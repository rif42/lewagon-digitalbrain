# Office App
### Signing up on Office
Any Kitt user has access to Office. The app used to be accessible only to Le Wagon employees, but we decided to make the app accessible to anyone who has access to Kitt. The first step is logging in using Kitt. You won't need to register manually.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F74b527ea-a4c8-4fd5-a258-7dd7dbbb6b69%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_5.26.55_pm.png?table=block&id=9368637c-7094-4448-8d89-51a81bea9b5e&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
### Cities' settings
As core team members, you will be automatically assigned to the cities you work in. This information is retrieved from Kitt and automatically set on the Office app.
If you are the first member from your city to join Office, you will first land on your city's setting page (shown below), where you'll be able to create your cities venues. This is a mandatory step to start using Office within your city.
The Staff Channel setting allows you to receive notifications related to your city. The channel must be created in the lewagon-alumni slack workspace. Notifications sent on this channel will include teacher requesting access to your city (more on that later), and teachers opening on Saturday for part-time batches. This is just to make sure you have teachers on campus when running a part-time batch on Saturdays.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F2109de16-89a6-4ba0-bffd-7fada5026202%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_5.30.45_pm.png?table=block&id=5f42d731-c155-4cd1-baaa-48ee066e830c&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
By scrolling down, you can also see all the staff members from your city listed on your city's settings page. As they sign up, they will be automatically added to the city. If you wish to add someone from another city, that's also possible by selecting a member from the dropdown menu and then clicking on Add Staff.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fb6b22f04-c87e-4da8-96bb-7ad7ef2fba62%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_5.35.58_pm.png?table=block&id=07efaeeb-3c6f-4e7c-97ee-6e43325a51ec&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Below the Staff section, there is the teachers' section, displaying all the teachers who requested access to your city.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F74313403-a2da-4d02-bd1d-d471b0f556d3%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_5.37.53_pm.png?table=block&id=429c0793-bddf-4986-9417-f5282d196274&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
### Connecting to Slack
Once at least one venue is created for the city, you can select your venue from the dropdown menu next to your avatar (top right), or click on the Office logo (top left). Please do not forget to link your slack account to make sure you receive appropriate notifications. Again, you should connect to the lewagon-alumni workspace. If the color assigned to you isn't to your liking, you can always change it by clicking on it and choosing another color 🎨​
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F1a3db369-bc48-4d48-bdb4-583e414e943f%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_5.48.33_pm.png?table=block&id=3c37e959-48ac-4568-8b67-863f7daab1e7&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
### City closed/open
The big green button at the top allows you to open or close your city. If the setting is set as closed, the Office app will automatically set your city as closed for the next day, until you set it as open again.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Ff4029350-a7c4-476e-b2b6-1b11171a7d7d%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_5.22.51_pm.png?table=block&id=c0aaf164-250e-46fc-a663-a6292410d13e&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
### Venues' settings
The little ⚙️ icon next to the venue name allows you to access the settings page of your venue (see below) where you can set your coworking space (if available) as open or close (more on that later). You'll also be able to change the name of your venue, change the maximum number of employees you'd like to have on campus (to respect social distancing), and add staff members to it
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F61e228e8-d009-487a-be25-e8ed360174c0%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_5.42.26_pm.png?table=block&id=e480b024-2181-4661-872c-49da1f7e2de1&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
### Set yourself as opening, office, wfh, or off
Each day has a default number of slots added to it (unassigned by default). Each team member can pick a slot and set themselves as either Opening the office, Working from the office, Working from home, or Off for the day. By clicking on one of the slots, a dropdown will appear. All you need to do is click on yourself to set yourself as working from the office. You can also unassign yourself by following the same procedure and clicking on Unassign slot. If all slots are taken, you can add some more slots by clicking on the little ➕ button on the right of the existing slots (until the limit is reached)
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F70da35dc-d484-410d-80bc-64e1d5ca051f%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_5.45.11_pm.png?table=block&id=9e743396-2aa1-44d1-92bc-b14866a0b873&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
If you'd like to work from home or set yourself as off, you can click on the three dots on the left and select the right option
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F4747c280-7d1e-4a22-8a88-d3826518d18b%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_5.47.10_pm.png?table=block&id=fe824612-7f40-4519-adf6-3f469fb32dce&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
You can also set your whereabouts for a whole week by clicking on the three dots next to Action in the week header.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fbe2a8229-53ff-4f30-b78f-a958fd5c7fd7%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_5.47.58_pm.png?table=block&id=65416cec-ffde-43a0-b4ff-b8211755371e&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)

### Events
If you have any events coming up, those events can also be added to the Office app by clicking on the little ➕ button next to the day's date. You'll have to enter your event's details (i.e. name, start time, end time, whether or not it happens during the whole day) and then click on Create Event
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F637cd274-9d8f-48d0-9c43-c02c631113d1%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_5.53.02_pm.png?table=block&id=f3f68d1e-dc29-40d0-ace3-4e50c094e8c5&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
You'll then be able to see the created events by clicking on the button that says X Events for each day.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F8c8f703f-dd20-47c3-829d-aab9f6507134%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_5.54.36_pm.png?table=block&id=5e004b59-8b7a-427e-91e2-0a7addd96545&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)

### Part-time
The part-time tab is where you'll ask your freelancer teachers to set their whereabouts for part-time sessions. It's a good way to keep track of who's going to be on campus and who's going to work from home. Only core staff members and teachers who requested access to your city will be able to use this page.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F39959003-786f-4d67-b013-89af23df8ac1%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_5.57.04_pm.png?table=block&id=359c7156-cd24-499c-b0f2-e8ff2a553168&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Teachers will first need request access to your city. They'll first need to select the city they want to have access to.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F3a2bb01a-a7fb-4eb7-9bac-8f4b8dce9567%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_6.22.04_pm.png?table=block&id=ffd95e22-2ea7-4c97-98ff-bdc6bd5b92da&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)

### Coworking
If the coworking space of your venue is enabled, Freelancers and teachers will be able to book a table in your coworking space to come and work from the campus. As core team members, you will first need to create tables with the right number of seats to make sure there are no overbooking. You'll be able to see all the bookings in the future by clicking on All bookings.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F1e83f6ea-77ed-4479-968a-9a1ab60a144b%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_6.05.20_pm.png?table=block&id=3d30be78-ab1d-4bfe-a8ce-036b0050f8a6&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Teachers and freelancers will then be able to click on Book your spot and select a date for their booking
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fa11fe451-65ef-4a0f-aa06-0fec51fa6f4b%2FScreenshot_2021-12-16_at_6.07.03_pm.png?table=block&id=b1046005-a7d9-4e85-9325-22c905fed425&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
### Notifications
By clicking on your avatar, a dropdown menu will appear with an option to access the notification preferences. You can enable/disable notifications at a city level.
