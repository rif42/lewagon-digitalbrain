# Enhance student tickets activity dashboard for PT students
## Context
Currently, teachers have the ability to see the average number of tickets made during the past 5 syllabus days for each student on the [dashboard](https://kitt.lewagon.com/camps/1992/dashboard). Thus, they do not have any information about the total number of tickets made during the whole batch, which would allow them to detect which students have more difficulties and which do not ask for help.
Furthermore, the tooltip is not accurately reflecting how the data is calculated (since it refers to camp days instead of syllabus days), making this data appears unreliable especially with PT and Flex batches.
## Solution
To solve this, the total amount of tickets per student has been added to the camp dashboard.
