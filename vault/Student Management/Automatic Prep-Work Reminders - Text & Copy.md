# Automatic Prep-Work Reminders - Text & Copy
## 1. Timing and Automatism ⚙️​
---
During 3 different moments, KITT will send a nudge to all applicants that are not at 100% completion of prep-work. These moments are calculated as per the batch start date:
- **5** **weeks** before;
- **3** **weeks** before;
- **10 days** before;
In each of the 3 moments the system will send, from the operations contact (Link missing), 3 different emails depending on the percentage of prep-work completion. This is to ensure that, depending on the completion, the tone and content of the emails is different.
1. **0-24%** completion - **Long-way to go**: Tone will be more directive and imperative to remind that a push is mandatory and needed;
1. **25-74%** completion - **Moving along:** Tone will be encouraging but directive;
1. **75-99%** completion - **Almost there:** Tone will be celebratory and encouraging to positively nudge to full completion;
## 2. Full Email Copy 📩​
---
The copy of the emails is not changed by city, but defined globally. These templates are not editable, but if you see any spelling or other mistakes, please do report them
