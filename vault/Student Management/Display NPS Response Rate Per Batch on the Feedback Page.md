While we collect NPS data centrally, we don’t currently display the **response rate per batch** on the results page. This means Program Managers and Ops teams must manually calculate the response rate for each batch to assess whether they are meeting our 80% response rate target.
Adding a simple display of the response rate per batch would streamline tracking, improve visibility, and allow more proactive follow-up on low engagement.
