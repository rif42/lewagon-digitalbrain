# Display students birthdays in batch calendar events
## Context
It was reported during the Cycle 18 Operations Framing session that the notifications currently sent to the batch managers channel were not actionable enough as they are sent in the morning, on the corresponding day.
In some cities, the student events calendar is leveraged to register all students birthdays during the batch, allowing staff to anticipate and buy a cake before arriving to the campus.
## Solution
**Expose birthdays in students Events calendar**
To save the time needed to manually register all students birthday, they are now automatically displayed on the corresponding day 👇​
