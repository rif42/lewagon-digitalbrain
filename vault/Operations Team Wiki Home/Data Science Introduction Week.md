## Typical day
⚠️ **Reminder : Lisbon and London need to adapt to the Paris time zone**
For **Full-Time students**, the schedule will run from **10:00am - 5:00pm (paris time zone)** each day, and follow the below format :
**Before 10am** : → Students watch the lecture of the day through Learn platform
**10am to 1pm** : → Students work on morning challenges. When stuck, they can request help through Learn tickets, where TAs provide support.
**1pm to 2pm** : → Lunch break
**2pm to 5pm**: → Students tackle afternoon challenges. They can request help through Learn tickets and join the Teacher's Zoom room for assistance. Students who complete challenges early may end their day. At 5pm, TAs share updates about the next day in the Learn forum before closing.
**Schedule per day :**
1. Data types, functions, and control flow
1. Lists and loops
1. Dictionaries
1. Classes
1. SQL Basics
**Only Full-time students **👉 **Monday - Friday : 10am - 5pm**
