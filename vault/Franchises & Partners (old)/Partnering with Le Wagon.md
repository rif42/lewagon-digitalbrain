Our hiring platform, **Huntr**, allows you to **search, filter, and connect** with job-ready alumni.
- Browse & filter talent by skills, experience, and location
- Easily post job listings to our Jobs Board
### 🎯 **2. Curated Candidate Shortlists**
Looking for a **tailored** hiring solution? We can provide **pre-filtered shortlists** of candidates that match your hiring needs.

Boost your job visibility by sharing your roles with **29,000+ tech professionals**.

Showcase your company and attract top talent through our **employer engagement initiatives**.
