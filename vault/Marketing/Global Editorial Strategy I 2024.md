> [!note] 
> **Why an editorial strategy?**
### 1️⃣ **Consistency**
It ensures that all content is consistent in terms of tone, style, and messaging. This helps to reinforce the company's brand and make it more recognizable to the target audience.
---
### 2️⃣ **Efficiency**
It helps to streamline the content creation process by providing clear guidelines and reducing the need for last-minute decisions or revisions. This can save time and resources.
---
### 3️⃣ **Effectiveness**
By aligning content with the needs and interests of the target audience, an editorial strategy can help to increase engagement and conversion rates. It also helps to ensure that content supports the company's business goals.

## **7. D****ISTRIBUTION**
---
**Our process is divided into 3 phases to ensure maximum impact:**
### 1️⃣ **Create and publish content**
Start by publishing your default content format, such as blog posts, videos, or podcasts. Start thinking about your secondary formats, even if you’re not able to produce them yet!
---
### 2️⃣** Share your content**
Distribute your published content within the team, on social media, or through paid campaigns to amplify its reach.
---
### 3️⃣** Repurpose your content**
Adapt your content to new formats for extended use. This can be a collaborative effort; for instance, someone else can create a blog post from your video transcript.
🔥 **Each piece of content begins in its default format and should be further adapted into additional formats**, to maximize engagement and effectiveness. Keep experimenting and innovating with content repurposing as a team! **For example:**

## 8. TRACKING PROCESS
> [!note] 
> To streamline our content creation and adaptation efforts, we'll use Google Sheets and shared Google Drive folders. By following this structured process, we enhance **collaboration**, maintain **clarity** on project statuses, and **maximize the effectiveness** of our content across various formats 💪 Here's how it works:
1️⃣** Track your content creation on Google Sheets**: [Le Wagon | Content creation tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/10oyr85lrCPXfYIB-KJrpU7fZ6XdAoGDr8EkTneP-N-w/edit?gid=0#gid=0)
*⚠️ When starting a new content project, add it to the Google Sheets tracker immediately. This ensures everyone has visibility into ongoing projects.*
---
2️⃣ **Once your content is ready,** update [Le Wagon | Content creation tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/10oyr85lrCPXfYIB-KJrpU7fZ6XdAoGDr8EkTneP-N-w/edit?gid=0#gid=0) and add your content to the Google Drive: [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1LDcW-YDQYQmdV5usaIWgRs8Kl2mdoXLW?usp=sharing)​
*⚠️ When adding your item to the folder, be sure to name the project by CONTENT TITLE_YEAR, ex: Data Science & AI Hero Video_2022*
---
3️⃣ **Enable team collaboration!**
Mark the status as complete and don’t forget to share the link with the team in our #marketing-content channel!
Other teams or regions might want to adapt and use your content.
