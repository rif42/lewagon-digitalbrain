# Printing Guidelines
> [!note] 
> Welcome to Le Wagon’s **printing guidelines** — your go-to reference for preparing, exporting, and printing all Le Wagon materials, from quick flyers to multi-page pamphlets.
---
## 🎨 1. Color Setup: Use CMYK for Printing
Le Wagon’s brand colors are designed for digital use (RGB).
However, **printers use CMYK color values**, so you’ll need to **convert our brand colors to their CMYK equivalents** before exporting your files for print.
**Why it matters:**
RGB colors look vibrant on screens, but may print dull or inaccurate if not converted properly.
**How to do it:**
- In **Figma**, install a plugin such as **“Color Space,” “RGB to CMYK,”** or **“TinyImage Compressor”** to convert color profiles.
- Check the CMYK values of Le Wagon brand colors in our [[Design Guidelines|Brand Guidelines]][[Design Guidelines|page.]]
- Always **proof your colors** with a test print before large batches — some colors (especially reds) may shift slightly.
> [!note] 
> *Pro tip:* Save your CMYK color styles in Figma as “Print Colors” to easily switch between digital and print versions when needed.

## 📄 2. Document Types & Recommended Settings
Le Wagon produces several printed materials. Here’s how to prepare them properly:
### **Flyers (1–2 pages)**
- Format: **A5 or A4**, depending on visibility needs.
- Bleed: **3 mm** on all sides.
- Margins: **at least 5 mm** inside safe area (no text near the edge).
- Paper: 120–160 gsm recommended for durability.
- Export: **PDF (Print)** with bleed and crop marks.
### **Pamphlets / Booklets (3–6 pages)**
- Format: Usually **A4 folded** (or custom sizes if needed).
- Set up pages individually in Figma (one per frame).
- For multi-page exports, use plugins like **Pitchdeck**, **PandaDoc Export**, or **Figma to PDF Export** to compile pages correctly.
- Add bleed (3 mm) and keep all important elements within safe margins.
- Export as **PDF (Print)** and double-check print order (especially if folded).
### **Posters & Large Prints**
- Format: Custom (A3, A2, or A1).
- Always check the printer’s required **DPI** (usually 300 dpi for best results).
- Export as **PDF or PNG (Print, 300 dpi)** with bleed.

## 🧰 3. Figma Tips for Print Design
Since most of our design work happens in Figma, here are key tips to prepare print-ready files:
✅ **Set your document in CMYK mode** using a plugin — Figma itself works in RGB by default.
✅ **Use “Frame” sizes** that match standard print formats (A4, A5, A3, etc.).
✅ **Keep text as vectors** (avoid rasterizing text or effects).
✅ **Avoid transparency or blending modes** that may not export correctly in print PDFs.
✅ **Add bleed manually** by extending backgrounds and images 3 mm beyond your frame edge.
✅ **Export as PDF for print**, not PNG/JPG — PDFs keep the best color accuracy and text sharpness.
📦 **Recommended plugins for Figma:**
- **Color Space** → Convert RGB to CMYK
- **TinyImage Compressor** → Export print-ready PDFs
- **Pitchdeck Exporter** → Compile multi-page PDFs
- **Mockup** → Preview how your printed material will look physically

## 🖨️ 4. Printing: Internal vs. External
### **Internal Printing (default option)**
Most everyday documents (flyers, posters, small brochures) can be printed **directly at your campus**.
- The **Campus Manager** usually manages access to the campus printer.
- Before printing, **share your PDF file** with the CM and check:
        - Paper size (A4, A5, etc.)
        - Double-sided settings
        - Color vs. black-and-white mode
        - Correct page orientation
> [!note] 
> *If you’re unsure, ask your CM to do a quick test print before printing in bulk.*
---
### **External Printing (for complex or high-quality needs)**
For larger runs or professional finishes (lamination, folding, binding, etc.), use a **professional printing company**.
**When to use external printers:**
- Booklets or pamphlets with more than 4 pages
- Events or admissions materials requiring premium quality
- Anything with special finishes (matte, glossy, thick cardstock, etc.)
Before sending files externally:
- Make sure the **export format matches their specs** (usually CMYK PDF with 3 mm bleed and crop marks).
- Ask for a **print proof** to validate color and layout before mass printing.
---
## ✅ 5. Quick Checklist Before Printing
**Before sending any document to print:**
- [ ] Converted all colors to CMYK
- [ ] Added 3 mm bleed on all sides
- [ ] Checked safe margins for text (5 mm minimum)
- [ ] Exported as “PDF for Print”
- [ ] Checked DPI (300 dpi minimum for images)
- [ ] Proofread text and layout
- [ ] Confirmed paper size and type with Campus Manager or printer
- [ ] Test printed one copy to check color accuracy
