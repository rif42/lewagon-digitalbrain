### 2. Check the rights
Most of the pictures can be used for social media, roll-ups and on our website.
If you want to use some pictures for an OOH campaign or other type of media, check the rights:
[Scope of rights](https://app.notion.com/p/lewagon/Scope-of-rights-3f96ede5c433456dad93b97de414b786?pvs=25)
The scope of rights is composed as follows:
- use: on which types of material the pictures or videos must be used
- territory: are the pictures usable in all countries?
- media: do we have the right to use these pictures with media buying ? Ex: I plan to get a OOH campaign, I must check that I have the rights to use this picture for this kind of media.
- duration: beyond the validated duration, we must renew the rights to continue to use the pictures
