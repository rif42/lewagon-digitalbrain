### **3️⃣ Content creation:**
Once you’ve received the Purchase Order or contract (workshops only), you will have approximately 10 working days to create content based on the agreed topics and format, such as articles, infographics, or videos.
### **4️⃣ Content optimization:**
Upon submission, Le Wagon’s team will review and optimize your content to align with our brand standards. This may involve refining tone, improving clarity, or incorporating branded visuals.

You can share invoices to Le Wagon in 1 of 2 ways:
1. **Monthly invoices**: At the end of every month, you can share an invoice for all content produced.
1. **Content specific invoices**: Once a piece of content is complete and approved by Le Wagon, you can share an invoice directly.
