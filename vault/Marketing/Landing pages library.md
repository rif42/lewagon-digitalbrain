# How to create a landing page
---
> [!note] 
> **Mixing V4 and V5 modules on the same page can result in styling issues. It's advisable not to mix them. If you must do so, please ensure you thoroughly test the styling in both live Desktop ****AND**** mobile modes.**
1. Go to **HubSpot** > **Marketing** > **Landing pages** > **Create** > **Landing** **page**
1. Name your page
1. Go at the bottom of the page and click on “**Other templates**”
1. Go to page 3 (approximately)
1. Find “**Landing V5**” then click on “**Select template**”
1. 🎉 You can now add V5 modules in your page!

## 🏆 Quick tips to improve your landing page
> There are a few ways to easily improve your landing page thanks to HTML!
> ℹ️ You can put your text in **bold**, underline, *italic*, or in a specific color **directly in a HubSpot module **
### Put some text in **bold**
```
My text in<b>bold</b>​
```
Result:
> [!note] 
> My text in **bold**
### Underline my text
```
My text<u>underlined</u>​
```
Result:
> [!note] 
> My text underlined
### Colorize my text
```
My text in<spanstyle="color: red">red</u>​
```
You can also use a specific color code like the [[Design Guidelines|branded colors of Le Wagon]]:
```
My text in<spanstyle="color: #371373">purple</u>​
```
Results:
> [!note] 
> My text in red
> [!note] 
> My text in purple
---
### Put some text in *italic*
```
My text in<i>italic</i>​
```
Result:
> [!note] 
> My text in *italic*
### Line break
One <br> = one return ; Two <br> = two returns (one empty line)
```
My first line<br>My second line<br><br>My third line​
```
Result:
> [!note] 
> My first line
My second line
My third line
### Link
```
My text has a<ahref="www.lewagon.com">link</a>​
```
Result:
> [!note] 
> My text has a [link](http://www.lewagon.com/)
### Image
```
<imgsrc="https://www.lewagon.com/assets/v4/logo-lewagon-9c19fb39a748cd3b1f49059ce0dc6c0dfc4cc2447d5a9a3e01bd2d5a214faf3c.svg">​
```
Result:

> Le Wagon logo

## ✅ The landing page checklist
---
1. **Have a Clear Objective:** Before you begin, understand what the **primary purpose** of the page is. Is it to capture leads? Promote a new offer? Share information about an upcoming event? The landing page's design, content, and call-to-action (CTA) should be aligned with this objective.
1. **Clear and Concise Headline:** The headline is the first thing visitors see, so make it strong and clear. It should communicate the value proposition quickly and effectively
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F74612a43-3eff-4c0e-a3fd-73972b2fdd7f%2FCapture_decran_2023-06-28_a_17.54.30.png?table=block&id=ee32179d-b2b6-40ad-9213-23cdb319d0cc&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1600&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fe213bc0d-d731-4359-9331-a7fd52eafc92%2FCapture_decran_2023-06-28_a_17.54.57.png?table=block&id=346e8edb-9555-4ec0-b6f2-ca05c59cb5f0&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1600&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
1. **Compelling Call to Action (CTA):** Every landing page needs a CTA guiding visitors towards your goal conversion. It should be prominent and compelling.
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fa371bd1e-c179-44a9-adb3-5efeb3d343c8%2FCapture_decran_2023-06-28_a_18.01.42.png?table=block&id=1c16b0e3-c2e1-4feb-a29a-17b85abdb24e&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1600&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
1. **Trust Indicators:** These could be testimonials, customer logos, or security badges. They provide social proof and help build trust with the audience.
1. Share the **benefits**, not the features
1. Give a sens of **FOMO**
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fa87980bb-f493-4d95-b615-61c6ce42d2e8%2FCapture_decran_2023-06-28_a_18.00.47.png?table=block&id=1158ef37-3a5c-46da-a6b0-c61ca57931cb&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1600&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
