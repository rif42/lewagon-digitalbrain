# 🖋️ Ad copy
### Retargeting Copy Categories
---
**There are 6 message categories: **
- **Job/Outcome focus** - explain what you can do after the bootcamp
- **Funding options/Accessibility focus** - highlight the local funding options accessible to finance the bootcamp
- **Money/Salary focus**** **- emphasize the salary increase you can make with Le Wagon’s bootcamp
- **Status/Emotion focus**** **- leverage a potential emotion the lead is feeling
- **Benefit focus**** **- showcase the value proposition of Le Wagon
- **Format focus**** **- explain the advantage of a specific format
### How to craft compelling copy
---
**Generic tips:**
- Remember the 3 Cs of copywriting: **clear, concise, compelling**
- **Know your audience** - Tailor your language to resonate with your target demographic.
- **Maintain Brand Consistency **-** **Align with your brand voice and values.
- **Optimize Readability **-** **Use formatting for easy reading.
- **Focus on benefits **- Emphasize how your product or service directly improves the customer's life.
**Retargeting ad asset specific tips: **
- Ensure your **copy resonates with your target audience** by providing ample context.
Consider whether a viewer would easily grasp who we are, what we do, and the subject matter at hand.
- Focus on** ****one**** compelling argument or selling point **per visual, ensuring a clear and impactful message.
- **Contextualize Le Wagon effectively** by incorporating terms such as tech, skills, training, data, course, etc.
- Crafting impactful ad copy involves choosing between **two effective structures**. You can opt for a well-crafted and complete title, or enhance it with a second line providing additional context. This second line, essentially an argument consistent with your title, can take various forms:
        - **Differentiator** - highlight what sets Le Wagon apart from its competitors, showcasing our unique strengths and advantages.
        - **CTA** - Propel your audience into action with a compelling call-to-action, guiding them towards the next step in their journey.
        - **ROI** - Emphasize the tangible outcomes of taking a course with Le Wagon, underlining the value and benefits students can expect.
        - **Solution** - Address your audience's needs by presenting Le Wagon as the solution to a problem. This is particularly effective following a question, providing a clear path forward.
        - **Figure** - Reinforce your message with a key figure, offering proof/ reassurance. Whether it's a statistic or a noteworthy achievement, use a figure to substantiate our credibility and impact.
