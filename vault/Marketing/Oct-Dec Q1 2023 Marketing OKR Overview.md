___________________________________________________________________
A. ****Need for a speedy launch **🏎️ **vs More time needed scope launch **📝
**
The launch work was building up in the background** ⚠️ with already some delay. As an organization, we needed to launch fast but we underestimated the number of tasks and the time **it would take to launch**.
** It made our job challenging in terms of target setting, asset production and website preparation, and GTM coordination.
**In the meantime, we were still trying to figure out who does what centrally. Starting to understand how to work together with NP, online team - while still not fully functional on all our global channels, and with regional leads “learning how to work with us”**
💡: Conclusion
**- We need more time to launch. We need a better process to launch
- We need to integrate team members into our marketing function to work with less friction**
B. ****Impact on global marketing roadmap **📝
Quietly, we were building up a backlog in all our marketing roadmaps as information came day by day and we had to stop a number of actions to make launches happen. We haven’t built our marketing stack for that and our tools and processes were not ready
**💡: Conclusion
**- We need to improve our processes & tools to go faster
**
****C. The good and the bad****
****☀️ Silver lining: The launches were done & live**🟢 ** but with some delays **🟠
**
**The good side of this, most of the critical documents have been done to launch in record time.
**
**🌤️ ** Silver lining: it could have been more efficient and we created a backlog in the rest of our roadmap **🟠**
The bad side of this is that we took significant delays impacting both our evergreen roadmaps and our new product roadmaps.
