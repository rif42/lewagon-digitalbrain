# **Q1 OVERVIEW
**
> [!note] 
> **1573 students in H1. 92% of the target was reached.
Q1: **Strong Q1 quarter in students (99% students achieved)
**Q2: **Okay Q2 quarter (88% of students achieved) with very solid signs of acceleration.
Notably, strong Q2 quarter in Leads (103% of target). However, CVR lead to apply lower (16% vs 19%).
- Quality of traffic & level of spending could have been more efficient.
a. Too slow to execute w/ agencies + delay in dashboard creation to better monitor perf
b. Haven’t spent the full budget in some key cities due to ok monitoring and Cash management issues
- Relaunched events globally and we’re seeing very clear trends of attendees going up (critical for our growth).
- Great progress in CRM (lead scoring + improving tool setup + B2B setup) but we need to prioritize revamping our lead nurturing flow in Q2 in order to continue to be strong in our funnel CVR (main Q2 priorities for the Growth team)
**Dashboard-wise,** we’ve released dashboards that have changed the way our organization is working. The next step is to automate them and improve them (growth ops role posted) and create new ones for paid only (better monitoring)
**Brand-wise**, we’re moving mountains but we got let down by one of our agencies. So, we’re catching up (delay of 1.5mo in release). We’ve achieved a new brand positioning and we’re close to launching a new brand + new website. We hired 2 strong profiles for the team.
**Team-wise**, we’ve created new roles at the regional level. We’ve also started new regional cadences with GMs + MMs. We’ve also recognized the need for more senior skills on our teams and we’re adjusting to this. Hiring Regional leads is our priority in Q2.
