Given the highly technical nature of the content we aim to produce, we will engage a diverse pool of Le Wagon-approved experts, both from outside and within our community, specializing in Web, AI, Product, Growth, and more.
These experts will receive a brief on Le Wagon's editorial strategy, including the key topics, tone of voice and content production process from the Central Brand team as part of their onboarding.
Check out the full list of Experts here: [😎Ressources I Expert content hub](https://app.notion.com/p/Ressources-I-Expert-content-hub-12050800181849358d11b00be1a17ddf?pvs=24)
1. **COLLABORATION PROCESS**
---
**1️⃣ Expert onboarding & topic familiarization →
**Experts who join our pool are briefed on our editorial strategy, focusing on tech topics that align with our brand's vision and their expertise. This is a continual process of topics.
---
**2️⃣ Continuous topics suggestions →
**Experts, along with the Marketing Team (including Brand, Acquisition, and Product Marketing), can continuously suggest article subjects they’d like to produce.
---
**3️⃣ Article selection →
**Once the marketing team has selected the content, a [Purchase Order](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Zy1h-3ZiOJjlo3SG7PSNYlX56ulsxzXh/edit) must be created and filled in accordingly. Once completed, share the PO with the legal team via the **#marketing-content-creation-contracts** channel so that it can be sent to the freelancer via DocuSign.
**4️⃣ Content production →
****
**Experts will have ≈10 working days to write each article.
*Please keep in mind experts most likely have a full-time job and write articles on the side! *
---
**5️⃣ Publication →
****
**The Central Brand team (alongside the relevant support eg. Product Marketing) will validate, eventually add some imagery and publish the articles.
---
**6️⃣ Content Variations**** →
**The Central Brand team will lead efforts to develop content variations for infographics, social media channels, nurturing emails, and other marketing initiatives.
> [!note] 
> **Local adaptation & distribution
**Articles will be written in English only.
The Central team will publish content on the Global platforms.
Regional Marketing teams may choose to cover translation costs for this content, to adapt it for their local blog language and publish it on their local channels.
