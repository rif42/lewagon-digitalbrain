# Competitors - Data Science
### **Direct Competitors** (European Bootcamps):
1. **Ironhack (Data Science & Machine Learning Bootcamp)**
        - **Price**: €6,800 - €8,000
        - **Strengths**: Offers live online classes, hands-on projects, and solid career services. It’s designed for beginners and career-changers.
        - **Weaknesses**: Some feedback indicates teaching staff may lack deep real-world experience.
        - **Link**: [Ironhack](https://www.ironhack.com/gb/data-science-machine-learning/remote)
1. **Jedha Bootcamp (Data Scientist Fullstack)**
        - **Price**: €7,495
        - **Strengths**: Focuses on practical training with personalized support and face-to-face training. Also includes AI and machine learning modules.
        - **Weaknesses**: Lacks deeper theoretical content, which might be a disadvantage for some job markets.
        - **Link**: [Jedha](https://www.jedha.co/formations/formation-data-scientist)
1. **Neue Fische (Germany)**
        - **Price**: €7,500
        - **Strengths**: Hands-on learning, small class sizes, and strong job placement services. Well-connected with local German companies.
        - **Weaknesses**: Primarily for German speakers, limiting international reach.
        - **Link**: [Neue Fische](https://www.neuefische.de/en/bootcamp/data-science)
1. **SPICED Academy (Germany)**
        - **Price**: €8,000
        - **Strengths**: Offers comprehensive training in data science with strong focus on projects and portfolio building.
        - **Weaknesses**: Highly selective admission process.
        - **Link**: [SPICED Academy](https://www.spiced-academy.com/en/program/data-science)
1. **DataScientest (France)**
        - **Price**: €6,900
        - **Strengths**: Hybrid learning model with certification from La Sorbonne, covering everything from data management to machine learning.
        - **Weaknesses**: Less hands-on than other bootcamps.
        - **Link**: [DataScientest](https://datascientest.com/formation-data-scientist)
### **Indirect Competitors** (Online Platforms):
1. **Coursera (Data Science Bootcamps)**
        - **Price**: $975 - $13,000 (~€900 - €12,200)
        - **Strengths**: Self-paced learning, highly flexible, partnerships with top tech companies like IBM and Google.
        - **Weaknesses**: Lacks the immersive experience and career services of traditional bootcamps.
        - **Link**: [Coursera Data Science Programs](https://www.coursera.org/)
1. **DataCamp**
        - **Price**: €300 annually
        - **Strengths**: Low-cost, self-paced courses focusing on specific data science skills. Ideal for professionals looking to upskill quickly.
        - **Weaknesses**: Not as comprehensive as a full-time bootcamp.
        - **Link**: [DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/)
1. **Springboard (Data Science Career Track)**
        - **Price**: $11,300 (~€10,300)
        - **Strengths**: Offers mentorship and job guarantees, with strong career support.
        - **Weaknesses**: Higher cost than most online programs and requires some prior knowledge.
        - **Link**: [Springboard](https://www.springboard.com/)
