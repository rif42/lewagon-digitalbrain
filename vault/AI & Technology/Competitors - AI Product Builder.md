# Competitors - AI Product Builder
## Le Wagon Positionning
There is no direct competition in France or Europe on our specific format. Other bootcamp companies offering similar content target career changers and require longer commitments (at least 3 months) at significantly higher price points.
With this course, Le Wagon positions itself in the **upskilling industry**, not the career change market. The AI Product Builder is designed for professionals already working in tech who want to level up fast.
---
## **Competitors**
### **1. **[Builders Camp](https://www.builderscamp.com/)** (Online, Portugal-based)**
The closest competitor to Le Wagon's AI Product Builder in terms of content and tools. They offer a **membership model** (€599/year or €749 lifetime) giving access to 40+ bootcamps, including:
- *Using AI to Build Your First Product* (4 weeks) - from idea to launched MVP using Vibe Coding
- *Building with Lovable* (2 weeks) - full-stack prototypes without writing code
- *Building with Cursor* (2 weeks) - AI-assisted development
- *Zero to Shipped with Vibe Coding* (1 week)
- *From Idea to Launch with AI* (1 week)
- *AI Product Management* (4 weeks)
They have 4,000+ alumni and a strong online community. However, their model is subscription-based and fully asynchronous, with no in-person or city-based experience.
### **2. **[General Assembly](https://generalassemb.ly/)** (Online)**
They offer paid 4-hour workshops (€299) covering:
- AI Agents
- AI Fundamentals
- AI for Product Managers
They also advertise short part-time courses (32h, online) on:
- AI Workplace Fundamentals
- AI-First Product Management
- Project Management with AI
- Agentic AI Strategy
- Building AI Agents
Note: as of April 2026, most of their course pages return a 404 error, suggesting the offer is not yet live or has been taken down.
### **3. **[La Capsule](https://www.lacapsule.academy/formation-ia-automation-nocode/full-time-paris)** (France)**
**Formation IA & Automation - Product Builder NoCode**
- **Duration:** 10 weeks (full-time or part-time)
- **Price:** from **€5,000**
- **Format:** In-person (Paris) or remote
- **Tools:** ChatGPT, Make, n8n, Airtable, Bubble, Webflow, Langflow, Pinecone, Voiceflow
- **Highlights:** "Concepteur de solutions NoCode" diploma, career center included, rated 4.98/5 across 400 reviews
- **Angle:** Focused on business process automation and no-code, primarily targeting career changers and upskilling profiles
### 4. [Liora / DataScientest](https://liora.io/formation/data-ia/product-builder-no-code) (France, Remote)
**Formation Product Builder No-Code & IA**
- **Duration:** 3 months (bootcamp) or 7.5 months (part-time)
- **Price:** **€7,990** (CPF-eligible)
- **Format:** 100% remote
- **Tools:** Webflow, Airtable, Notion, Make, Figma, Zapier, Voiceflow, Bubble, Shopify, Power BI
- **Highlights:** RNCP certification (level 6), Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne partnership, rated 4.7/5 on Google
- **Angle:** Broader scope covering Data Product Management, UX/UI, and BI in addition to no-code
