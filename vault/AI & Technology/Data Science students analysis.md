# Data Science students analysis
> [!note] 
> Data collected from 01/2020 to 10/2024. Data Science application that became students in European regions. More information on [metabase dashboard](https://metabase.lewagon.com/dashboard/297-applications-insights?application_date=2020-01-01~2024-10-14&training_type=Bootcamp&course=Data%20science%20%26%20AI&region=France&region=Germany&region=Global&region=Southern%20Europe&region=UK&region=NECH&lead_source=&personal_source=&became_student%253F=true)
### Key takeaways from data science bootcamp applications:
1. **Academic background**:
        - The majority of applicants have a **master’s degree** (48%), followed by those with a **bachelor’s degree** (25%).
        - There is a significant portion with **phds** (7%), indicating interest from highly educated individuals.
        - A smaller, but notable, number come from **high school** (4%) or have **no formal education** (1%), suggesting a varied education background.
1. **Age distribution**:
        - The largest age group is around **22.5 years** (35%), closely followed by those around **30 years** (30%).
        - There are applications from individuals in their **late 30s** (8%) and **45+ years** (3%), indicating a mix of early-career and mid-career professionals.
1. **Gender**:
        - The majority of applicants are **men** (65%), with **women** representing **23%**, and **unknown** gender entries making up **9%**.
        - The disparity highlights an opportunity for initiatives to encourage more women to apply.
1. **Experience level and personas**:
        - The most common persona is **builder - upskiller** (15%), across various experience levels, showing many applicants seek to **improve their current skills**.
        - **Switcher - meaning** (8%) is also common, reflecting individuals looking to **change careers** for more purpose.
        - There’s a smaller but distinct number of **entrepreneurs** (3%) and **gap year students** (1%), suggesting diversity in goals and motivations.
1. **Application trends**:
        - **Word of mouth** (41%) is the top source for applications, followed by **online research** (32%), meaning **reputation and visibility** play a crucial role.
        - **Online reviews** (6%) and **events** (4%) also contribute significantly, underscoring the importance of **community engagement** and **networking**.
1. **Popular application periods**:
        - Application peaks are observed in **august 2022** (4%), **march 2022** (3%), and **september 2022** (3%), with a consistent flow in **autumn and spring months**.
        - Applicants tend to plan well in advance, suggesting the need to target campaigns months ahead of these peaks.
1. **Career background**:
        - Many applicants come from **traditional companies** (29%) and **tech companies** (13%), showing a mix of traditional and modern industry backgrounds.
        - A significant portion are **students or recent graduates** (14%), a prime target for early-career professionals.
### Insights for target persona:
- The **typical applicant** is likely a **male in his early 20s or 30s** with a **master’s degree** and **looking to upskill** or **change careers**.
- Applicants come from both **traditional sectors and tech companies**, but there’s also an entrepreneurial streak in some.
- **Word of mouth** and **researching online** are the primary sources for new applicants, so maintaining a strong **online presence** and **positive reputation** are crucial.
- There’s an opportunity to **broaden gender diversity** and attract more women into the programme.
