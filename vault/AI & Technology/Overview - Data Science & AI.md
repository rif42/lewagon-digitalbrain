# Overview - Data Science & AI
### 🚀 **Data Science & AI bootcamp overview**
**📌 Summary**
Le Wagon’s Data Science & AI Bootcamp equips you with the skills to build and deploy intelligent systems using Python, machine learning, and modern AI frameworks. In just 2 months, you’ll go from data wrangling and statistical modeling to deep learning and MLOps—perfect for curious minds ready to shape the future of tech and innovation.
---
### 📅 **Duration & format**
- **Length:** 2 months (full-time)
- **Format:** Online or on-campus
- **Schedule:** Weekdays, hands-on & immersive
---
### 🧠 **Prepwork**
- **Time:** 30–40 hours
- **Topics Covered:**
        - Python foundations & terminal commands
        - High school level math (algebra, stats)
        - Git, version control & notebook tools
        - Optional live Python intro sessions
---
### 📚 **Modules at a glance**
1. **Data Analysis** – Python, SQL, Pandas, data sourcing & visualizations
1. **Decision Science** – Hypothesis testing, regression, consulting challenge
1. **Machine Learning** – Supervised/unsupervised models, pipelines, XGBoost
1. **Deep Learning** – Neural networks, transformers, Hugging Face, GenAI
1. **MLOps & Engineering** – APIs, Docker, MLflow, FastAPI, cloud deployment
1. **Generative AI** – ChatGPT, Copilot, ethical & explainable AI, CI/CD
1. **Final Project** – Design and present a real AI product with a team
---
### 🛠️ **Key tools & technologies**
- Python, SQL, Jupyter, Git, Pandas, Scikit-learn
- TensorFlow, Keras, Hugging Face, Streamlit
- BigQuery, Google Cloud, Docker, FastAPI, MLflow
- ChatGPT 4.0, Copilot, LangChain, Statsmodels
---
### 🎯 **Who it’s for**
- STEM graduates or professionals pivoting to data careers
- Product or business professionals ready to upskill in AI
- Curious minds eager to build intelligent tools and models
- Career changers with foundational coding & math skills
---
### 🎓 **Career outcomes**
- **Roles:** Data Scientist, ML Engineer, AI Consultant, MLOps Engineer
- **Support:** 1:1 coaching, technical mock interviews, project reviews
- Lifetime access to Le Wagon’s career platform and hiring companies
