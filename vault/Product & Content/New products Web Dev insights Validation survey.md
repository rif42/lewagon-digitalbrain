# [New products] Web Dev insights Validation survey
We sent to 600 web dev gaduates from 2023 a validation survey. This survey aims at gathering insights on the different gaps they identify in our web dev program, difficulties they encounter while looking for a job and what kind of skills they would like to develop further after graduating.
With these insights we are looking to validate our hypothesis about a one week Optional Javascript advanced course available at the end of the bootcamp.
