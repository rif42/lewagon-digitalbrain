# Best products
Here are the best products from your web dev & data science alumni. **The aim is to have a great selection of products that we can update regularly on our website. **
# How can you use this page?
- **Bootcamp managers / teachers**: at the end of each batch, you can add the 3 "best" products in the table below.
- **Marketing managers**: use it to make sure your local products are up-to-date on your local course pages. You will just need to add a short description and a screenshot in the [admin](https://www.lewagon.com/admin) under Program(s) page > Products. We will hence be able to update our global selection accordingly.
