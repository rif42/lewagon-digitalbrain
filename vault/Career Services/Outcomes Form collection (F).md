# Outcomes Form collection (F)
### This page is shared with our franchises.
---
## **Why do we send this form?**
---
- Following the [[Career Form W1 (F)|!(https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fprod-files-secure.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2F2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f%2F222abe01-abc2-4e2e-997a-9e333acc9c37%2Ffile_10329927.png?table=block&id=1ee78903-5bf2-4e0e-8084-e49f87c0e0b2&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=200&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=renderMentionIcon)Career Form W1 (F)]] , this **form** is used to track **students’** **progress** during their careers **6 months** after the bootcamp conclusion on Demo day. Through their answers, we are able to produce [No access](https://app.notion.com/p/706ed8a64c204489b7f0091556dce315?pvs=24#706ed8a64c204489b7f0091556dce315)
- With** Job Reports** ready, we are able to advertise our updated **JPR** per quarter and other metrics to all **Le Wagon community** among alumni, future students, leads, press and other **stakeholders**;
> [!note] 
> To **summarize** - the **main** **purpose** **of** the sending the **Outcomes** **form** is to **get** **first** **hand** **information** from the **student** directly 6 months after their graduation, to **understand** what happened on their **post**-**bootcamp** careers to be able to help on making better decisions to improve our **Job Placement Rates** and events focused on these graduates and all our alumni community.
## **How does it work?**
---
### ***When*** ⏳​
The** Outcomes form** is sent to the graduated students 6 months after their Demo day.
### ***How*** ⁉️​
The** Outcomes form** is sent via **Kitt** **Bot 🤖** through the slack integration feature. The sender can be changed by changing the setting in **Kitt. **This message is in English. To understand your region's reminder process, please consult your [Not found](https://app.notion.com/p/845df43a75854b63a1588504874855d2?pvs=24#845df43a75854b63a1588504874855d2)
### ***Who***** 👥**​
The** Outcomes** **form** is sent to every graduated student in all Le Wagon bootcamp courses. (B2C, B2G)
### ***Goal***** 🎯**​
The **completion** **rate** of the** Outcomes form** for each quarter is at least **75%**,** **in order to achieve a better accurate Job Reports.
## **What’s the content of this form? **
---
The **Outcomes form** is divided in **3** **main** **sections** 👇​
### **Career Path Options**
---
- **Employment status?**
        - FT/PT Job
        - Internship/ Apprenticeship
        - Freelancer
        - Previous Job
        - Back to studies
        - Entrepreneur
        - Unemployed
---
### **Career Path Details**
---
- If **employed** - begin date, company info, location, salary, industry, job title
- If **unemployed** - field of study, degree, reason (sabbatical, maternity/paternity leave, personal/ professional projects)
- **Visa Permit**
---
### Course Evaluation
---
- **Useful skills** learnt during bootcamp
- **HR contact** details
- Interest in providing **mentorship**
- **Career fulfillment**
- Post-bootcamp **technologies learnt**
- **Late NPS** and its explanations
