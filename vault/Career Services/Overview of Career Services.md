# Overview of Career Services
---
> [!note] 
> **What career support does Le Wagon provide to Graduates?**
Our program is specifically designed to support students in their next steps after the bootcamp, whether they want to find a new job, build a company, or kick off their freelancer career.
Career support is self-paced and consists of three main programs
- Job Seeker Program
- Founders Program
- Freelancers Program
Any student is welcome to join more than one program.

### How to start using **Job Seeker Program 💼**​
Once you submitted Career Form in week 1 of your bootcamp
- We have sent you an **email with access content on Learn**
- We have sent you an **email with Huntr activation link**
Next steps:
1. Go to our Learn platform and watch introduction video and copy your Workbook.
1. Based on instructions, submit 5 mandatory assignments to your Career Advisor.
1. You will be then invited to Live Workshops, which we run every week.
1. Once you attended 4 mandatory Live Workshops you will get a link to book 1:1 with your career advisor and you will be visible to our hiring partners in Talent Pool.
