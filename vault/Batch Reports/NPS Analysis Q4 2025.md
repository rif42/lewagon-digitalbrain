## 💬 Qualitative analysis
### 🔹 Feedback themes all programs combined
❤️ **Most liked commun themes across cities and bootcamps**
- Teacher/TA support and availability (190 mentions)
- Community and cohort atmosphere (93 mentions)
- Hands-on projects and practical learning (59) mentions
❌ **Least liked commun themes across cities and bootcamps **
- Lecture pacing and timing (38 mentions) rushed segments, and overlap with project weeks cited.
- Challenge instructions clarity and steps (37 mentions) sometimes unclear instructions or overly step-by-step guidance causing confusion or loss of focus.
- Cohort management (33 mentions) missing preparation from teachers or disappointing level, group size/composition affecting learning dynamics.
