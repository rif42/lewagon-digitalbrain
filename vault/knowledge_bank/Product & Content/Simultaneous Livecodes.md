# Simultaneous Livecodes
## 💡 Overview - Solution ideation
*Describe what is the problem and what potential solutions we might have to solve it.*
Livecode has been a sensitive topic for a while. While some batches seem to take to it naturally, other batches are incredibly resistant to it. In either case, what we have noticed is that many students zone off, turn off their cameras, don’t contribute, or, even worse, leave for the day earlier.
This has been observed for a while and initially the approach taken (at least in Brazil) was to reinforce importance and enforce attendance through teachers. There has been no noticeable impact on students satisfaction or interest in livecodes even with reinforced surveillance and encouragements.
The new solution, which will be tested in Web FT Batches for Q3/23, is to have two simultaneous livecodes. The first will be the usual livecode present on Kitt, which will be hosted by the on-site teacher; the second will be a challenge of any given day that either encapsulates the content best or that most students have had difficulty with, this will be called the ‘Reboot Livecode’ and will be hosted by the online teacher. Students from the concurrent batches will be able to elect which livecode they wish to attend on a daily basis.
