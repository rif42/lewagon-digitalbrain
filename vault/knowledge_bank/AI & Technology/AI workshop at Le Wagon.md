# AI workshop at Le Wagon
## 💡 Overview - Solution ideation
*Describe what is the problem and what potential solutions we might have to solve it.*
AI is a powerful tool that can be extremely useful if used properly, or harmful if misused, in the context of learning. This experiment aims to achieve the following objectives:
- Objective 1: Use AI as a learning tool
- Objective 2: Stop students from misusing AI in the context of learning (solving challenges)
- Objective 3: Use AI as a personal growth tool
