### FC & Prerequisites ⚙️​
---
- Prerequisites: fluency with spreadsheets (Excel - [Google Sheet ](https://www.w3schools.com/googlesheets/#gsc.tab=0)- Tables - etc.) and especially [pivot tables](https://zapier.com/blog/google-sheets-pivot-table/) + a first work experience (internship ok).
- No need for programming (but it's a bonus if you already know VBA, SQL or even Python)
- No need for a high level of math (we are looking for a logical mind)

### Interview 🧑‍🏫
---
- Introduce yourself
- *Ask for the background*
- *Why DA?*
- *Do they understand the difference between DA and DS ?*
        - See if DS is more adapted
        - See if DEV is more adapted
        - See if Skill Course DA is more adapted
- Have they used excel/google sheets during their work experience*?*
- Present the program
- Merger Emil / Le Wagon - *if needed*
- Differences between DA - DS
- Level of English (like DS)
- Present the post-training jobs
- Continuation of the process : Quiz / contract / deposit / prepwork
- Career Week
- *Fundings? *
- *Any question ?*
- If needed : History Le Wagon Web then DS
### Quiz / Assessmemt ❓​
---
- Quiz about 2 hours : [<https://cj4n3v071z1.typeform.com/to/TQTtaJ1X#order_id=1111>]
- They will need paper - a pencil - a calculator
- Single choice quiz: logic and spreadsheet questions, KPIs, basic analysis.
- One week to complete, 2 days if Bootcamp approaching
- The most important subjects are in order (they all are): analytical - spreadsheets - data - Kpis
- A good quiz starts at 3/5, with priority given to the subjects listed above
