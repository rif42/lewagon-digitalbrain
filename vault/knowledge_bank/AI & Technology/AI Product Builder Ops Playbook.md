## Pedagogical Content
---
<!-- unhandled: header_4 -->
Zoom per module
- **Module 1 - Build AI agents & Workflows with Low Code** : Build a chatbot with extended capabilities to answer users with the most relevant answer.
- **Module 2 - App deployment : **Build & deploy an app with AI-powered features.
- **Module 3 - AI assisted and vibe coding** : Build a job tracker application using AI coding tools.
- **Module 4 - AI product strategy** : Begin building your final project with proper strategic planning : frame a problem, define KPIs, select a POC-appropriate architecture
- **Module 5 - Final project** : Continue working on their project in the morning and demo the final product in the afternoon
