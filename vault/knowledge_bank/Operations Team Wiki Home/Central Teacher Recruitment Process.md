# Central Teacher Recruitment Process
> [!note] 
> This document provides a clear overview of the **recruitment process** managed by the **Central team** to hire new teachers.
> [!note] 
> Roles & Responsibilities
- **Central Team**
        - Source new candidates
        - Manage the recruitment process from *Sourcing/Application* to *Meet the team* step
        - Manage Kitt onboarding
- **City Managers**
        - *Meet the team *step
        - Manage local onboarding
        - Provide local support
> [!warning] Database view not exported
> [[Recruitment steps]]
> [!note] 
> Share your needs
Direct message on slack to @Pauline Paris when you have :
- a precise need that you can’t staff yourself
- an emergency for staffing
⚠️ The process can* evolve in the coming weeks, according to feedbacks and practical use*
> [!note] 
> Onboarding
- You will be notified by @Pauline Paris once a teacher is **confirmed and ready to onboard**
- Please prepare:
        - Local onboarding
        - Access to city-specific tools/platforms
        - Schedule for shadowing / first classes
- @Pauline Paris will contact you after after 4 weeks of local integration to get feedback
