# Example of the rules in place in Paris
## Basic Rules:
- A student can pass the certification right after his batch, or on the following session.
- If the student fails the certification, he/she will be able to retake it in the next session.
- To pass the certification a batch must be completed at least one week ago (I suggest 2 to limit breakage and let them review) so that the certification does not take place on Career Week.
- Professors / TAs are not allowed to assist students during certification.
- If a student posts a sassy message on the certification Slack channel, he/she will be removed from the channel for the day.
- Students cannot send DMs to the technical and operational referents on the session. Communication is only done on the Slack channel dedicated to the certification.
## Eliminatory faults:
- Forgetting to register.
- Forgetting to submit your link / not submitting your link before the closing time of a test.
- Not attending the exam.
- Behavioural problems
