Now that you are able to navigate thought LW’s CRM let’s talk about the Objects that we do have inside !
The first thing that you need to know it’s that within our CRM all the info about a lead, *called Contact, *are separated in different categories, these categories are called Objects.
And, all of these objects are linked to the Contact, each of them grant different information about him !
