# Embedded form for Skill courses
Click on Create new embedded apply form skill course and fill in the required fields.
There are two ways to create an Embedded Apply Form for Skill courses:
### 1. Letting prospects choose the session they want to apply for
You can pick the program you want people to apply for - or leave it to All , which will leave the choice of programs and sessions to the prospect in the form:

## Adding the form to your landing page
> [!note] 
> Once created, you can copy the **Form URL** at the bottom of the page and use it to embed the form in your Hubspot landing pages.
> [!note] 
> If you need to embed the form in a different locale, copy the F**orm URL** and add the locale after the domain name as if it was a page of the website.
For instance for 🇫🇷
https://www.lewagon.com/**fr/**embedded_forms/97/applies/new
