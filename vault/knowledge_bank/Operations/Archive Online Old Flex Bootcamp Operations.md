The Flex part-time Bootcamp is a **24-week programme**, which requires students to :
- commit about 16 hours of study per week
- It includes **3 hours of scheduled live sessions **
- **& 13 hours of personal study** supported by 1:1 practice, peer-to-peer learning & recorded content.
The Bootcamp has been designed to fit around a full-time job or other personal commitments. It’s perfect for students who want to have more options when it comes to choosing their study schedule.
**Just like our current Part-time format, we’re covering 2 learning units per week, each unit corresponding to a full-day on the full time Bootcamp. **
### Example Module 1: Monday - Wednesday
- Student deadline to watch Lectures + play flashcards at home | Tuesday 6PM
- Once the lecture and flashcards are done, student need to practice and start working on the challenges whenever he/she wants.
- For the buddy system, students get on Gather Town whenever they want to collaborate. It can take place outside Helpdesk hours.
- We provide dedicated TA support during Helpdesk session where students are on Gather Town, can collaborate with classmates and get help using the ticket system.
- Once they’ve done the exercise, they have to come to a mandatory Interactive Live Code. They can pick the slots that work best for them : Tuesday 8PM or Wednesday 6:30PM.
