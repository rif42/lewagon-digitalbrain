# Remote video interview guidelines
### 🤔 **What to expect**
---
- **Format:** Conversational, about 30 minutes
- **Recording:** Remote via Google Meet
**Before the recording:**
- [ ] Confirm your availability
- [ ] Check your technical setup
- [ ] Preparation
**Day of the recording:**
- Check your microphone and headphones.
- Relax! We aim to make this a fun and engaging conversation.
**Post-recording:**
- We’ll handle the editing and production.
- You’ll receive promotional assets (social media visuals, audiograms, etc.) to share with your network.
