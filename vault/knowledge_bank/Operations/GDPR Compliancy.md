# GDPR Compliancy
# About the project
---
### Context & Problem
*mandatory to create the idea** What is the challenge or pain point this project comes to solve?*
> [!note] 
> Our organization faces critical compliance and operational challenges that require immediate attention through a comprehensive GDPR project. We are currently operating with significant compliance gaps that expose us to regulatory risks and impact our marketing effectiveness. Over the years, we've accumulated a high number of unsubscribed contacts due to excessive email volume, but our current system doesn't offer topic-specific subscription options—meaning users who might be interested in sales offers are completely opting out because they don't want nurturing emails.
### The project
*mandatory to move in backlog** What does the project look like? What is the expected outcome?*
> [!note] 
> Implement a comprehensive GDPR compliance system that transforms our current fragmented approach into a unified, compliant, and user-friendly data management framework. The project involves completely overhauling our HubSpot configuration to enable proper data privacy settings, legal basis tracking, and automated consent management. We will redesign our subscription preference center with granular topic-based options (monthly insights, special promotions, event invitations, product updates, educational content), consolidate 5 fragmented subscription properties into a unified system, and implement proper consent tracking workflows.
The project includes creating GDPR-compliant forms with explicit consent checkboxes and multilingual implementation (English, French, German), establishing automated data retention policies with EU-compliant storage, building data subject rights automation (access, rectification, erasure, portability), and developing SMS/WhatsApp compliance frameworks. Additionally, we will implement a re-engagement campaign framework using Legitimate Interest Assessment to reconnect with unsubscribed contacts legally, create comprehensive audit trails for all GDPR-related activities, and establish a compliance monitoring dashboard for ongoing oversight.
