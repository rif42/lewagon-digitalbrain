# Events Playbook
**Content Navigation**
---
### ✋ Before going any further
---
Before jumping into planning, take a step back 🧘. Not every idea needs to become an event.
Use the following questions to test your idea and make sure it aligns with our goals, audience, and resources.
**1. What’s the goal of this event?**
Be clear on what success looks like. Which part of the funnel are you targeting?
- Are you trying to generate new leads (ToFU)?
- Are you nurturing existing leads ( MoFU)?
- Are you aiming to convert hot leads into customers or candidates (BoFU)?
---
**2. Who is this event for?**
Know your audience before you build anything.
- Is the audience clearly defined?
- Does the event target one of our core **bootcamp personas**? [[Product Marketing Wiki|🧃Product Marketing Wiki]]
        - If yes, **which persona** is it?
> ⚠️ If you cannot clearly define the persona this event is targeting, then it is **NOT** a fit for us. Please do not proceed further.
> 
> ✅ If you’ve confidently answered all of the above — awesome! You’re ready to move forward with planning your event. 👏​
### Event Categories
---
There are 4 big event categories that serve the different funnel stages.
- **Tech Talks & Career Talks (ToFU)** - introduce a career or discuss the future of work/tech.
- **Workshops & Webinars (MoFU)** - teach actionable tech skills in a limited amount of time.
- **Admission events (BoFU) **- Refers specifically to the information sessions that assist with curriculum and logistics inquiries for prospects.
- **Demo Days (BoFU)**- showcase the impact of our education through student presentations.
