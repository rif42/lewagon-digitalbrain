# Managing registrants attendance on the day of the on-site event: A step-by-step guide
> [!note] 
> Follow this quick tutorial to easily check attendance at your in-campus event. This method eliminates the need to repeatedly export/import your list, making it a more eco-friendly approach. Plus, it gives you a clearer overview of who attended and who didn’t once the event done, directly in hubspot.
# Step 1 - Duplicate and Rename the Event Registration View
- Open this link: [HubSpot Event Registration View](https://app.hubspot.com/contacts/4419217/objects/2-15745806/views/44367088/list).
- Click the **duplicate** icon on the right side of the page.
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/attachment%3A6ca1338b-c8db-49a2-9bd5-f26f9137049e%3AScreenshot_2025-01-29_at_10.48.42.png?table=block&id=18a8924a-f014-8082-af8a-cc220afc740b&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=830&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
- Rename the view title following this format:**[City] | Registrant List | [Event Type]***(Example: Paris | Registrant List | In-Campus Event)*
# Step 2 - Apply Your Event ID Filter
- When you create an event on AVO, an **Event ID** is automatically generated.
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/attachment%3A96db9b23-efea-4284-a8dd-6e092ed2f403%3AScreenshot_2025-01-29_at_10.51.03.png?table=block&id=18a8924a-f014-80e2-a428-c2b24ad6fc18&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=830&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
- Copy the **Event ID** and paste it into the **Advanced Filter** in HubSpot, especially on the event ID property.
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/attachment%3A40a42ce1-a56d-4642-9650-13f8d7fe8c00%3AScreenshot_2025-01-29_at_10.52.06.png?table=block&id=18a8924a-f014-8093-9085-f4620d550fee&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=930&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
- Close the filter window and save your selection.
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/attachment%3Aad0e2583-824c-4291-85e8-1eb4fb5833a9%3AScreenshot_2025-01-29_at_10.53.16.png?table=block&id=18a8924a-f014-8045-95ba-d324ed225247&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=350&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
✅ **Your registrant list is now ready!**
# Step 3 - Update the attendance for each registrants
- Open your event view in HubSpot.
- Locate the **Attendance** column.
- For each registrant, update their attendance status.*(Use the ****First Name, Last Name, and Email**** columns to help you identify attendees. The list is already sorted alphabetically by Last Name.). **DON’T Forget to save every time !*
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/attachment%3A8a7a2f52-7ff1-4dbb-acfb-440b62c41cb4%3AScreenshot_2025-01-29_at_10.53.57.png?table=block&id=18a8924a-f014-801e-8114-edda1f693a47&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=740&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
