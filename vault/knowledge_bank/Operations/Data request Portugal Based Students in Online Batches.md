## Context & Problem
> [!note] 
> TODO: Explain in great details the context surrounding this issue
<details><summary>**Suggested Checklist**</summary>
</details>
Portugal-based students are eligible for an additional certification document linked to the Portuguese Ministry of Education. Producing it requires collecting extra information at the end of the training. Because we don’t have a centralized view of Portugal-based students in online cohorts, eligibility is tracked manually and often late, making the process more error-prone and operationally heavy, and increasing the likelihood of delayed or missed documents
