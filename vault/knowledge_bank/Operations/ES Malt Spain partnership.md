# [ES] Malt Spain partnership
# About the project
---
### Context & Problem
> [!note] 
> A complementary project within Le Wagon's global partnership with Malt. Our main challenge was to increase visibility and lead generation among **freelancers in Spain**, a key audience for our remote bootcamps. While Malt has a strong local presence, our brand is still underexposed, particularly outside of Barcelona.
### The project
> [!note] 
> Co-organize **three community events with Malt **(Barcelona, Madrid, online) to connect with Spanish freelancers and present Le Wagon's bootcamps as relevant upskilling opportunities. The project combined **events** with **pre and post-communications**, and a **nurturing strategy** leveraging both Malt's and Le Wagon's channels. Expected outcomes: boost in high-quality leads, increased awareness in new cities, and deeper engagement with the freelance ecosystem.
