## 💬 Qualitative analysis
---
### 🔹 Feedback themes all programs combined
❤️ **Most liked commun themes across cities and bootcamps**
- **Teachers and TAs**, describing them as *supportive, knowledgeable, and approachable*.
- The **collaborative and inclusive community**, emphasizing friendships, peer support, and positive atmosphere.
- A strong sense of **personal and professional growth**, with many citing newfound skills and confidence.
❌ **Least liked commun themes across cities and bootcamps **
- Students felt the **schedule was fast-moving and demanding**, leaving limited time for reflection or deeper exploration.
- **Minor curriculum gaps**, with some wishing for more depth in certain technical areas.
- **Occasional technical issues**, such as software setup or platform bugs.
### 🔹 Most Liked** Across All Cities**
**Top Themes Across All Cities:**
- **Teaching Quality & Support** - Teachers, TAs, and staff availability are consistently the #1 appreciated aspect
- **Hands-on Learning & Challenges** - The practical, challenge-based approach is highly valued
- **Course Structure** - The daily routine of lecture → challenges → recap works well
- **Community & Buddy System** - Networking and collaborative learning are significant positives
- **Project Weeks** - Real-world application through projects is highly appreciated
### 🔹 **Least Liked Accross All City**
I identified systemic issues that need attention:
**Critical Problems:**
- **Pace Too Fast/Overwhelming **- Students feel rushed, can't absorb material deeply
- **Outdated/Unclear Challenges** - Content maintenance is a an issue
- **Lecture Issues** - Too long, not interactive, hard to stay focused
- **Remote Teaching on Campus** - Hybrid bootcamp remains a pain point. Students paying for in-person experience get online lectures
- **Specific Week Problems** - AI/LLM week and MLOps week are often mentioned as poorly structured
### Recommendations
- Establish quarterly content review cycle to fix outdated materials
- Think about more "Reboot Days"
- Think about end-of-day recaps (no new content, more interactive, shorter)
- Standardize teacher training and quality
- Fix facilities issues (AC, furniture, lighting)
- Maintain and amplify current strengths (don't change what's working!)
