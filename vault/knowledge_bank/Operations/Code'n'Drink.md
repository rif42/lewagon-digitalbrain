# Code'n'Drink
> [!note] 
> It’s a fun event that mixes, as its name suggests, code and drinking 😉​
### PURPOSE
To create cohesion and bring some animation within a batch.
In Paris, it is been used as the main animation of the Mid-Batch Party, usually organised on the 4th or 5th Friday.
### CONTEXT
The idea comes from @louismarieBonnefont from Batch #100 who wanted to re-create the famous scene in the movie The Social Network where Zuckerberg challenges developers at Harvard before hiring them at Facebook (<http://bit.ly/2KPRW4x>).
The first edition was such a success that we decided to organise it for every batch.
### FORMAT
There should be two separate areas: The classroom and the drinking area.
- The classroom should be reserved for the coding challenges, where each participant take a seat and bring his/her laptop.
- The drinking table, where shots will be served.
This way you to reduce the risk of having liquids spilt over students' laptops.
Depending on the number of participants, you decide how many rounds there will be until the final round with two participants competing for the first place, in front of the whole class !
For each round, you send them the link of a codewars exercice on slack. It's a race, so the quickest to complete the exercice are selected for the next round.
Between each round, they must leave their computers in the classroom and go to the drinking table to take a shot and come back.
The first round usually serves as a training where no one loses and the competition really starts at the second challenge ;)
**Tips**
- Use the first exercice as a training without telling the participants at first.
- Could be fun to plug the computer of the first to finish on the video-projector for the next round to add difficulty and pressure
- You can change computers' place (in the classroom) while students take their shot at the drinking table. It can create confusion when they come back for next round and this way you make sure that they do not copy on each other at every round.
- If there aren't too many rounds, you can offer them a shot before the first round.
Of course, do not force people to drink if they don't want to :)
- You can think of a prize to win
**Pros:**
- Can be very fun and a good way to start the night.
- Can be used as the main animation of the Mid-Batch Party
- It's a unique event they'll never reproduce anywhere else and that they will surely remember ! :)
- Talented coders can get in the spotlight.
- It's usually a great evening where students get together and bond before the pitching night happening the following monday.
- You can ask former students and TA's to join the event and help with the organisation/animation.
**Cons:**
- Requires a little bit of organisation ( preparing shots and shot glasses in advance, asking students to register in advance to make sure there are enough participants)
