# Expats persona- campaign results & activation guide
## 🆙 Strategy:
Leverage the European Job Report to highlight the tech and soft skills most in demand in each country, positioning it as a valuable resource for navigating the local job market. The primary call to action is: Download the report. This campaign is designed to attract expats by addressing two of their biggest pain points: limited access to the job market and language barriers. For this reason, the majority of assets are in English to ensure clarity and accessibility.

## 🎯 Campaign Description
This LinkedIn campaign tested messaging for a new Expats persona across six European markets to identify high-performing audience segments, optimize targeting, and guide localized activation strategies.

## 🇩🇪 Germany
**Campaign results**
- **Spend:** €739.43
- **Impressions:** 18,643
- **Clicks:** 68
- **Conversions:** 1
- **CTR:** 0.37%
- **CPC:** €10.87
- **Conversion rate:** 1.47%
- **Cost per conversion:** €739.43
**Insight**
> Despite a low CTR, Germany yielded a conversion. Targeting may need stronger localization (language, trust signals).
### Retry Strategy
**What to improve:**
- The conversion happened despite a low CTR and high CPC, suggesting good intent but poor engagement.
    **What to test:**
- **Localize landing pages** fully in German with trust-building content (certifications, testimonials, clarity about career outcomes).
- Use **static carousel ads** to explain “how it works” and reduce bounce.
- Partner with **English-speaking expats in Germany** (like Americans in Berlin) for UGC videos addressing job access and reskilling.

## 🇬🇧 UK & 🇳🇱 Netherlands
**Campaign results**
- **Spend (combined):** €1,077.44
- **Impressions:** 43,904
- **Clicks:** 203
- **Conversions:** 0
**Insight**
> Traffic was solid, but the total lack of conversions suggests drop-off post-click. Likely issues: landing page friction, message mismatch, or insufficient trust.
### UK — Retry Strategy
**What to improve:**
- High impressions and clicks but no conversions—drop-off likely on the landing page or due to unclear value.
    **What to test:**
- Emphasize **outcomes** (job titles, hiring partners) in creative assets.
- A/B test **short-form testimonials vs. program explainer videos**.
- Add **exit-intent popups** or “check eligibility” CTAs on landing pages.
### Netherlands — Retry Strategy
**What to improve:**
- Good CTR, zero conversions suggests misalignment in funnel or message.
    **What to test:**
- Create bilingual assets (Dutch + English) to increase relevance.
- Add **video content** explaining visa-friendly training options.
- Collaborate with **UK/US expats in Amsterdam or Rotterdam** on Reels/LinkedIn posts about their upskilling journey.
---
### ✅ Local team activation framework
1. **Use the persona breakdown above** for targeting.
1. **Localize creatives** and test them in Stories, Reels, and feed posts.
1. **Partner with local expat influencers** to generate UGC content.
1. **Run A/B tests** with different program CTAs: "Switch to tech" vs. "Start over smart".
1. **Retarget video viewers** and engagers using LinkedIn or Meta.
