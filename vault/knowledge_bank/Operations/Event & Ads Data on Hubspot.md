### Context & Problem
***Mandatory to create the idea.**** What is the challenge or pain point this project comes to solve?*
> [!note] 
> The marketing team currently lacks **centralized reports on HubSpot** to measure the real impact of Events & Ads campaigns throughout the funnel. Without consolidated visibility on key data points (Leads, Applies, GOs, CVR, Revenue, ROI), it's difficult to assess the business performance of each campaign and make data-driven decisions quickly. This project aims to bridge this gap by creating dedicated HubSpot reports.
