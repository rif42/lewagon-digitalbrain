# CRM 2026 Strategy & Roadmap
### **Strategic Context for 2026**
Our CRM plan is built on the foundation of understanding that modern education marketing requires a multi-touch, multi-channel approach. With the competitive landscape of coding bootcamps intensifying and student expectations evolving, Le Wagon's CRM strategy focuses on three core pillars:
1. **Intelligent Lead Management**: Leveraging AI, prediction and automation to qualify, priorise, and nurture leads more effectively
1. **Personalized lead Journey**: Creating tailored experiences based on persona, intent, and engagement level
1. **Data-Driven Optimization**: Using comprehensive metrics and analytics to continuously improve conversion rates and student outcomes
2026 - two focus:
- More focus on persona and less on product
- Lifecycle journey - Redesign Lead Lifecycle Management System
### **Team Structure & Expertise**
The CRM & Ops Squad consists of 2 specialized professionals who works on
- **Strategic Leadership**: Overall CRM strategy and cross-team collaboration
- **Campaign Management**: Global campaign execution and regional coordination

- **Phase 2: The Customer Lifecycle Journey**
        - objective: Paint the ideal customer journey
        - **Stage 1: Lead Capture/Qualification**
                    - **Objective**: to define
                    - **Touchpoints:** Website (blog, free workshops), social media (LinkedIn, Instagram), events (online & offline), paid ads etc.
                    - **CRM Strategy & Campaigns:**
                                    - Qualification
                                    - **Lifecycle stage**
                                    - Persona
                                    - **Lead Magnets:** Offer high-value content (e.g., "First Steps in Coding Guide," "Tech Career Guide") in exchange for an email.
                                    - **Workshop Nurturing:** Automated email sequence for workshop registrants: reminders, post-event slides, and a call-to-action to apply or book a call.
                                    - **Lead Scoring:** Automatically score leads based on actions (e.g., visited pricing page = +10, attended webinar = +20). High-scoring leads get prioritized by admissions staff.
                                    - etc.
        - **Stage 2: Consideration & Nurturing (based on lifecycle stage, or pain/persona)**
                    - **Objective:** to define
                    - **Touchpoints:** Email nurturing, webinars with alumni, student testimonials, 1-on-1 calls with admissions advisors etc.
                    - How to work with Sales and admissions _> connection with Revops strategy
                    - **CRM Strategy & Campaigns:**
                                    - **Persona-Based Drip Campaigns:** Send targeted email series based on the lead's persona.
                                                        - *Career Changers* get alumni success stories and job placement stats.
                                                        - *Entrepreneurs* get info on building products and the alumni startup network.
                                    - **"Why Le Wagon?" Series:** A 5-part email series covering the curriculum, the global community, career services, and financing options.
                                    - **Application Deadline Countdown:** Automated reminders as the application deadline for a specific bootcamp batch approaches.
                                    - etc.
        - **Stage 3: Conversion**
                    - **Objective:** to define
                    - **Touchpoints:** campaign push etc., Application form, financing options page, future products etc.
                    - **CRM Strategy & Campaigns:**
                                    - **Application Abandonment**
                                    - Blockers to treat
        - **Stage 4: Advocacy & Loyalty**
                    - **Objective:** to define : exTurn happy alumni into a powerful growth engine.
                    - **Touchpoints:** Referral programs, review platforms (Course Report, SwitchUp), social media.
                    - **CRM Strategy & Campaigns:**
                                    - **Automated Referral Program:** After an alum updates their status to "Hired," trigger an email inviting them to the referral program. The CRM should track referred leads back to the source.
                                    - **Testimonial Request:** 3 months after graduation, send a personalized request for a review on a key platform.
                                    - **Guest Speaker/Mentor Program:** Invite experienced alumni to speak at workshops or mentor current students, tracked and managed via the CRM.
