## Context & Problem
Events are an important part of the student experience:
- Community building
        -
- Career inspiration
        -
- Social moments
        -
In Paris:
- There are 3 campuses
        -
- Most events take place at Gaudelet
        -
### Problem for students and teachers
- Today:
        - Students cannot indicate if they will attend an event or not.
                    -
        - Teachers also don’t have a way to show their participation.
                    -
- Because of this:
        - Students don’t feel engaged or committed.
                    -
        - Teachers don’t know if students are interested.
                    -
        - Many students decide at the last minute or don’t come.
                    -
### Painful operational process
- Before an event:
        - Teams don’t know how many people to expect.
                    -
        - Attendance is guessed or asked informally.
                    -
- During the event:
        - Sometimes the room is too big or too small.
                    -
        - Some events feel empty, even if they are valuable.
                    -
- After the event:
        - It’s hard to understand if the event was really successful.
                    -
### Missing information
There is no clear visibility on:
- Who plans to attend
        -
- How many students are interested
        -
- Whether teachers will be present
        -
This lack of visibility makes events harder to manage and less engaging for everyone.
