# Kitt cannot accommodate students and teachers who don't go by their legal name
## Summary
Kitt currently uses a single name field for all purposes, making it impossible to distinguish a person's legal name from the name they actually go by, creating both compliance issues and situations where people cannot be addressed as they wish.
## Business Impact
- **Time lost**: PMs manually update names back and forth between legal and preferred versions throughout a batch. One PM made **6+ name changes** for a single student in one batch.
        -
- **Compliance risk**: Official documents (certifications, diplomas, certificates of completion, teacher purchase orders) must legally match the person's government-issued ID. Any mismatch creates a regulatory risk.
        -
- **Inclusion impact**: Being called by the right name is a fundamental part of feeling seen and respected. Forcing a legal name as the only name on the platform can inadvertently expose sensitive personal information (e.g. gender transition, identity not yet publicly assumed) to peers and teachers.

## Context & Problem
A student going through a gender transition was registered on Kitt under their preferred name **Blue**, but their legal ID still reads **Sarah (ATS: **<https://kitt.lewagon.com/applications/131411>). Their certification convocation was sent under the wrong name. The PM had to repeatedly update the name field throughout the batch, creating confusion and privacy risks for the student.
A teacher's legal name is **Dareios** but goes by **Dareos** daily. Purchase orders were issued incorrectly due to the lack of distinction in the system.
More broadly, many students and teachers, especially from non-Western countries, never use their legal name in daily life. Currently, there is no way to honour that without creating issues on official documents.
