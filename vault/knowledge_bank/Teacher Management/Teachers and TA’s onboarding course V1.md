# Teachers and TA’s onboarding course V1
## 💡 Overview - Solution ideation
*Describe what is the problem and what potential solutions we might have to solve it.*
---
<!-- unhandled: ai_block -->
The Teachers and TA's onboarding course aims to create a Learn course for global batch managers and program managers to invite new teachers and assign courses based on their roles. The course will cover various topics and follow a structured format with slides, videos, tools and resources, and an assessment quiz. The course will be piloted in Berlin and London, and success will be measured by the number of teachers and TA's trained, certificates generated, statistics on teacher performance, and increase in government grants. If successful, the course will be exposed to the global program management community for testing in Q3.
We will create a Learn course for global batch managers and program managers. They will use this course to invite new teachers and assign courses based on their roles. The courses will cover various topics and follow a structured format.
- Onboard 100% of all new TA’s and teachers
- Standardise teaching practices
- Certify our teachers
- Support local teams and remove workload

*How will the experiment look like?*

- Slides + Video
- Tools and resources
- Assessment Quiz
