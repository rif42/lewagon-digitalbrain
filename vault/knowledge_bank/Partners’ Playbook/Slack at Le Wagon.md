# Slack at Le Wagon
> [!note] 
> Slack is our main communication and collaboration tool at Le Wagon. **S L A C K** is actually an acronym → **S**earchable** L**og** **of **A**ll **C**ommunication and **K**nowledge, and all messages ever sent since the beginning of Le Wagon can be found within Slack — don’t underestimate the power of searching through Slack when you need information! 🕵️
Here’s some info on how we use Slack, including Best Practices, Shortcuts and a [[Slack at Le Wagon|channels list]] to help you choose the best place for your message or to search for information.
# **General best practices**
- [ ] Everyone should have their name, photo, job title and location on their profile
- [ ] Use short messages 👊 Messages should be no longer than 3 or 4 lines, unless you are making a big announcement. There should only be one topic per message. So be crystal clear on what you want to say!
- [ ] Mention people 📣 You should always tag @name someone when mentioning them, whether in group channels or individual messages — this way there is no room for misinterpreting a message or misspelling names.
- [ ] Respond in a thread, always 📬​
- [ ] We use the @channel command very sparingly in public channels as we have colleagues working all over the globe in different time zones. You can use @here to ping only people who are online.
- [ ] #channels are open for anyone to join and searchable; 🔒channels are invite-only and private to those in the channel. This means you shouldn’t link to messages sent in 🔒 as not everyone will be able to view the message.
- [ ] We rarely send emails internally 🙅‍♀️; instead select the best [[Slack at Le Wagon|Slack channel]] from the list below to share your information or ask your questions.

# Emoji reaction meanings
Reacting to messages is fun! You can hype up someone’s news or achievement ✨ and reacting to messages sent in channels and direct messages can save time on sending a reply with emojis that carry a specific meaning 👇​
> [!note] 
> *Action items
*🕵️ I’m looking into the problem/request, and I will solve/do it. (So others don’t start working on it, too!)
👀 I’m also interested in what’s happening here, but I’m not working on it.
⏳ I’m still looking at it, it takes a bit of time, but be patient and I’ll do it.
✅ Problem / request is solved.
❌ No, this is not a good idea, and I want others in the channel to know it without reading a long thread.
> [!note] 
> *Importance level
*ℹ️ This is some information, no need to discuss anything, but you may react with questions in threads.
⚠️ This is important information for everyone in the channel
❓This is a question which does not need an urgent response
🔥 This is a question which requires an urgent response
💡 This is an idea I want to share, and I can pair it with 🧠 to invite others to brainstorm with me in a thread.
> [!note] 
> *Responses
*👍 / 👎 I (dis)agree with what has been said
🙏 “Thank you!” or “Please could you...” ?
🚦(green light) Approved, let’s go!
