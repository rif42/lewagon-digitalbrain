*Mission explanation:*
- [ ] **Transform:** Emphasizes the significant impact we aim to have.
- [ ] **Individuals and organizations:** Highlights our dual focus on personal and professional development.
- [ ] **Accessible:** Stresses the inclusivity and reach of our educational offerings.
- [ ] **Cutting-edge tech education: **Focuses on the advanced and relevant nature of our training.
---
*Vision explanation:*
- [ ] **Turn:** Emphasizes action and proactivity. We lead change, not just adapt.
- [ ] **Tech Transformations:** Refers to ongoing and future tech advancements reshaping industries and societies. We must stay at the forefront.
- [ ] **Into Opportunities: **Focuses on leveraging tech changes for new possibilities and advantages. Every shift is a chance for growth, innovation and career development.
