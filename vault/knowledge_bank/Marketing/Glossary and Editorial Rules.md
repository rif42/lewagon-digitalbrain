# Glossary and Editorial Rules
> [!note] 
> These rules apply to English. In other languages, it is possible to modify these rules according to the practices and spelling rules of the language.
## 1. Le Wagon
When we talk about Le Wagon, we describe ourselves as:
✅ **a tech training company **
✅** a tech education company **
✅** a training company **
**Let’s move away from the school terminology** which is confusing with longer training (at least 1 year) :
❌ Tech school
❌ Coding school
**You can check the official description of Le Wagon here: **[[Le Wagon Description|ℹ️Le Wagon Description]]** **
## 2. Our offer
---
1. **Bootcamp**
We offer **intensive tech training** called **bootcamps. **
✅ Unless the word is at the beginning of a sentence, **we do not capitalize it**
✅ A bootcamp it’s an **immersive**, **intensive** tech training
✅ We can use synonymous like **courses**, **training**
1. **Format**
We offer **full-time** and **part-time** learning formats.
✅ Full-time format
✅ Part-time format
❌ Flex part-time: we can explain that our part-time format is flexible, made for busy learners BUT we don’t talk about a Flex part-time format.
❌ Full-Time > we do not capitalize the T
❌ Part-Time > we do not capitalize the T
## 3. Program names / Program skills
---
We always write the program name or the program skill in uppercase.
**Example: **
- Join our **Data Analytics bootcamp** in 2 months
- Master **Data Science & AI** with Le Wagon
- Our **Web Development**** course** is made for you if…
- Want to learn **Data Engineering**?
- We teach web and **Data courses **
❌ Web Development: without a “-”
❌ A.I > we don’t add a . on AI
## 4. Job titles
---
We always write the job titles in uppercase.
**Example**:
- Land a job as a **Data Analyst**
- Become a **Data Scientist**
- Check out the salary of a **Junior Software Engineer **
- Master the skills of a **Data Engineer**
But we don’t capitalize team names:
- The career services team
- The admission and sales team

## 6. Course content
---
1. **Curriculum**
The curriculum outlines the course content and the learning outcomes:
- The skills the students will learn
- The tools they will use
- The projects they will be working on
1. **Syllabus**
The syllabus is a more detailed document with the exact day-by-day description of the course.
Additionally to this detail, our syllabus contains also:
- Le Wagon methodology
- The format of the course
- The curriculum
- The program details per module
- Le Wagon services: career services, financing options..
1. **Lectures:**
The lectures at Le Wagon are formal presentations conducted by instructors to impart knowledge on specific topics.
1. **Units**
Our courses are organized into units to facilitate structured learning, allowing participants to focus on distinct aspects of the curriculum. For the full-time format, a unit corresponds to one day of teaching.
1. **Module:**
A module is a group of units i.e. the machine learning module has 10 units inside of it.
1. **Challenges:**
Le Wagon’s methodology focuses on hands-on projects. That’s why students are invited to practice a lot through challenges. Challenges provide hands-on experience, reinforcing concepts learned during the lectures
1. **Live code:**
Live code is a session in which teachers and students review the challenges and key concepts of the day / of the week.
In external communication, we can replace *live code* with *live session* to be more comprehensive.
1. **Flashcard:**
Flashcards are exercises to be completed individually to ensure that skills have been acquired.
1. **Final project**
The project phase or final project is the ultimate experience of the course. Students have to invent, design, code, and deploy an original project in teams of 3 or 4 people.
1. **Demo Day:**
Demo Day is a celebration of the skills acquired, allowing participants to present their achievements to peers, mentors, and potential employers. During demo day, students present their final project in front of an audience.
