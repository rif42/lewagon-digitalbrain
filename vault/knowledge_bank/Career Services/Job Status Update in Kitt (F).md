# Job Status Update in Kitt (F)
### This page is shared with our franchises.
---
> [!note] 
> It's natural to feel confused about the various tools we use to track career progress of our Alumni. Let's clarify the purpose and importance of **the job status update in Kitt.**
## What is the Job Status update in Kitt?
---
Located in the **Alumni tab**, this feature helps us:
1. Calculate **job placement rates** accurately
1. Gather key information about **hiring partners**
1. Focus on alumni who haven't **yet secured employment**
1. Identify potential alumni for **testimonials**

## Why use this when we have the Outcomes Form?
---
> [!note] 
> Both the job status update and the outcomes form apply **to all alumni**, regardless of their participation in career services.
While the outcomes form (sent 6 months post-bootcamp) is valuable, the job status update serves additional purposes:
1. Allows for timely calculation of **3-month job placement rates**
1. **Captures employment changes** that occur soon **after** completing the outcomes form
