# Careers Guide for Franchises & Partners
## Recorded training 📹​
> [!video] 
> [!note] 
> Central responsibilities
- **Automated workflows **
    Such as automatic sending of career forms, adding programs’ participants to dedicated slack channels or deactivating inactive profiles on Huntr
- **Live sessions**
Organising [live sessions](https://calendly.com/lewagon-livesessions), both mandatory ones and additional ones
- **Content**
    Maintaining and updating Programs content on Learn
        - [Job Seeker](https://learn.lewagon.com/c/8758/b/23701)
        - [Freelancer](https://learn.lewagon.com/c/8760/b/25512)
        - [Founder](https://learn.lewagon.com/c/8759/b/22917)
- **Animating Slack channels **
    Managing slack channels such as
    #job-posts
    #career-insight
    #[city]-alumni-jobs
    #freelancers
    #founders
    #career-job-seeker-program
    #career-founder-program
    #career-freelancer-program
- **Data**
    Collecting and summarising data about programs use and outcomes and making them available to franchises & partners (upon request)
---
> [!note] 
> Franchise & Partner responsibilities
- **Communication with students**
    Communication with students about programs and encouraging them to actively participate in them
- **Dedicated 1:1 support**
    Providing a dedicated advisor for students who will be assigned to them on Huntr and will perform 1:1 coaching
- **Adapting to local needs**
    Organising live sessions if due to timezone or language differences, central [live sessions](https://calendly.com/lewagon-livesessions) do not satisfy local needs.
    Adding local jobs to Job Portal on Huntr.
- **Hiring Partners**
    Building and maintaining a hiring partners network for local needs by inviting partners to join Huntr and organising events with them
- (optional) writing a yearly outcomes report

### Sales pitch
Below you can find key benefits for our career services that you can mention on a call with prospective students and leaflet you can share with them for more details

- **Personalised 1:1 Career Support: **Every student is paired with a dedicated Career Advisor who offers tailored support, feedback, and guidance throughout and beyond the bootcamp.
- **Early and Ongoing Access: **Students gain access to career services from Week 1, and can access continuing support well after Demo Day.
- **Self-Paced Job Seeker Program: **Structured but flexible online content which covers key job strategy topics such as networking, interview skills and application tips.
- **Weekly live workshops: **Interactive sessions on high-impact topics such as job hunting strategies, CV writing, portfolio building, mock interviews, and more—available during and after the bootcamp and created for Le Wagoners.
- **Q&As, Panels & Industry Speakers: **Regular events with alumni and tech professionals give real-world insights.
- **Job Search Tools: Huntr Access: **Students get access to Huntr, a job tracking tool that helps manage applications.
- **Alumni Network & Community Access: **Join a global community of 29,000+ alumni with active Slack channels.
- **Practical, Outcome-Focused Support: **All support is geared towards getting hired

### Hiring Partnerships
Local franchise & Partner teams are responsible for building and maintaining their local partners networks.
There are now two key resources:
👉 An [[Employer Partnerships (F)|internal overview]]** **- breakdown of how we engage with employer partners, including info on Huntr, the services we can offer employers & who to speak to in the careers team if you are speaking with an employer.
👉 An [[Partnering with Le Wagon|external Notion page]]** **-** **a shareable resource for prospective employer partners, highlighting how they can connect with graduates and what support we can offer. This directs them to our web form.
If a company would like to access students profile on Huntr, they should register via link: <https://huntr.co/lewagon/employer-signup>

### Career Policy
To align expectations between students and Le Wagon, we created [[Career Services Policy|🤝🏽Career Services Policy]]
