# Getting started with Career Services
> [!note] 
> If you have a question that was not answered by this FAQ, write us in #career-services-help Slack and one of our associates will promptly answer you.
### I have submitted the Career Form - what do I do next?
**Job Seeker Program 💼**​
- We have sent you an **email with access content on Learn**
- We have sent you an **email with Huntr activation link**
Next steps:
1. Go to our Learn platform and watch introduction video and copy [your Workbook](https://learn.lewagon.com/c/8758/b/23701).
1. Based on instructions in your Workbook, complete 5 mandatory assignments.
1. Send a link to your workbook in Notion to your Career Advisor through Slack,
1. You will be then invited to Live Workshops, which we run every week.
1. Once you attended 4 mandatory Live Workshops you will get a link to book 1:1 with your career advisor and you will be visible to our hiring partners in Talent Pool.
### Video
**Freelancer 🏄 **and/or** Founder 🚀 Program**
- We have sent you an **email with access content on Learn**
- You have been added to a **#career-freelance-program and #career-founder-program channel **with info about **live events**
Next steps: You have now access to all the content, perks, and events. You can use them forever.
If you have questions, please write a message on #**career-services-help **channel.
