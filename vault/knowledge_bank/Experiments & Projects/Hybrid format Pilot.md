# Hybrid format Pilot
## **💡 Overview - Solution Ideation**
Le Wagon is facing challenges in staffing teachers and teaching assistants due to an increasing number of campuses with small batches. These small batches result in a low pedagogical margin, and some batches are too small, necessitating cancellation. This issue impacts the operational efficiency and potential growth of our campuses.
To address this issue, the product team proposes a hybrid approach where students can participate in online batches from a campus setting. This model leverages the strength and distinctiveness of having physical campuses while integrating the flexibility and scalability of online education.
- **Objective 1:** Make small batches more profitable by increasing the pedagogical margin.
- **Objective 2:** Reduce the number of batches that are closing due to lack of students.
- **Objective 3:** Allow cities to open all programs that they would not open normally.
