# **Next Steps: from Sales Hub to Sales Playbook**
To turn this information hub into a sales playbook, we will focus on making it more actionable.
Here are the key priorities:
**1. Add a "How-To" section for sales conversations**
The Hub tells us *what* to say; a playbook shows us *how*. We will create a new section that includes:
- **Essential Discovery Questions:** A list of the top 5-7 questions to ask each persona (career-changers and upskillers) to uncover their true motivations and fears.
- **Sample Email Templates:** Three ready-to-use email templates for the most common follow-ups, such as a post-call summary or a response to the price objection.
**2. Build the "Story Library" to make social proof easy**
To turn "telling a story" from a suggestion into a tool, we will create a simple Story Library. This will be a database of at least 10 diverse alumni success stories, each categorized by:
- Their previous career (e.g., "Marketing," "Chef")
- Their initial hesitation (e.g., "Worried about cost," "No tech background")
- Their successful outcome (e.g., "Hired in 2 months," "Built their own startup")
**3. Create competitive battlecards to define our advantage**
We will inevitably be asked, "How are you different from X?" We will create simple, one-page "battlecards" for our main competitors. Each will honestly summarize:
- Their key strengths and weaknesses.
- The top 3 reasons Le Wagon is the better choice for our specific student profiles, with clear proof points.
**4. Activate the playbook with role-playing**
A playbook is only effective if the we feel confident using it. We should dedicate time in the sales meetings to role-play common scenarios. Starting with the top 3 objections and practice handling them with the A.A.A. framework until it feels natural, confident, and effective.
