# How to Edit Batch Card Information
---
From now on, the information displayed on our website’s bootcamp cards can be handled directly through Kitt.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F4b50dcf6-2ae3-47be-a080-332f53fc872e%2FScreenshot_2022-09-16_at_6.01.01_PM.png?table=block&id=b7b98a8a-819d-462b-aa0a-16e124606d9f&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
In order to edit this information, you can head to your upcoming batch’s settings, and then the WWW section.
From there, you can set the Apply status. The card will appear on the website starting the Open for registration status, meaning Unlisted will remove it from the website.
You can also set start and end dates that are different from the **actual** start dates of the batch (first and last day of class) to include events such as prep week, career week, or holiday breaks.
