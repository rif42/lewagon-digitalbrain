# Pipeline Review - Checklist Best Practices
> [!note] 
> A key best practice that can save you HOURS in the week, is to save a 30min. spot in your calendar to review the pipeline and the process of each applicant ⌛
You'll make sure that everyone is accounted for, that you didn't forget any pending emails or actions, and that all next steps per lead are clear.
### ⚙️ Structure
---
In this Notion doc, you'll have To-dos for each section of the pipeline - to do for each applicant - that will become second nature to you after the first 2 or 3 reviews you do.
To keep this all handy, you also have a checklist at the end of each section on how your pipeline should look like after you review it 😎​
*Disclaimer: *Whenever you read an action such as "call" or "email" you can always use the KITT reminder function to set this to another date, keeping your review faster but maintaining the peace of mind that you haven't forgotten any lead 💆‍♀️​
> [!note] 
> We need to be hands-on and proactive with our leads, especially at the beginning of the quarter if you have the time → CALL your applicants to get instant feedback.
### 📨 Inbox
---
**TO DOs**
- **For "Just-in" Leads → **Send first contact reminder emails
- **For "Old "Leads (>24h ago) → **🥇 **Call **to insist to book an interview / ask if they have questions
        - Or... 🥈 send second contact reminder email / Whatsapp
**Your pipe should look like this after To-Dos**
- [ ] Leads applied <24h/short-time ago and have received a first contact email
- [ ] or... Leads applied >24h/long-time ago and you have contacted them through another method (call/whatsapp) to book interview
**You should not have people on first contact for more than 3 weeks.**

### 🤙 Interview
---
**TO DO**
- For leads that have canceled interviews → **Call **to know the reason why, document, and try to have them book another slot
**CHECKLIST** after To-Dos
- [ ] Leads have interviews booked
- [ ] Or... Canceled interviews leads have been called to know the reason why.

### 💻 Assessment
---
**TO DO WEB/DATA**
- For leads studying for the assessment/ within deadline (🟢) → Send email asking how they are progressing and if they have any questions
- For leads yet to deliver test + and it's past deadline (🟡) → 🥇 Call / 🥈 Email to know if all is well and if they have questions
***Assessment:***
- If the assessment is still to be corrected → Correct and send feedback asap
**CHECKLIST** after To-Dos
- [ ] Leads are studying for assessment within deadline (🟢) and have been contacted to follow-up on their track and maintain engagement
**Extra points**: Instead of sending just email feedback, Call the prospect to share the good news and ask if there is any doubt regarding the next steps

### 🤝 Contract
---
**TO DO**
- For leads that haven't signed the contract in 4 days → Call to know why, and to maintain availability for questions. In this call, make sure you set a new deadline for when they sign the contract
**CHECKLIST** after To-Dos
- [ ] Leads have received the contract <4 days ago
- [ ] Leads have received the contract >4 days ago, have been contacted, and a new date was set for the contract deadline

---
**TO DO**
- Send Welcome Guides; Events News or any other idea you think is worth it for your GOs to maintain updated!
**CHECKLIST** after To-Dos
- [ ] Leads have had a touchpoint after signing the contract in the past 15 days
