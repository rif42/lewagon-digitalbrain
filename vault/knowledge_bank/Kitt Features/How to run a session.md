# Onboarding
> [!note] 
> This section concern only [[How to create an exam session|sequential exam sessions]].
> [!note] 
> Onboarding should be done **before the certification exam**.
We suggest to do it one week before the beginning of the exam.
In any case, the onboarding **should be closed 48h before the starts of the exam**.
To onboard candidates to a session, copy/paste the onboarding link and share it to the students through Slack.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fa2888614-7a7a-4068-be81-f3af836b50cf%2FScreenshot_2023-03-28_at_15.27.22.png?table=block&id=c2feaee0-2aff-4fd8-b1b8-5d457a6a1f5f&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
You will see appear candidates by clicking on the Candidates tab. You will have to accept each candidate by clicking on the Accept button (you can also accept all pending candidates thanks to the Batch actions dropdown).
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F6c99481b-216f-4dae-9763-d7a0ca356f64%2FScreenshot_2023-03-28_at_15.36.32.png?table=block&id=5901cf70-daf5-48cc-8b0e-aba663a1a6de&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
> [!note] 
> For RNCP session, you should accept candidates who used the Sign in with Kitt function.
You can use [this blazer request](https://assess.lewagon.com/blazer/queries/17-candidate-without-github_nickame-per-exam_session) to ensure that every candidates sign in with Kitt.
If one of student didn’t register using the Kitt sign in, just ask them to sign out and then Sign in with Kitt before accepting them.
## Close the onboarding
When all the candidates are onboarded, close the registrations step by clicking on the Close registration button on the top right of your screen.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fdc499f50-4ff1-4832-a86b-cab94f8c863d%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_13.10.26.png?table=block&id=78ffd5a2-217b-4ed0-a9a8-e134697c959a&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
You will need to confirm that everyone has been properly onboarded.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F29ed24cb-14d1-4a94-8e0f-172de0284e4a%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_14.22.38.png?table=block&id=683a895e-8ea8-4e93-a728-2322632819d0&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
# Before the session
- Before the certification (ideally during the bootcamp), run a presentation to inform students about what to expect from the Le Wagon Certification and help them figure out if they need to take the exam or not 👇​

- Create a dedicated slack channel to communicate with the candidates during the exam session.
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fc307135c-c78e-4700-bc0b-112c8cb3efbd%2FScreenshot_2022-01-27_at_18.55.26.png?table=block&id=1817452a-35ad-42a1-b4ea-4ee22ceb0e83&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1330&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
- Add all the candidates manually in this channel.
- Communicate about the setup requirement for the exam session.
    > [!note] 
    > For RNCP exam session, you can share these documentations:
    - [Web dev](https://app.notion.com/p/d0974b35d1cc4b5bba61c6234f9b96d6)
    - [Data](https://github.com/lewagon/data-setup#keep-your-setup-current-after-the-bootcamp)
- We recommend to staff a teacher familiar with the exam, to assist candidates in case of setup problems, and invite her/him to the Slack channel
- Make sure candidates are aware of the certification rules.
        [[Example of the rules in place in Paris]]
- The day before the first challenge: post a message on the certification channel to announce the dispatch of students for the identity verification on Zoom.
        - The identity check must be completed before 9:00 a.m. (start time of the tests)
        - The day before the certification, students should be divided into several Zooms (as needed) and given the assignment, so that they know what to do the next day.
        - In the Zoom, a different room should be created in order to proceed with the identity verification
        - Invite the students one by one into the room so that you can check their identity.
        - Finally, once the student's identity has been verified, ask them to leave the Zoom
            ![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F126b281b-e2d5-4f7b-9772-66b5bd3f408c%2FScreenshot_2022-01-27_at_18.56.50.png?table=block&id=7c2c796c-6956-4e59-b009-541a484e2838&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1260&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
# RNCP exam session schedule
**Day 1:**
- **8:45 a.m.: **Identity verification via Zoom
- **9:00 a.m. :** Launch of the first test
- **6:00 p.m.: **Close the day of certification
- **6:15 p.m.: **Post a message on the certification channel to announce the dispatch of students to the identity verification Zoom
**Day 2:**
- **8:45 a.m.: **Identity verification via Zoom
- **9:00 a.m. :** Launch of the second test
- **6:00 p.m. : **Close the certification
**Notes**:
- If for some reason delay happened, adjust start and end to fit official duration of the challenge.

# Close a challenge
After starting a challenge, the top right button of your screen change in order to le you close the ongoing challenge.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F537828bd-ae41-4013-b0c1-dd41bdfdba24%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_14.29.20.png?table=block&id=fb641199-1884-489f-a7de-b4dff71a683e&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Check the session dashboard to see if all the candidates have sent their deliverables.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F7ab4bd61-b91d-4870-a00a-6f87040ac5e5%2FScreenshot_2022-01-27_at_17.52.03.png?table=block&id=3d6d463b-fb18-4a9a-9f99-60ab9ad04e39&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
You can send them a message to warn them before closing the challenge:
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fe740adbc-42ba-449d-b475-841a55f61ccb%2Fimage_(5).png?table=block&id=1d100b99-c2bb-44f9-9c6c-30503e851c94&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
When everything seems alright, close the challenge and confirm (by clicking on All good).
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F811c459b-acd6-4728-a141-52d105d10f45%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_15.00.04.png?table=block&id=968390e2-5950-4420-9f3a-0850c2327580&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
> [!note] 
> After closing a challenge, candidates will not be able to submit anything.
After closing a challenge, the top right button of your screen will allow you start the next challenge (if there is one) or to close the assessment step (which is automatically opened after closing the last challenge). Rinse and repeat until the last challenge 🚀​
