# Find my course
## **Overview**
The **Find my course** form uses a branching system to guide users through a series of questions, leading them to the most relevant course based on their responses.
When a user submits the form:
1. Typeform sends the data to HubSpot via its built-in integration (essentially submitting it to a HubSpot form).
1. A dedicated HubSpot workflow maps the user’s answers to the corresponding records in the CRM.
The form is embedded in a HubSpot landing page, which is linked in the website’s navigation bar.
