# Batch Manager role
> [!note] 
> There is now a new role on Kitt called *Batch Manager*. A Batch Manager is responsible for running the batch and making sure that students and teachers get the Le Wagon experience. Often times, this will be a freelancer who also teaches on the batch.
With this new feature, the Batch Manager will get increased access to Kitt and to all the Slack messages required to maintain an overview of the batch.
### Slack channel and messages
Once a slug is assigned to a batch, a new Slack channel will be created on the alumni workspace called batch-<slug>-managers. All existing batch managers will be automatically added to this channel.
**Messages**
The following Slack messages will be sent to this channel:
- templates for messages to be sent to the batch
- absence form notifications
- daily lateness and absence checkins
- notification about when a teacher starts the Zoom call
- TA applications (sent both to this channel and the city operations channel in the team workspace)

### Adding **someone as a Batch Manager**
A City Manager can navigate to the calendar settings of a batch and create one or several batch managers on the Batch Managers page. The dropdown shows all the teachers who have already been added to the calendar of the batch and all the city managers of the city of the batch.
> [!note] 
> Everyone who is involved in the management of a batch (including the City Manager, Events and Community Manager, etc) should be added as a batch manager.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F81837676-54fa-44cb-a4a7-7b655aaa8d18%2FScreenshot_2021-11-26_at_12.12.10.png?table=block&id=017c622e-efb3-4b17-a5e3-097cb3870e6e&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
If a batch manager gets added *after* the batch-<slug>-managers channel has been created, they will still be automatically invited to the channel.
### **Additional access on Kitt**
A batch manager now has increased permissions on Kitt.
**Calendar**
A batch manager has access to add teachers to the calendar and staff them. They can also publish the calendar and invite teachers to the batch-<slug>-teachers channel.
On top of that, batch managers can request an extra TA slot in the calendar. The request needs to be reviewed by a City Manager, who gets to either accept or decline it. Batch Managers won’t be able to see the teacher’s rates hen requesting an extra TA slot (cf. [No access](https://app.notion.com/p/7c7f2bda06b5497996671346db87bf97?pvs=24#7c7f2bda06b5497996671346db87bf97))
> [!video] 
**Additional access**
- They can trigger the flashcards reminders
- They can create the Slack channels for the project teams
- They have access to the Teacher Onboarding section where all the TA applications come in and they can manage them (accept or reject them, manage the wishlist and onboard a new teacher)
- They can also access the other Human Resources sections on Kitt: See all teachers from previous batches in that city, find a teacher & MiniJobber contracts
- They have access to the quiz sessions
- They can resend the GitHub invite in case one of the student didn't receive it or change their email 👇​
