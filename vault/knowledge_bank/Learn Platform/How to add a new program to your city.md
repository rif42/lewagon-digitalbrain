# How to add a new program to your city
---
## Context
A new program has been released and you want to launch it in your city and sell it on www.
---
## **How-to**
### 1. Create a local course
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Ff5844672-5402-473e-a894-6dcb9b90fda7%2FUntitled.png?table=block&id=02c75b3f-ec10-42b5-9022-30fa01bb7bc6&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
[Le Wagon - External Log Inhttps://www.lewagon.com/v4/admin/resources/local_courses/new](https://www.lewagon.com/v4/admin/resources/local_courses/new)
In the admin, create a **new local course, **linking your **city** and the **program** you are launching.
> [!note] 
> **Do not tick ****Published** just yet, you need to make sure your page looks great 💅 before publishing it.
Keep the page unpublished and **add header pictures** for the page (see above for Amsterdam example).
> [!note] 
> Once created, head to the bottom of the page and **create a course format ****for each of the formats**** your city offers:**
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fa62e3198-cc3c-48ba-b3c8-aca3f5be0259%2FUntitled_(1).png?table=block&id=ab50b813-85bf-480b-bffa-110b60458191&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F25db1ab8-d042-47d1-9594-3f0a8897eb64%2FUntitled.png?table=block&id=c276d677-8bea-478c-9498-36a757dd1ef9&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
This will ensure you can see the Program unit info item and program schedule items related to that program and course format:
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fc1eb4e2b-6ea9-4f05-b046-48551d1bb0db%2FUntitled.png?table=block&id=bdfc0bbd-ebd4-43d5-b966-b590984c2e7d&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1440&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)

### 2. Fill in the local course page in editor mode
Now your local course page has been created so you can **head to the editor mode** to view your page:
*For Data Analytics for Berlin for example:*
<https://www.lewagon.dev/berlin/data-analytics-course>
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fe2f0cf46-4fa4-467d-9754-dc617f6cb1d9%2Fswqsq.png?table=block&id=331ed609-f41a-491e-a2c0-f396ba201bf7&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)

![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F0cc7e498-65a4-4c65-8c4c-18ad24f20c7c%2FUntitled.png?table=block&id=872a4266-1ca2-4428-8452-3f28640f1f91&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
### 3. Once filled, publish the page 📣​
Make sure the page is fully filled in and has pictures, head back to the admin and edit your local course as **published**** **✅​
