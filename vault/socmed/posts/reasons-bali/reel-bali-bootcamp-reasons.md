# Reel: 3 Reasons Bali Is the Best Place to Do Your Coding Bootcamp

> **Reframe of [[reel-bali-3-things]]** — the 3 facts are unchanged (villa luxury, world food, weekly social events); what changed is the copy spine. Each fact now answers *"why for MY bootcamp?"* instead of *"why as a tourist?"*, and a new "Bali routine" beat (`0:20 – 0:26`) proves fun **during** the program, not just in Bali. Production apparatus — timing, BGM/SFX map, typography, safe zone, flight tease, loop — carried over as-is.

**Format:** Instagram Reel (9:16 vertical, 1080x1920)
**Style:** No talking head. Two cutdowns in this doc: **§2 — no-VO** (SFX + BGM, full copy as on-screen text overlays) and **§2b — voiceover** (VO narration + short captions).
**Duration:** ~45 seconds
**Topic:** 3 reasons Bali is the best *classroom* — (1) luxury is cheap: a Bali mansion for 5–10M IDR a month — about a week of US groceries, (2) eat the world: every cuisine on earth, one Canggu street, (3) you'll never be bored: board game nights, tech mixers, something on every week. Destination-sell for the **Le Wagon Bali offline bootcamp** — the bootcamp is the vehicle, Bali is the star, the facts hold, the close converts.
**Tone:** Punchy, fast-cut, aspirational but factual. No travel-brochure clichés, no "vacation" framing — a bootcamp is real work, and Bali is the reward system that makes the grind survivable.
**Goal:** Drive applications to the next Le Wagon Bali batch. The thesis hook (`0:00`) reframes the decision; the brand seed at `0:03` names the program; the "Bali routine" beat (`0:20`) is the fun-while-bootcamping proof; the "flight covered" tease at `0:32` opens the second retention loop; the end card converts.

## 1. Retention Hooks (pick one to open the cut)

- **Hook 1 — Thesis + Contrast (Recommended — loop anchor)**
  - *Text overlay:* "The same 9-week bootcamp." → **TWO VERY DIFFERENT LIVES**
  - *Visual:* Split-screen — gray city apartment window (rain) vs. misty rice paddies at dawn; the Bali side wins the frame and expands to full screen on "different lives".
  - *Type:* Pattern Interrupt / reframe. It names the bootcamp decision without burying Bali.
  - *Variants to test:*
    - **1b — Pattern Interrupt: "Coding bootcamps are hard. Bali makes it enjoyable." (Slam cut from a tight code-screen macro to the villa pool; whip pan to rice fields at golden hour.)**
    - **1c — Direct Enumeration:** "3 reasons Bali is the best classroom on earth." (Fast text-slams on each "reason" as it lands later in the cut.)
- **Hook 2 — Negative Curiosity Gap**
  - *Text overlay:* "Weeks comparing syllabi." → "Zero minutes on where you'll actually live."
  - *Visual:* Macro push-in on a laptop scrolling bootcamp comparison tabs; pan up and out to reveal the villa pool right outside the window.
  - *Type:* Negative Curiosity Gap — the gap is the 9 weeks of daily life everyone ignores while they compare the spreadsheet.
- **Hook 3 — Contrarian Stance**
  - *Text overlay:* "Nobody said your campus has to be a building."
  - *Visual:* Flash-cut montage of city office buildings with red "X" stamps, 1.5x digital jump cut on the last stamp, then pull back to the real "campus": villa, rice field, pool, laptop on the table.
  - *Type:* Contrarian Stance — flips the assumption that a campus means a building in a city.

## 3. Production & Shooting Notes

- **Talent:** None. Every frame is B-roll or text — the text overlays carry the story, so no lip-sync, no framing constraints for a face, and cuts can land on any beat.
- **Camera:** 4K @ 30fps, 1/60s shutter (avoids motion-blur artifacts on whip pans and handheld tracking). Shoot villa B-roll at golden hour (first/last hour of light) for the warm "luxury" grade.
- **Drone:** Fly slow, one axis per move — tilt-down on the hook, push-in on the villa. Never combine yaw + tilt.
- **BGM:** One instrumental track — upbeat tropical/afro-house, ~120 BPM, wordless (vocal chops OK). Energy map: `0:00` sparse intro (filtered kick + airy pad) → `0:03` drums in → `0:12` percussion layer → `0:20` full groove (riser under the routine montage) → `0:26` hold → `0:32` pull-back (low-pass, drums out) → `0:36` drop + impact → `0:41` outro resolves to the loop point. Pick a track with a clean loopable phrase.
- **SFX glossary (beat-synced):** text-slam thud on every overlay pop; §2 hook: rain ambience → dawn birdsong · §2b hook: rapid keyboard clatter → pool splash + birdsong (`0:00`); whoosh transitions (`0:03`); soft chime brand ping (`0:03`); water splash (`0:07`); sizzle + knife-chop + plate clink (`0:12`); moped pass + low café chatter (`0:16`); keyboard clatter → wave crash → splash → glass clink (`0:20`); crowd chatter + board-game clatter (`0:26`); record-scratch "wait" + plane flyover (`0:32`); impact + sub drop + brand sting (`0:36`); text-pop ticks (`0:41`).
- **Honest copy (figures to confirm before publish):** before posting, confirm: `[CONFIRM]` the row-3 stat — the 5–10M IDR/month rental range, and the "week of US groceries" equivalence (exchange rate + grocery-basket definition at publish time); fallback: "A private-pool villa for less than a studio back home", `[CONFIRM]` the row-4/5 claim — the cuisines shown are actually findable on one Canggu street (availability, not price), `[CONFIRM]` row-7's events still run at publish time — weekly board game night (Sundays, Joon Canggu: free, solo walk-ins matched) and the tech/AI developer mixer (Canggu), per canggu_events_schedule.md; trim specific examples if the calendar turns over, and `[CONFIRM]` the flight-offer terms — which regions are covered, batch dates, any conditions (rows 9–11). If a number can't be verified, cut it and let the comparison stand alone.
- **Safe-zone alert:** Keep all text overlays horizontally centered in the 1080x1350 safe zone — clear of the top 15% (status bar), bottom 20% (caption gradient + CTA), and right 15% (like/comment rail).
- **Typography (Ads & Social hierarchy):** Inter only — headlines/CTAs **Inter Bold 700**, subtitle lines **Inter Medium 500**, uppercase, letter-spacing 0, line-height 1.1–1.3 (headings) / 1.2 (subtitles). No italics, no red font colour. Over B-roll use White/Cream text with a subtle scrim; Orange Peel `#FF9D0B` pill chips for the "Reason 1/2/3" accent labels only.
- **CTA strategy:** Tease the offer visually at `0:32`; land it on the end card: "LE WAGON BALI · NEXT BATCH · FLIGHT COVERED · LINK IN BIO". Pin a comment with the flight-offer terms + next batch date. Comment-trigger: "Comment **FLIGHT** and we'll DM the details."
- **Brand seed:** Keep a small "Le Wagon Bali" corner logo lockup on-screen from `0:03` through the flight tease — the front seed is text + logo, without touching the `0:00` hook.
- **B-Roll checklist:** split-screen gray city apartment (rain) + rice field drone (§2 hook); code-editor macro (typing, dark IDE) + poolside laptop reveal shot (§2b hook); Le Wagon Bali campus exterior/sign + classroom (students from behind, no faces); pool + bedroom interior; 3 food flat-lays; Canggu street walkthrough; day-routine shots (laptop in villa, surf at beach, pool float, dinner table — students from behind, no faces); events footage (board game table, tech mixer, quiz night, sunset DJ pool party — no faces); sunset silhouette; plane-window/boarding-pass prop. All footage is B-roll so text timing can be re-cut freely in post.
