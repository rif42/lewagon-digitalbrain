# Reel: Meet the Current Batch

**Format:** Instagram Reel  
**Duration:** 60 seconds  
**Topic:** Introduce the currently running Le Wagon Bali data science batch — high-caliber students with strong technical backgrounds.  
**Tone:** Natural, documentary-style, community-focused. Avoid corporate voiceover.  
**Goal:** Build credibility and belonging. Show peer quality and make prospective students want to join the next batch.

---

## Shot list

| # | Time | Shot | Visual | Text Overlay | Audio |
|---|------|------|--------|--------------|-------|
| 1 | 0-3s | Hook | Wide shot of campus or empty classroom in morning light | "This batch is not what you'd expect." | Upbeat ambient track |
| 2 | 3-6s | Transition | Students walking in, laptops opening | "Data science bootcamp. Bali." | Beat drop |
| 3 | 6-18s | Student A | Medium close-up at desk or campus space | Name + previous role + current project | Natural voiceover or clean name card |
| 4 | 18-30s | Student B | Same framing | Name + previous role + current project | — |
| 5 | 30-42s | Student C | Same framing | Name + previous role + current project | — |
| 6 | 42-50s | Group / collaboration | Pair programming, whiteboard, demo prep | "You'll learn as much from your peers as from the curriculum." | Voiceover |
| 7 | 50-55s | Bali environment | Campus to beach or sunset, one aspirational shot | "Study. Build. Reset." | — |
| 8 | 55-60s | End card | Static graphic with Le Wagon Bali branding | "Apply for the next batch. Link in bio." | — |

---

## Student interview questions

Ask these in one take. Let students ramble; edit the best lines later.

1. "What were you doing right before this batch?"
2. "What made you drop everything and do a data bootcamp in Bali?"
3. "What's the most unexpected thing you've learned so far?"
4. "What's one project you're working on that actually excites you?"
5. "Describe the person sitting next to you in one word."

---

## Voiceover script

> [0-3s] "This batch is not what you'd expect."
>
> [3-42s, over profiles] "Astrophysicist. Product lead. Biotech researcher. Former lawyer. They all ended up here. Not because they needed another credential. Because they wanted to build things with data."
>
> [42-50s] "And the best part? You'll learn as much from them as from the curriculum."
>
> [50-55s] "Study. Build. Reset."
>
> [55-60s] "Le Wagon Bali. Next batch — apply now."

## On-screen text template per student

Replace for each profile:

```
Name
[First name only]

Before
[One previous role or field]

Now
[One thing they're building]

One word
[Self-description or cohort vibe]
```

Example:

```
MARCO
Before: Astrophysicist
Now: Building a climate risk model
One word: "Obsessive"
```

---

## Profile shot flow

Each student profile should follow this rhythm:

1. Student looks up from laptop.
2. They say their name and previous role in one line.
3. Cut to hands on keyboard / project screen / notebook.
4. Final frame: their "one word" with a small smile.

Keep framing consistent across all profiles for clean cuts.

---

## Natural hook options

Choose one or test two versions:

- "I didn't expect to be sitting next to a [former role] today."
- "Everyone here is overqualified and under-slept."
- "The best part of this bootcamp isn't the lectures."
- "This is what happens when you put brilliant people in a room in Bali."

---

## Production checklist

- Get written consent from every student filmed.
- Confirm pronunciation of names and preferred job titles.
- Film in good natural light: morning or late afternoon.
- Keep camera at eye level for profiles.
- Capture clean campus B-roll separately.
- Record room tone for clean audio edits.
