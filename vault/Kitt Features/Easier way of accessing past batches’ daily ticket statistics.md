# Easier way of accessing past batches’ daily ticket statistics.
When staffing, it can be useful to see what were the ticket statistics of the same day in previous batches. How many tickets were opened, which moment of the day was the busiest, etc., are all helpful things to know.
This data is available in the Ticket Statistics dashboard. But there’s currently no easy way to retrieve it for previous batches. You have to go to the batch's tickets and statistics dashboard, which will always be on the last day of the bootcamp. From there, you have to go through all previous day's statistic until you reach your targeted day. Alternatively, you can edit the query params of the request, but that requires knowledge of the exact date and the exact path name. Both approaches are quite annoying.

Having a better interface to access this already existing data for past batches. Maybe have all the days in a list or calendar format, allowing access to the ticket statistics of specific days with a single click.
