Thanks to the [[Expose challenge completion to students|Expose Challenge Completion to Students]] feature, students can track their challenge completion percentage.
On Web & Data Science tracks, we also monitor flashcard completion as part of project eligibility. This data is shown per unit to students but not as a total, even though the **Ops team has access to the overall flashcard completion %** on the internal dashboard.
This creates a visibility gap that leads to student confusion and high message volume, particularly in the weeks leading up to Pitch Night, when eligibility becomes critical.
Improving this would align the Flashcards tracking feature with Challenge tracking, make students more autonomous, and save time for the teams.
