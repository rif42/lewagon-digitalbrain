# Kitt staffing improvement
## Summary
> [!note] 
> Explain the problem in one sentence.
Currently, when a CM/PM/BM staffs a Teacher for a day, even if the teacher hasn’t accepted yet the days, the teacher can’t refuse the date, and can’t be staffed on an other calendar.
This problem was shared with us by a teacher directly.
This is the screenshot from a teacher point of view :
![](https://app.notion.com/image/attachment%3A0f7efa57-ef08-4084-902e-036a1a052f0a%3Aimage.png?table=block&id=2878924a-f014-80b3-bec4-eff466d30adc&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
## Business Impact
> [!note] 
> Explain business impact of your issue
On the picture above, the teacher has been staffed on Week 9 in Lisbon. He hasn’t accepted yet the days.
The teacher only option here is to accept the days. If for some reason he needs to refuse, he will have to contact the ops contact, to be removed. Sometime it takes few days for the CM/PM to answer, meanwhile the teacher is “stuck” and can’t be staffed in an other city.
In the mean time, no other city can add him to their calendar.
## Context & Problem
> [!note] 
> TODO: Explain in great details the context surrounding this issue
It is a teacher who shared this problem with us [here](https://github.com/lewagon/teachers/issues/3485#issuecomment-3364540823).
It would be good to avoid go and back messages, and give more option on the teacher interface, allowing him to refuse or accept the days. Maybe give the option to other cities to staff him and see where he is potentially staffed.
It’s a quick fix to earn time on slack.
