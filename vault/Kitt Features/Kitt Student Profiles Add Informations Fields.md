## Context & Problem
> [!note] 
> TODO: Explain in great details the context surrounding this issue
Unless teachers are involved from Setup Day, they rarely get formally introduced to every student. The **Students tab on Kitt** is often the only way for teachers to identify who's in the class and learn basic information about them.
Not all teacher take time to redo a class tour to know the students. Students might also be tired of repeating several times basic informations about themselves.
Two key informations could be added** **on student profiles, to make easier the way teachers address and interact with students:
1. **Pronouns: **Teachers have no way of knowing a student's pronouns other than assuming or asking repeatedly. It can become burdensome for students across multiple batches and teachers.
        1.
1. **Preferred name / nickname: **Many students go by a middle name, nickname, or shortened version of their name. Without a dedicated field, teachers frequently get it wrong, especially with students who have multiple names.
        1.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/attachment%3A8bf3b13d-4c01-465e-ade5-904fe724c2ce%3Aimage.png?table=block&id=3198924a-f014-805e-b0a9-ca6e8d8ff9bd&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
→ Those fields could be optional for students, but existing when they created their Kitt profile.
