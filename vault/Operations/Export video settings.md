# Export video settings
## **📤 Best settings, file types, and where to store finished videos.
**
### **Recommended export settings:**
- Format: **MP4 (H.264 codec)**
- Resolution:
        - **1080p (1920x1080 or 1080x1920)** → standard for social media
        - **4K (3840x2160 or 2160x3840)** → optional for high-end / archival use
- Frame rate: **30fps** (default) or **60fps** (for smooth action/B-roll)
- Bitrate: **10–12 Mbps for 1080p**, **35 Mbps for 4K**
- Audio: AAC, 128 kbps minimum
### **Aspect ratios by platform:**
- **9:16 (Vertical)** → Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Stories
- **16:9 (Horizontal)** → YouTube (long-form), webinars, internal presentations
- **1:1 (Square)** → Facebook feed, LinkedIn posts (optional, less common now)
- **4:5 (Portrait)** → Instagram feed posts (can work better than square for visibility)
### **Where to store after exporting?**
- Upload final versions to the **Content Library (Notion/Google Drive)**.
- Organize by folder → Year > Campaign > Video Type.
- Add link to video in the corresponding Notion card for easy reference.
