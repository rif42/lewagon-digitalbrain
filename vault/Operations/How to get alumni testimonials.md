# How to get alumni testimonials
> [!note] 
> Here you can find a list of best practices and interview questions for student/ alumni testimonials.
Please reach out to @Lynsey Cook if you have any questions or suggestions!
### Best practices
---
- Get in touch with your local career advisors, lead teachers or batch managers to identify top students and interesting profiles.
- Utilize your top quotes for social media and the website.
- Transcribe your video testimonials into blog posts and make the most out of your content.
- Utilize AI to facilitate the creation of your content
        - Use Otter to transcribe your interview
                    - Host a call or an in person interview with your student/ alumni and record it using <https://otter.ai/home>.
                    - This AI tool records and transcribes your discussion for an easy blog adaptation 🔥​
        - Use ChatGPT to help with your blog brief 💪​
