# Lead Journey Analysis - Hubspot
# About the project
---
### Context & Problem
*mandatory to create the idea** What is the challenge or pain point this project comes to solve?*
> [!note] 
> In today's fast-paced and competitive market, understanding the complete lead journey—from first brand interaction to becoming an applicant—is essential for crafting effective marketing and sales strategies. Our organization faces a critical conversion challenge: while our SQL/MQL conversion is strong (over 50%), the Applicant-to-SQL conversion remains significantly low (under 12%, with a target of 30%). This represents a major gap in our funnel that directly impacts our ability to convert qualified leads into actual applicants.
Currently, we lack comprehensive visibility into where leads drop off in the funnel, which acquisition channels perform best in converting leads to applicants, and how effective our current lead nurturing strategies are. Without this data-driven understanding, we cannot make informed decisions to enhance user experience, enable personalized outreach, or implement targeted optimizations that drive higher engagement and conversions. The absence of clear insights into lead behavior and interaction points prevents us from identifying bottlenecks and inefficiencies that are costing us potential applicants.
### The project
*mandatory to move in backlog** What does the project look like? What is the expected outcome?*
> [!note] 
> Conduct a comprehensive analysis and mapping of the complete lead journey within HubSpot to identify gaps, obstacles, and optimization opportunities. The project involves a thorough audit of lead behavior and interaction points, systematic identification of drop-off points throughout the funnel, and detailed mapping of key touchpoints in the lead journey.
We will analyze quantitative and qualitative data from multiple touchpoints, segment leads by source, behavior, and journey stage, and use advanced visualization tools to map and analyze lead flow. The analysis will leverage HubSpot Analytics, Google Analytics, Hotjar for behavioral data, and Metabase for data visualization. Additionally, we will explore AI customer journey mapping tools to gain deeper insights.
Based on findings, we will implement targeted improvements aimed at boosting lead activation rates and significantly improving the SQL-to-Applicant conversion rate. The project includes creating a comprehensive monitoring dashboard for ongoing lead journey optimization and establishing scalable strategies to improve the overall lead funnel performance.
