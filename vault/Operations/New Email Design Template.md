# New Email Design Template
# Summary
### Context & Problem
Our previous email design had become outdated, failing to align with current trends and standards. This stagnation in design quality was impacting our engagement metrics and, most critically, our conversion rates. The need for a modernised approach was evident to ensure our communications resonated effectively with our brand image, audience and facilitated our marketing goals.
### Solution
To address this issue, we embarked on a comprehensive redesign of our email templates. The project resulted in the creation of 9 new, fresh templates, each designed with modern aesthetics and user engagement in mind (mobile responsive). Recognising the importance of flexibility and personalization in email marketing, each template and its accompanying modules were crafted to be fully adaptable and customisable. This allows for a wide range of uses, catering to different content types and marketing strategies. The templates were coded in HTML and integrated into HubSpot, ensuring seamless deployment. This update not only revitalizes our email marketing campaigns with a more appealing and contemporary design but also sets a robust foundation for improving our conversion rates through enhanced user engagement and interaction.
### Goal & KPI
Improve the global conversion rate lead to apply by improving the email design
