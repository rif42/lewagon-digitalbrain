# Events Cadence
### Important Reminder for Local Teams:
The centralized event calendar outlines the minimum number of events we aim to run across regions. If you're part of a local team, you're encouraged to host additional events based on your capacity and local opportunities.
We understand that working with partners can sometimes involve delays or last-minute changes, including cancellations. That’s why **anticipation is key:**
- Reach out to partners at least 2 months in advance
- Always have a backup plan in case the original collaboration falls through
- When timelines are tight, leverage the [[Events Hub|Events Library]] in the Event Hub to launch quick, high-impact MVP events that still drive value
The goal is to remain proactive, flexible, and resourceful — even under constraints.

- Thursday morning: Best for targeting niche audiences such as freelancers or entrepreneurs
