# DIY Video editing best practices
## **✂️ What to consider during the editing process?
**
### **Rhythm & pace**
- Keep clips **short (3–6 seconds each)**.
- Cut out pauses & filler words (“uhm,” “like”).
- Use jump cuts for quick flow.
### **Timing**
- Hook in first **3 seconds** → don’t bury key info at the end.
- CTA at the last **3–5 seconds**.
### **Transitions**
- Simple cuts work best.
- Avoid heavy transitions (wipes, spins) → looks unprofessional.
### **Subtitles & overlays**
- Always add captions → 80%+ people watch without sound.
- Use **brand colors** (red/black/white).
- Highlight keywords (bold or different color).
