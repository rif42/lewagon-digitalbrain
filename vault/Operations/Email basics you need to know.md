# Email basics you need to know
> [!note] 
> **Hey there!** Emails are important and they’re a great way to engage with our community.
At Le Wagon, mass emails are sent by **central marketing team** for TOFU content, brand campaigns, monthly newsletter, transactional, promotional, nurturing, or automated campaigns. Your POC on this topic is the CRM team :)
They are also sent by **regional marketing teams** sharing content that is more local, personalized, or promotes specific regional topics or events. This is generally done by the regional marketing managers, or city managers!
Lastly, the **sales team **also sends emails to leads but they are mostly 1:1 emails such as meeting invites, follow-ups, or doc sharing.
Regardless of your team, you never know when the need to make & send an email may pop up. So here is everything you need to know about emailing at Le Wagon! ⭐️
# Lets imagine you want to make an email, where do you start?
Go to Hubspot and click on Marketing Emails, from there, click on Create Email in the top right corner. Choose whether you want to make a Regular email or an Automated email (an email that is added to a workflow).
Once you see this screen, you should know that these templates are old and we no longer use them. To see the current templates, click on the Drag and Drop button.
Now you are viewing our most current templates, and can select any of these based on your need. We use templates instead of the native hubspot editor to ensure that we maintain the same branding and email quality across all our regions , as well as make the email creation process more efficient.

## Personalization 👋​
To make the email more personal use Tokens, tokens are properties that are known about a contact. You have *many ***personalization token** options. From basic ones like First Name to more advance ones such as Currency, or Bootcamp Prediction.
[No access](https://app.notion.com/p/f6843a9e8ead42789f39561bee15ebfa?pvs=24#f6843a9e8ead42789f39561bee15ebfa)
For more information about available and most common properties, refer to [No access](https://app.notion.com/p/2188532e726f40eda77877564c69f0d7?pvs=24#2188532e726f40eda77877564c69f0d7)
We also often use **Smart Rules (SR)** to further personalize emails to our audience. You can add several lists - SRs - as different sections and the content will be adapted automatically for the lead based on which list they are a part of. We use smart rules when we want to different content to different leads, or same content but in different languages, and other use cases.

## Segmentation and lists 📋​

There is no one-size-fits-all segmentation. The list of audience you create depends on many factors: who are you targeting? what is your content? what is your goal?
Find a more detailed tutorial on creating lists here [No access](https://app.notion.com/p/1058924af01480a18991cf383a77c60c?pvs=24#1058924af01480a18991cf383a77c60c)
Also make sure to read all about our available Properties as this is what will give you the best idea of which ones to use to create the list you need without any overlapping.
To start, go to Lists —> Create List —> Choose Active or Static. Add a name for your list, then begin choosing your segmentation properties. If you are working for the B2C segment,** make sure you always choose Core/Franchises —> Core, Lead Type —> B2C**. Otherwise you may accidentally include b2b leads or franchises. We **never** send manual or automated email campaigns to b2b leads or franchises, unless specifically requested.
You should also know that for most campaigns it is advised to **exclude Alumni**, as they already completed one of our courses and the TOV/communication for this audience should be different than to SQLs/MQLs/etc. If you want to target alumni, you can create a separate list and email or use SRs.

### Pre-send checklist ✔️​
- [ ] **Check Your List**: Are you sending the email to the correct segment? Are you sure there are no overlaps?
- [ ] **Test Email**: Send a test email to yourself or a colleague to ensure everything looks good—no broken links, images, or funky formatting.
- [ ] **Preview on Mobile & Desktop**: Emails might look different on mobile. Make sure it’s optimized for both views.
- [ ] **Check Personalization**: If you’re using smart rules, ensure they’re set up correctly and showing the right content to the right leads.
- [ ] **CTA Links**: Double-check that all your links work and point to the correct landing pages.
- [ ] **Make sure you choose the correct subscription type!!!**
- [ ] **Final Proofread**: Look for any spelling or grammar mistakes.
- [ ] **Confirm Timing**: Schedule the email at the optimal time and date for your audience.
- [ ] **Ensure you have created a proper Campaign & all CTAs have UTMs**
- [ ] **Monitor After Sending**: Once the email is sent, check the stats in HubSpot—open rates, click-through rates, etc. to see how it performed.
