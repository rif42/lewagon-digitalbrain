# Running a webinar
# Preparation on Learn
Congratulations, you were selected to run a webinar for Le Wagon!
Make sure to complete each of those steps to be ready and offer participants the best possible experience 🙌
You should have received an invitation to join a batch on [Learn](http://learn.lewagon.com/), the platform that hosts our workshops. If it's not the case, ask @Anouk Hajage to invite you again.
When you join the batch on Learn, you should land on the **Instructions**:

## Watch the video
The workshop is split into a 1h15 lecture and 45 minutes of practice where you act as a TA. Start by watching the replay of the lecture with a good set of headphones, in a quiet place, where you won't be interrupted, and take notes actively.
![](https://lewagon.notion.site/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Ff75c2ae2-0956-4d89-9194-d84863b124cc%2FScreenshot_2020-04-07_at_20.49.03.png?table=block&id=9f70c5ad-6909-4721-a1f7-2c1d47643308&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1380&userId=&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
## Repeat the livecodes / demo
Make sure you are able to perform all the livecodes and demos shown in the video. Practice as much as you need to make sure you will be able to **run the lecture in 1h15** maximum. Whatever the time it takes for you to give the lecture, it's key to stop 1h15 after the start of the event. It takes practice to find the right pace!
## Read the takeaways
The *transcript* of the lecture is available in the **Takeaways** section. Make sure you read it thoroughly and don't hesitate to enrich your notes with examples and conclusions taken from there. It's key for the teacher to know the takeaways by heart and to be able to share them clearly and concisely.
![](https://lewagon.notion.site/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fc2e9cdff-cd17-472f-a107-65c1f8b00919%2FScreenshot_2020-04-07_at_20.50.48.png?table=block&id=ba4f719d-e12f-40d9-b520-af28523bc127&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1380&userId=&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
## Take a look at the challenges
In the 2nd part of the workshop, attendees will sign in on Learn and do the challenges by themselves. While they are signing in, you will have to show them the platform so they don't feel lost when they start practicing on their own.
Make sure to take a look at the challenges at least once to be able to guide the attendees efficiently.
![](https://lewagon.notion.site/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F30cc85a2-eed4-4c92-bc15-02fa3ca863fb%2FScreenshot_2020-04-07_at_20.51.31.png?table=block&id=1e7a2753-0686-4e42-bb15-31b855c04852&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1380&userId=&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
# Mock up webinar with a Le Wagon Tech member
A few hours before the real webinar, you will be invited to **do a mock up webinar **by one of Le Wagon Tech member. The format would be really easy and quick, 10 / 15 min webinar with feedback at the end to have the best possible setup for the real one!
When it's Mock up webinar o'clock, join the webinar room by clicking on **the link in the onboarding email**. Once you're on the platform, set up your microphone and camera and click on "Go on stage" in the pop up to access the room.
Then you can proceed to the test! If you have any questions regarding the technical aspect of our webinars, this is the good time to ask them.
***NB****: if you are from Le Wagon tech team and need to access the webinar room, ping **@Anouk Hajage** on slack, she will add you as a team member.
*
**Technical settings check list:**
- [ ] Open 2 browsers with Learn:
- **1 with the Slides in fullscreen mode on a 2nd dedicated desktop**: keep it there the whole time (don’t refresh!)
- **1 in your primary desktop** to do a quick tour of the platform at the end of the lecture (emphasise the Feedback!) during students onboarding
- [ ] Open your slides on learn before the webinar starts as they might take a bit of time to load
- [ ] Set your screen resolution to **720p** (1280x720)
- [ ] The only app running on your computer should be Finder, Sublime Text, Terminal, Browser and Livestorm (quit all other apps)
- [ ] Have your text editor and terminal with a **light background**. You can install the [Solarized](http://ethanschoonover.com/solarized) Light theme, with the [Sublime Text package](https://github.com/braver/Solarized) and the [OSX terminal theme](https://github.com/tomislav/osx-terminal.app-colors-solarized)
- [ ] Zoom a lot your font size (⌘=) in Sublime Text and your terminal
- [ ] If you use your browser hide your Bookmarks bar → ⌘ + shift + B on Mac
- [ ] When you don't use your browser in full screen hide your dock menu → System preferences > Dock > Tick Automatically hide and show the Dock
- [ ] Switch from one file to the other with the ⌘P (Command Palette) in Sublime Text
- [ ] Cut your slack/other app notifications
- [ ] Mute your livestorm notifications (click on thee bell near the action button)
- [ ] You should use two windows layouts:
- [ ] Don't put a bright light behind you → don't sit with a window in your back
- [ ] Sit in a bright room. If it is not well lit by natural light turn on the lights inside
- [ ] Get a good pair of headphones and if possible a microphone
- [ ] Don't move around your apps or switch between desktops too often → it gets very hectic
- [ ] Look into the camera when you speak to people
- [ ] Make pauses at the end of each section and take a moment to display to remind key info to the participants :
- Attendees must ask their questions in the **Question section.** TAs will answer them in live
- Participant will receive a **replay link** of the workshop by email after it ends.
- At the end of the workshop they will have a **lifetime access to Learn** and access workshop takeaways, exercises and corrections to keep practicing on their own.
# Running the webinar
Before joining the room, take a moment to read this next section so you have all the steps in mind.
## Last check up with the team
20 minutes before the webinar starts, the city manager will invite you and the TAs to join the room for a **quick brief & a last check up**.
Before the start, make sure you **open the Slides in a tab of your browser** (be careful, some decks may take time to load), and test the resolution of your computer on livestorm. **All the technical guidelines checked during the Mock up webinar must be applied carefully** 👌
Le Wagon city manager will then go live, **start the webinar and do the intro**.
## **City Manager Intro (5 minutes)**
👋 **Introducing Le Wagon**
> [!note] 
> Le Wagon is a coding school that teaches students the tech skills and entrepreneurial mindset they need to thrive, through intensive coding bootcamps. We founded Le Wagon in 2013 and since then more than 10,000 people have graduated from our courses, either full-time (9 weeks) or part-time (24 weeks).
We are the world’s leading coding bootcamp. Le Wagon is the most acclaimed coding bootcamp worldwide on Switchup according to student reviews. We’re trusted by students to give people the best quality tech education in our 41 campuses.
☝️ **Reminding the rules **
- Attendees must ask their questions in the **Question section.** TAs will answer them in live.
- Participant will receive a **replay link** of the workshop by email after it ends.
- At the end of the workshop they will have a **lifetime access to Learn** and access workshop takeaways, exercises and corrections to keep practicing on their own.
🎓 **Explaining Workshop Format **
- The webinar starts with **1h15 of lecture** during which they should listen carefully - no need to take notes as takeaways will be on Learn then.
- After the lecture they will be invited to join Learn and have access to all the materials used during the 1st part : take aways, exercises and corrections to keep practicing on their own.
- Insist on the fact that **we will ask for their feedbac**k at the end of the webinar
> [!note] 
> **Important**: don't onboard the attendees on Learn before the end of the lecture.
🤾‍♀️**Introducing the team**
- One by one, the lead teacher (you) and the TAs introduce themselves and their background, why did they get interested in coding?
- After the intro, the city manager and TAs cut their cameras.
When everyone is ready and the city manager introduced Le Wagon to the attendees, it's your turn to enter the scene. Start the workshop and enjoy!
## Lecture (1h15)
During the whole lecture, you** only focuses on the lecture** and don't look at the chat / answer questions. It will be **TAs role to answer questions** in the question section.
Run your lecture, find the right pace and keep an eye on the watch, 1h15 is short!
**Take a pause between each section** so TAs have time to answer questions. Each time you do, make sure to **remind participants those 3 key info**:
- They must ask their questions in the **Question section.** TAs will answer them in live
- They will receive a **replay link** of the workshop by email after it ends.
- At the end of the workshop they will have a lifetime access to Learn and access workshop takeaways, exercises and corrections to keep practicing on their own.
## Onboarding (10 min)
When your lecture is over, it's time to onboard attendees on the batch on Learn. The city manager will be the one sharing them the url to have them onboard in 1 click!
While they are signed in, sign in yourself and keep sharing your screen to show them the platform.
## Show them the platform & ask for feedbacks
- Ask them to go to the **Your Turn** section where they will find the instructions of the challenges to keep practicing on their own.
- Show them the **Takeaways** section: they will find here all the key concepts covered during the workshop.
- Ask them to fill the **feedback form** before they leave. You can remind them they will keep their account on the platform for life.
![](https://lewagon.notion.site/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F136510af-483c-4684-a1a3-4f09dc89a154%2FScreenshot_2020-04-07_at_20.54.55.png?table=block&id=d329148f-0567-4284-9977-9c8fd0d85d6a&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1380&userId=&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
After you made sure that onboarding went smoothly and that there is no more questions to answer you can then ask the city manager to end the webinar.
