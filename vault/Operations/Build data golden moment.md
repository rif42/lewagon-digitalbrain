### Context & Problem
*mandatory to create the idea** What is the challenge or pain point this project comes to solve?*
> [!note] 
> The marketing team is facing a data crisis where concepts are unclear, data scopes are not properly defined (particularly around batch-based measurement and campus-based vs. origin-based tracking for B2C), KPIs lack clarity on what matters to whom, and existing dashboards may not be providing the right visibility.
### The project
*mandatory to move in backlog** What does the project look like? What is the expected outcome?*
> [!note] 
> Establish a clear understanding of our data definitions within Marketing and produce a dual-model data framework for marketing performance measurement that distinguishes between batch-centric completion tracking (dynamic, campus-based) and acquisition tracking (static, based on first country/campus).
