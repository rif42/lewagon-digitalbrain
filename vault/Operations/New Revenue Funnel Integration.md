### Context & Problem
> [!note] 
> Le Wagon's revenue funnel lacked a shared definition. Lifecycle stages existed in HubSpot but were inconsistently defined and used. There was no clear ownership between Marketing, Sales, and Admissions, leading to overlapping communications, missed re-engagements, and no reliable funnel analytics. The Revenue Funnel project (RevFun) completed the strategic definition across 4 meetings. This project is the implementation phase: turning the validated paper model into a working HubSpot configuration.
