## **Job description**
Our current global organization is well designed to manage our acquisition and activation channels (paid marketing, SEO, email marketing, events,...) and ensure lead generation and students. To go further and to better support new product launch, we want to create new levers for growth through innovative solutions. These solutions might require the creation of interactive tools/landing pages such as a platform with free access short courses, dynamic qualification forms, implementation of a referral program…all ideas are welcomed!
As a member of the Global Marketing Team and more specifically the Global Growth Team, you will contribute to the strategy and roadmap of growth innovations, and you will be in charge of its execution.
You will also be responsible for assessing the performance of these innovations in different markets, and then iterating and deploying them internationally.
In short, under the direction of a Growth Marketing Manager, and in close collaboration with the other experts in the team you will be working on all innovative projects aimed at improving acquisition and activation on our existing offer, as well as on the launch of new offers.
**YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES**
As a Growth Project Manager at Le Wagon, you will :
- **Find innovative ideas to diversify our acquisition and activation**
        - Understand our personas and our ecosystem to find levers and channels that could impact our growth the most.
        - Analyze our funnel to understand our challenges.
        - Benchmarks tools and tactics used by other companies.
- **Build new acquisition, qualification and activation mechanisms**
        - Based on the innovation growth roadmap, launch all tests and POCs planned.
        - Our Engineering team will always be there to help you, but you need to be as autonomous as possible, to build POC and iterate fast. To do so, you will have to use no-code tools such as Bubble, Maker, Airtable or any other relevant tools.
- **Measure performances, iterate and deploy globally**
        - Monitor performances of each project launched to assess the impact and help decision on deploying or iterating.
        - Iterate until the results are satisfactory and meet the objectives set.
        - Work closely with Regional Marketing teams to deploy globally.
- **Content Marketing**
    To support some initiatives or the launch of new offers, it will require content production
        - **Content Project Management:** Work in collaboration with the new product team to define the content needed to promote the new programmes (introductory courses, workshops, tutorials, etc.) and with the content team to ensure production.
        - **Content Production:** When launching new offers, you will be responsible for updating some content on our different media (website, emails,...)
**PREFERRED EXPERIENCE**
- You are a **clear and confident communicator,** with exceptional written and verbal **English language skills**
- **2 years of experience** in growth/acquisition for high growth companies.
- You **master a no-code tool** and some web development skills.
- You are a creative problem solver - **highly structured, organized, and have the ability to work to tight deadlines** to provide practical solutions on multiple projects
- You have **strong project management skills** and you are data-driven & **detail-oriented**
- You are **hands-on and persistent** – you try everything!
- You can take initiative, work independently and demonstrate your impact internally.
- Ability to coordinate internal and external, local and international resources
