# Finding subjects / people to record
## **👥 Who can you record? How to approach them?
**
### **Possible subjects:**
- Current students (classroom life, testimonials).
- Alumni (career stories, short interviews).
- Staff & teachers (explainers, tips, event introductions).
- Guest speakers at events.
### **How to approach them?**
- Ask in advance and explain the purpose clearly.
- Reassure them it’s casual and can be re-shot if needed.
- Provide simple guidelines (look into camera, speak naturally, smile).
- Always get verbal consent before recording → share final cut with them if possible.
