Similarly to the average of the general Le Wagon alumni, they have a degree - most of them have a bachelor and some of them have also a masters.

### **3. Experience:**
Similarly to the general Le Wagon persona, they have completed high education, almost all of them bachelor’s (70%) but around 36% do also have masters.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fprod-files-secure.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2F2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f%2Faed0925e-0512-4837-a4a2-a397dbd810a4%2Fimage.png?table=block&id=12e8924a-f014-80e9-9fd4-e828c3ca948a&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
### **4. Motivation:**
Regarding the motivation, the full-time students are career switchers while the part-time students are upskillers. That is the main difference and it probably has to do as well with the fact that full-time are unemployed while part-time are working.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fprod-files-secure.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2F2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f%2F7c2a31fb-9293-4aad-9e32-c0003380c7ac%2Fimage.png?table=block&id=12e8924a-f014-8092-89f1-c3cd1c1c47e3&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
