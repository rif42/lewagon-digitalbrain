# Audio best practices
## **🎙️ How to achieve optimal sound quality (even without dedicated microphones).
**
### **If you have a microphone:**
- Use a clip-on lav mic or shotgun mic for clarity.
- Place it close to the speaker’s mouth (but not visible).
### **If you don’t have a mic:**
- Record indoors in a **quiet room** (avoid echoes).
- Ask the subject to **stand close to the phone (1–2 meters max)**.
- Use **soft furnishings** (curtains, rugs) to reduce echo.
- Avoid windy/external noise when outdoors.
