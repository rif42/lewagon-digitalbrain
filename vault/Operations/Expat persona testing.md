# Expat persona testing
# About the project
---
### Context & Problem
*mandatory to create the idea** What is the challenge or pain point this project comes to solve?*
> [!note] 
> We currently lack a clear understanding of the expat audience living in Europe — their motivations, needs, and engagement triggers. This limits our ability to tailor messaging, content, and services across marketing touchpoints. Without data and insights, it’s difficult to validate assumptions or build personas that reflect real user behavior. This project addresses that gap by using a targeted campaign to **test messaging, gather engagement data, and lay the foundation for a data-driven Expat persona**.
### The project
*mandatory to move in backlog** What does the project look like? What is the expected outcome?*
> [!note] 
> We’ll run a **paid social media campaign targeting expats across Europe** to promote the download of our “European Job Report.” The campaign will test various creatives and messages (career growth, relocation trends, local job opportunities) to identify which resonate best.
