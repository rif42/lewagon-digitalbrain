### Spot Highlights

- **Munduk Coffee** *– a scenic coffee estate surrounded by misty hills, where students can enjoy fresh local coffee, stunning views, and join hands-on experiences like coffee tasting, plantation walks, or traditional craft workshops.*
    - Visit **Munduk Coffee Plantation** or **Munduk Moding Plantation**, known for coffee tasting with panoramic valley views.
    - Learn about local Arabica roasting and try fresh plantation-brewed coffee or chocolate.
    - Optional short **waterfall trek** — *Munduk Waterfall* or *Melanting Waterfall* (10–20 mins walk).
    - Experience local workshops:
        - Coffee brewing or farm walk experience
        - Traditional cooking class using local ingredients
        - Guided plantation tour with stories from local farmers
    - Optional photo stop at **Twin Lakes Viewpoint (Buyan & Tamblingan)** nearby.
    
    Web: [https://mundukmodingplantation.com/munduk-coffee/](https://mundukmodingplantation.com/munduk-coffee/)
    
    Maps: [https://maps.app.goo.gl/YLe9vCeHcWcvqX8L7](https://maps.app.goo.gl/YLe9vCeHcWcvqX8L7)

- **Menjangan Island** *– part of West Bali National Park, known for its crystal-clear water and incredible coral reefs — great for snorkeling beginners.*
    - Take a short boat ride to **Menjangan Island**, part of Bali Barat National Park.
    - Beginner-friendly snorkeling with calm, clear water.
    - See coral walls, turtles, clownfish, and underwater temples.
    - Entrance fee: IDR 200k–300k/person (includes park and boat).
    
    Maps: [https://maps.app.goo.gl/c12DKf3TDKL5EusKA](https://maps.app.goo.gl/c12DKf3TDKL5EusKA)

    - Learn how local groups protect sea turtles and coral reefs.
    - Optional: help release baby turtles (depending on hatching season).
    - Insightful, hands-on environmental learning experience.
