## Quick Comparison

| **Option** | **Travel Time from Canggu** | **Main Highlights** | **Pros ✅** | **Cons ⚠️** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Amed & Tirta Gangga (East Bali)** | ~3.5–4 hrs | Snorkeling at Lipah Beach, Tirta Gangga Water Palace, Bukit Cinta sunrise trek, optional Lempuyang Temple visit | - Beautiful blend of culture and sea  
- Great sunrise and snorkeling  
- Authentic local charm  - Affordable stays and food | - Long drive (~4 hrs)  
- Limited cafés and nightlife  - Some rocky snorkeling spots |
| **2. Pemuteran & Menjangan Island (West Bali)**  | ~4.5 hrs | Menjangan snorkeling, Turtle Hatchery, Biorock coral project, Munduk coffee stop | - Best marine biodiversity in Bali
- Peaceful and eco-conscious  
- Unique conservation experiences  
- Scenic road via Munduk | - Farthest route (~4.5 hrs)  
- Higher park/boat costs  
- Limited restaurants and shops |
| **3. Padangbai & Candidasa (East Coast)** | ~2 hrs | Blue Lagoon snorkeling, Virgin Beach, Tenganan Village, Bali Asli lunch | - Closest and smoothest travel  
- Balanced mix of activity and chill  
- Great dining and villa options  
- Ideal for flexible schedules | - Less “untouched” feel  
- Popular snorkeling areas may get busy  
- Smaller village exploration area |
