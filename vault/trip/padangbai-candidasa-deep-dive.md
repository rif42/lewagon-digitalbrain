## 🗺️ Location Deep Dives

### 1. Padangbai — The Gateway to East Bali

A small, traditional fishing port town on Bali's east coast. It's the main ferry terminal for Lombok and the Gili Islands, but the town itself has a relaxed backpacker vibe with a beautiful curve of beach, colourful outrigger boats, and a handful of excellent warungs.

**Why stop here:**
- **Blue Lagoon & Tanjung Jepun** — the best snorkeling on the east coast (see below)
- **Ozone Café** — iconic Padangbai breakfast/lunch spot right on the beach
- **Traditional boat harbour** — great photo ops at sunrise/sunset
- **Small-town charm** — no big resorts, just local life

**Good to know:** Padangbai is compact. Everything is walkable. You'll park your vehicles near the harbour entrance (parking Rp 5-10k per vehicle) and walk to the snorkeling meeting point. If someone has a car, agree on a meetup point beforehand — cell signal is fine in town.

**Maps:** [Padangbai town](https://maps.app.goo.gl/2mF1MxqDJCpu3ot79)

### 2. 🐠 Blue Lagoon & Tanjung Jepun — Snorkeling Paradise

The star attraction. Contrary to the name, Blue Lagoon is a **small white-sand bay** with calm, crystal-clear water — not a resort pool. It's widely considered the best **beginner-friendly snorkeling** on Bali's east coast.

#### Marine Life You'll See

| Species | Frequency | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Clownfish (Nemo!) | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Dozens of anemone clusters |
| Parrotfish | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Bright blue/green, easy to spot |
| Butterflyfish | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Multiple varieties |
| Moray eels | ⭐⭐⭐ | Peeking from rocks (guide points them out) |
| Sea turtles | ⭐⭐ | Occasional — best early morning |
| Blue starfish | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | On the sandy patches |
| Pufferfish | ⭐⭐⭐ | Hides in crevices |
| Coral varieties | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Brain, staghorn, fan corals alive and healthy |

**Tanjung Jepun** (the adjacent bay, just a 5-min swim/boat ride away) has deeper water and larger coral formations — more large fish, occasional reef sharks (harmless), and better visibility in the afternoon.

#### Best Conditions

| Factor | Ideal | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Time of day | **08:00-11:00** (clearest), **11:00-12:30** (still good) | Your group goes 11:00-12:30 — still decent visibility, and the sun is high enough for great underwater photos. Trade-off: slightly more boat traffic |
| Tide | Mid-to-high | Lower visibility at low tide |
| Season | **Apr-Oct** (dry) | Calm seas. Nov-Mar (rainy) still good — just check wind |
| Water temp | 26-30°C year-round | No wetsuit needed (long-sleeve rash guard enough) |

### 3. Candidasa — Coastal Relaxation Base

A quieter, more relaxed coastal town 25 minutes north of Padangbai. It's a narrow strip of beachfront hotels, villas, and restaurants along the main road. The beach itself has dark volcanic sand and is lined with traditional fishing boats.

**Why stay here:**
- Close to Padangbai (25 min) for the snorkeling morning
- Close to Virgin Beach (15 min south)
- Quieter than Padangbai at night — better for group chill time
- More villa options at good prices

**Note:** The main Candidasa beach isn't swimming-friendly (rocky in places, some current). The swimming happens at Virgin Beach (next section).

**Maps:** [Candidasa area](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pU5hzT5HVEjHnJpo6)

### 4. 🏖️ Virgin Beach (White Sand Beach) — The Hidden Gem

Secret name: **Pasir Putih** ("White Sand"). It's a hidden bay about 15 minutes south of Candidasa, tucked behind a hill. This is where you actually swim — soft white sand, gentle waves, crystal water.

**Why it's special:**
- Secluded feel — limited warungs, no big resorts
- Safe swimming — very gentle waves, shallow for 30+ metres
- Photo gold — turquoise water against white sand, framed by green cliffs
- Cheap drinks — fresh coconut Rp 30-50k, Bintang Rp 30-40k

**Facilities:** Basic warungs (food, drinks, loungers), parking Rp 5-10k for scooter, Rp 20k for car.

**Pro tip:** Go late afternoon (16:00+) — the light is golden, the crowd thins out, and it's still bright until 18:00.

**Maps:** [Virgin Beach](https://maps.app.goo.gl/AKBrdMBYNMuPfmUZ6)

### 5. 🏘️ Tenganan Village — Bali's Oldest Village

One of three **Bali Aga** (original Balinese) villages left in Bali. Tenganan has preserved its pre-Majapahit culture, unique architecture, and traditions. It's not a tourist show — it's a living village.

**What to see & do:**

| Activity | Cost | Duration |
|----------|------|----------|
| Walk through the village | Free (donation 50-100k) | 30-45 min |
| Watch **geringsing** weaving (double-ikat textile) | Free to watch | 10-15 min |
| Buy authentic geringsing fabric | Rp 300k-2mil | — |
| Visit the village temple | Included | 10 min |
| See traditional houses (all identical layout) | Included | Ongoing |
| **Mekare-kare** festival (when scheduled) | Free | June/July only |

**Cultural notes:**
- Tenganan has its own dialect, traditions, and even a different calendar
- They're the only village that makes **geringsing** (ceremonial double-ikat textile)
- No photography inside the temple, but fine everywhere else
- Donation goes to the village cooperative (fair)

**Pro tip:** Go early morning (09:00-10:00) before the tour buses arrive around 11:00.

**Maps:** [Tenganan Village](https://maps.app.goo.gl/BEr1mxr58FV1rPqj9)

### 6. 🍽️ Bali Asli Restaurant — The Lunch With A View

The perfect lunch stop before heading back to Canggu. Perched on a hillside overlooking **rice terraces with Mount Agung** (Bali's highest volcano) as the backdrop. It's genuinely one of the most stunning restaurant settings in Bali.

**The Experience:**
- Authentic Balinese cuisine, family-style or individual plates
- The menu changes seasonally based on local harvest
- Cooking classes available (with village tour) — Rp 300-400k/pax, 3-4 hours
- Herbal drink tasting included if you ask

**Cost:** Rp 100-150k/pax for lunch (mains + drink)
**Opening hours:** 08:00-17:00 daily
**Maps:** [Bali Asli Restaurant](https://maps.app.goo.gl/JuBqFgsizuZakNxd9)

**Pro tip:** Ask for a table on the **upper terrace** for the best Mt Agung + rice terrace photo angle. Arrive by 11:30 to beat the lunch crowd.

## 🏡 Accommodation: Bloo Lagoon Eco Village

No riding after snorkeling — Bloo Lagoon is perched on the hill **right above Blue Lagoon**. From your villa you can see the bay you just snorkeled in.

### Why Bloo Lagoon

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Location** | Hillside overlooking Blue Lagoon bay |
| **Distance from snorkeling** | 5 min shuttle (free) or short walk down |
| **Villa types** | 1BR, 2BR, 3BR villas (all unique, open-concept) |
| **Pool** | Resort pool with slide, lap area, shallow zone |
| **Restaurant** | On-site — farm-to-table, Indonesian & international, room service available |
| **Breakfast** | Included buffet (continental, veg/vegan options) |
| **Free shuttle** | 24-hr shuttle to Padangbai town, Blue Lagoon beach, Bias Tugel beach |
| **Spa** | Spiral Spa — massages, treatments with ocean views |
| **Yoga** | Daily morning yoga (07:30) overlooking the sea |
| **Parking** | Free on-site |
| **Rating** | 9.0 Booking / 4.4 TripAdvisor (#2 of 8 in Padangbai) |

### How to Fit 8 People

Bloo Lagoon doesn't have a single 4BR villa. For 8 people, the best setup is:

| Option | Setup | Est. Total/night | Notes |
|--------|-------|-----------------|-------|
| **A. 3BR Villa + 1BR Villa** | 6 in 3BR (3 queen beds + sofa bed), 2 in 1BR | ~$274-322 | You get two separate units but the resort pool & restaurant are the common space |
| **B. 3BR Villa (tight)** | 6 on beds + 2 on sofa bed in living area | ~$150-198 | Tight for 8 — depends how close the group is 😄 |
| **C. 2BR Villa + 2BR Villa** | 4+4 in two units | ~$290-350 | Similar to A |

**Recommendation:** **Option A** (3BR + 1BR) gives everyone a proper bed and fresh towels, while keeping the group close by at the same resort.

### What Bloo Lagoon Villas Are Like

- **Open-concept living** — indoor-outdoor design, partial open walls. You're immersed in nature (geckos, birds, frogs — it's an eco resort)
- **Living area** — sofa, coffee table, small kitchenette. Fine for card games and hanging out
- **AC** — bedrooms have AC; living areas may be fan-only
- **Views** — sweeping sea, coastline, Mt Agung on clear days
- **Villas vary** — each of the 25 is owner-designed, so layouts differ. Bookings can request specific villa numbers

**Maps:** [Bloo Lagoon Eco Village](https://maps.app.goo.gl/a1KJrRDTamRNWNpN8)

## 🏍️🚗 Self-Drive Guide

### Vehicle Plan

Your group has 8 people with a mix of motorcycles and cars. Plan the convoy:

| Vehicle | Capacity | Best for | Fuel est. (round trip) |
|---------|----------|----------|----------------------|
| **Motorcycle (x2-3)** | 1-2 pax each | Riders who enjoy the coastal road | Rp 100-150k/motor |
| **Car (x1-2)** | 4-5 pax each | Groups who want AC + music + shared ride | Rp 200-300k/car |

**Recommended formation:** 2-3 motorcycles + 1-2 cars. Mix it up so everyone has options.

### Route

**Day 1 (Canggu → Padangbai):**
```
Canggu → (Jl. Raya Canggu) → (Bypass Ngurah Rai) → Sanur → (Jl. Prof. Dr. Ida Bagus Mantra) → Kusamba → Padangbai (65 km, ~2 hrs)
```

**Day 2 (Padangbai → Tenganan → Bali Asli → Canggu):**
```
Padangbai → Tenganan (5 km, 15 min) → Bali Asli (10 km, 20 min) → (same road back) → Canggu (70 km, ~2.5 hrs)
```

**Maps:** [Route Canggu → Padangbai](https://maps.app.goo.gl/fXz4BPpJxZK8LQTu5)

### Convoy Tips

| # | Tip |
|---|-----|
| 1 | **Share live location** on WhatsApp for the whole trip duration |
| 2 | **Assign a lead + sweep** — one person leads (knows the route), one person stays at the back (ensures nobody gets lost) |
| 3 | **Waypoint meetups** — don't try to stay bumper-to-bumper. Set waypoints: (1) Sanur McDonald's, (2) Kusamba viewpoint, (3) Padangbai harbour |
| 4 | **Fuel up before departure** — there are SPBUs along the route (Sanur, Kusamba, Padangbai) but fill up in Canggu for peace of mind |

## 🌦️ Seasonal Considerations

November-March is **rainy season** in Bali. Here's how it affects this trip:

| Factor | Impact | Mitigation |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Snorkeling visibility** | Slightly lower after rain — still 10-15m vis | Go early morning (clearest) |
| **Rain showers** | Usually 1-2 hours afternoon, not all day | Plan outdoor activities in the morning |
| **Road conditions** | Good — main roads are paved. Drive carefully on wet roads | Go slower in rain, allow 15-30 min extra, and motorcycles be extra cautious |
| **Bloo Lagoon views** | Mt Agung may be cloudy, still beautiful | Sunrise is best bet for clear views |
| **Temperature** | 27-32°C — same as dry season | Same packing, just add rain jacket |
| **Crowds** | Lower! Fewer tourists | Bonus — more space everywhere |

**Verdict:** Totally fine to go in rainy season. The snorkeling is still excellent, prices are lower, and crowds are thinner. Just bring a rain jacket for the 1-hour afternoon shower.

### Why Choose Padangbai Over Other Options

| Compare to | Padangbai Wins On | Other Option Wins On |

### Best For This Group If...

- ✅ You want a **zero-driving-after-arrival** trip (snorkel → check in → done)
- ✅ You want **beginner-friendly snorkeling** that everyone can enjoy
- ✅ You want to stay somewhere with a view over the actual Blue Lagoon bay
- ✅ A resort-style stay with pool, restaurant, spa, yoga appeals to the group
- ✅ Budget is around **Rp 1.3-1.5mil** (mid-range with breakfast included)
- ✅ You want a **new Balinese experience** (east coast is different from Canggu)

- ❌ **Group hangout space is critical** — you're in separate villa units at Bloo Lagoon, not one big house. The resort pool/restaurant is the common area
- ❌ Someone has motion sickness (snorkeling boat + winding coastal roads)
- ❌ The group prefers active adventures over resort relaxation
