## Summary
> [!note] 
> Explain the problem in one sentence. ***Don’t think/talk about a solution ❌***​
Currently, when a GO is pushed less than two weeks before a batch starts, Admissions Managers must manually post it in Slack ( in
• #online-support-admissions-sales ) to notify the Ops team, a process that is error-prone and hard to scale.
## Business Impact
> [!note] 
> Explain business impact of your issue
Delays in communication with late GOs affect student readiness and sending messages burden the Admissions team during a busy period.

## Context & Problem
> [!note] 
> Explain in great details the context surrounding this issue
<details><summary>**Suggested Checklist**</summary>
</details>
Two weeks before the start of a batch, the Ops team begins sending essential information to incoming students:
- For all: onboarding email, Back to School, reminder about Welcome Meet Up
        -
- For DS: Introduction Week invitation
        -
- For GM: Gmail account request
        -
When a GO is pushed after this 2-week window, timely communication becomes urgent. Currently, we rely on Admissions Managers to copy-paste each late GO into a shared Slack channel. However:
- The message is always the same
        -
- Managers may forget or not be able to post right away due to interviews or workload
        -
- It's not scalable with the volume of batches in Q4
        -
We would like GOs to be automatically pushed to a dedicated Slack channel when they are added to Online batches, including the batch number. This would reduce dependency on Admissions, ensure no students are missed, and save time across teams.
