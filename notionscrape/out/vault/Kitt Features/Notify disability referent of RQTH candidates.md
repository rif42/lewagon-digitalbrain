# Notify disability referent of RQTH candidates
## Context
Currently, there is no systematic way to notify the disability referent when a candidate in France indicates an RQTH status (via tag or flag), which can result in missed legal compliance actions.
@Florine Humbert and @Cybil Lechiguero have been working on this process — [No access](https://app.notion.com/p/2478924af01480679c32f5ae53cff6ac?pvs=24#2478924af01480679c32f5ae53cff6ac) — to follow up candidates with disabilities. However, this may not be enough to ensure compliance with French legislation, as we need verifiable proof that we are tracking people with special needs.
## Solution
To ensure proper follow-up of candidates with disabilities and provide verifiable proof of tracking, a dedicated Slack channel ([#rqth-france](https://teamwagon.slack.com/archives/C09PX1618LA)**)** has been created.
**Slack notifications**
Two notifications will now be sent to this channel:
1. When the Disability flag is added to a candidate
