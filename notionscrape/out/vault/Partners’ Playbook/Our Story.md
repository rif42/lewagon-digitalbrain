# Our Story
Read on for a whistle-stop tour of the journey of Le Wagon so far! 🚗​
# 2013
Back in 2013, our founders [Boris](https://www.linkedin.com/in/boris-paillard-86722670/), [Seb](https://www.linkedin.com/in/saunier/) & [Romain](https://www.linkedin.com/in/romain-paillard-27575711/) decide to launch a coding school to teach creatives and entrepreneurs how to code their own tech products through an intensive 9-week Bootcamp.
They felt that traditional university degree courses were failing to provide students with the right tech skills — that “**higher education has missed the train”**. They didn’t want a name using words like “hack”, “code”, “tech” or “app” — as for us, **code is more than that**, it’s a means to develop products, solve problems and reach goals. And a wagon is a vehicle that takes a group of people (like a class of students) **on a journey to a destination**.
And so, Le Wagon was born! *Read more about building the Le Wagon brand *[here](https://medium.com/le-wagon/from-bootstrapping-to-building-a-brand-that-scales-26b0eda92ddb)
# 2014
On 6 January 2014, our first ever bootcamp kicks off in Paris with 15 students: Batch #1!
Our founders literally helped build the office (bootstrap mode on 💪 ) and Seb and Boris had to change rooms several times per week for the first batch 😬​
# 2015
In 2015, Le Wagon expands by opening campuses in Brussels, Bordeaux, Lille and Beirut.
# 2016
By the end of 2016, Le Wagon has opened campuses in Amsterdam, Marseille, Lisbon, Nantes, Copenhagen, São Paulo, London, Barcelona, Lyon and Rio de Janeiro.
# 2017
Come 2017, Le Wagon expands to Montreal, Shanghai, Berlin, Tokyo, Chengdu, Bali, Sydney, Belo Horizonte, Milan, Melbourne and Casablanca.
We are voted the **Best Coding Bootcamp **on CourseReport for the first time (spoiler, we also get voted this every following year 😉)

# **2018**
In 2018, we adapt our proven teaching methods to help forward-thinking enterprises hire, train and retain diverse tech talents by launching **Le Wagon for Business**. Le Wagon has worked with 50+ of the most respected global companies, including IKEA, Total, LVMH and Société Générale, transforming their workforces worldwide.
Don’t forget that campus counter! We also open in Buenos Aires, Mexico, Tel Aviv, Kyoto, Lausanne, Shenzhen this year.
# **2019**
We launch a new **Part-Time Bootcamp** so people can learn alongside jobs, education or other commitments.
We also reach the **5000 alumni **milestone this year! 🤩
Cities opened: Oslo, Rennes, Madrid, Singapore, Stockholm, Seoul, Istanbul, Munich
# **2020**
We were busy in 2020!
We **secured a funding round** led by AfricInvest & Cathay Capital. We raise funds for the first time after 7 years of bootstrapping in order to consolidate our group, by bringing several franchises into the core group.
After reaching 5000 alumni last year, we double it in one year 😱 by **reaching 10,000 alumni**!
We launch our second bootcamp: **Data Science**.
Cities opened: Dubai, Brasilia
Le Wagon moves from on campus only to **online learning** as the world changes due to the global pandemic. We have 600 attendees at our first ever online workshop for beginners!
# **2021**
Le Wagon **partners with Honoris Universities** to run training across Africa!
We launch **Part-Time Data Science Bootcamps!**
Online Learning becomes a permanent part of our offering with **Flex Online Bootcamps!**
Cities opened this year: Santiago, Mauritius, Nice, Cologne, Belize, Lima!
We have some iconic out of home marketing campaigns, including a red bus in London!
# **2022**
- [ ] We complete our first acquisition ([Emil](https://teamwagon.slack.com/archives/GA0DWF4TB/p1655918064405539) - read about it [here](https://blog.lewagon.com/news/acquisition-emil/)!) and launch Skills Courses, shorter online courses!
- [ ] We launch a new bootcamp: **Data Analytics!**
- [ ] We reach the 15 000 alumni milestone!
- [ ] The number of startups founded by our alumni to have raised funds reaches 100, now totalling $800M+.
- [ ] Cities opened: Zurich, Medellin, Porto, Cape Town
- [ ] We refresh our branding this year, and our logo goes from 🔴 to 🟥!
# 2023
We reach 20,000 alumni!
We launch another bootcamp: **Data Engineering!**
# 2024
Le Wagon celebrates **10 years**! 🎉​
We launch our latest Bootcamp: **Growth Marketing!**
Startups founded by our alumni have raised funds totalling $1B+!
We announce several leadership changes as we head into our next chapter: Virginie (new CEO), Munique (new COO), Jerome (new CMO), Edward (new CTO)!
# 2025
*Cruising* ⛵
We launched a new Partnership in India 🇮🇳
*That brings us to now! How are you going to shape our journey? *🤘​
