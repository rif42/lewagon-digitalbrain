Students currently receive the NPS form via Slack at the end of their bootcamp. However, many stop checking Slack regularly after graduation, leading to missed responses. Since Kitt remains a reference platform for students post-bootcamp displaying a reminder or access to the NPS form directly on Kitt (e.g., via the notification bell or banner) would ensure greater visibility and higher response rates.
