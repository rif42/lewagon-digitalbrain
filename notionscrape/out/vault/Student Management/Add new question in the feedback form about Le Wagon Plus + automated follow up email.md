# Add new question in the feedback form about Le Wagon Plus + automated follow up email
## Summary
> [!note] 
> TODO: Explain the problem in one sentence. ***Don’t think/talk about a solution ❌***​
We currently don’t have a way to identify interested alumni through the feedback form and automatically follow up with relevant information and an invitation to Le Wagon Plus.
## Business Impact
> [!note] 
> TODO : Explain business impact of your issue.
Without the ability to capture alumni interest and have an automated follow-up, we are unable to:
- Roll out Le Wagon Plus in a controlled and scalable way, especially given the limited number of available seats
- Ensure that only interested alumni receive detailed communications about the platform
- Increase the probability that alumni who enter the platform are genuinely motivated to engage and participate actively
## Context & Problem
> [!note] 
> TODO: Explain in great details the context surrounding this issue
Le Wagon Plus is our new alumni community platform that is currently being prepared for launch.
After finishing the bootcamp, alumni complete a feedback form where they reflect on their experience and think about what comes next with Le Wagon. The form already includes **Question 5**, which captures alumni expectations after graduation (and actually most alumni mention wanting to stay connected with the community and networking.)
Adding a question related to Le Wagon Plus right after this fits naturally and helps us capture alumni interest at the right moment. The proposed question is:
**“Would you be interested in finding out more about Le Wagon Plus, our new alumni community platform?” **
This would help identify alumni who want to learn more about the platform and trigger an **automated follow-up email** (sent to those who answer “yes”) with more details and instructions on how to request access or download the app.
This extra step helps us target communication better, invite only interested alumni, and build a more engaged early community for Le Wagon Plus.
