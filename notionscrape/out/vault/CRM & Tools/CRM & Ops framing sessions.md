### Who's Taking the Lead in Each Region?
In each corner of our world, it's the Marketing Leads who've got the ball. They're on a mission to scoop up all the CRM wishes and wants from their turf. (Feel to organize the needs collection.) You can share this notion but you will stay the owner of your region. Only you will be invite to the session during the phase II
# 🛞 How it works?
Each quarter, the CRM team will organise a **framing session** which takes place in three stages:
1. ***Phase I :*** Needs collection
⏱️ Duration: 3 weeks
✍️ Description: Based on notion document, each region will have 1 month to fill this one (projects, features needs, big bug to fix etc.)
When ? Mid-week on the Second month (in a quarter)
1. ***Phase II:*** Week session
⏱️ Duration: 1 week
✍️ Description: 45mn max in visio with each each region (marketing lead) to discuss on each needs.
When ? 2nd week of the Second Month
1. ***Phase III***: Post-session Review
⏱️ Duration: 1 week
✍️ Description: Cross-referencing the demands and needs of each region. Prioritization. Planning. (Note: Based our Bandwidth , some needs will be re-reschedule in next quarter)
When ? The third week in the second Month
