# Mini Jobber Calculations
## Vocabulary
---
**current year**** **- running year which resets each 12 months after the starting date of main contract
**freelance amount**** - **the value equivalent to the freelance rates (i.e. 100eur for TA day)
**gross amount** - the value applicable for the MiniJob (75% [ mini ] or 80% [ midi ] of the freelance amount)
**delta**** **- the remaining amount after the last payslip (either calculated for a current month or as total)
**danger zone** - salary range in which the taxes are unfavorable for the Midi Jobber (looking at individual month)
## Contract overview
---
On the [Mini Jobber page](https://kitt.lewagon.com/alumni/ninahackenbroich/mini_jobber) we display the following values:
- status of the contract (mini or midi)
- current year paid payslips (gross)
- open balance until the end of the contract (gross)

You will see the change link below the badge if the teacher **is a mini jobber **according to current calculations. If you know that they will have more days scheduled in the future and would like to already change the monthly limit for them, you can change it do a midi job.
In case you want to revert it, clicking on change will switch the contract status to mini.
Both changes affect the upcoming payslip, all the paid payslips remain intact.
