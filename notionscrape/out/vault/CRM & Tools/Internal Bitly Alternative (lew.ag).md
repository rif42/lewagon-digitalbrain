# Internal Bitly Alternative (lew.ag)
## Context
Le Wagon’s Marketing department (and sometimes Ops & other departments) have been relying on [![](https://docrdsfx76ssb.cloudfront.net/static/1739300982/pages/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/favicon.ico)BitlyBitly Connections Platform | Short URLs, QR Codes, and More](https://bitly.com/) to generate short links using the great domain lew.ag
Usage of this tool is quite small though, with very limited traffic and number of links created / month. Having a cool vanity domain linked to our account means that we can’t rely on the Bitly free plan, and the first plan is quite pricey.
## Solution
Engineering has built a small Rails app to replace Bitly internally, allowing any Staff members to login with their Kitt credentials and start generating links, QR codes, and track statistics about the traffic they get (clicks / scans)
Try it now! 👉 [https://lew.ag](https://lew.ag/)
> [!note] 
> ~3k links and ~100 QR codes have been successfully imported from the current Bitly account, ensuring continuity of URLs.
