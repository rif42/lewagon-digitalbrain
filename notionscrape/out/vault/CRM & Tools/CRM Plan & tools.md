# CRM Plan & tools
> [!note] 
> Le Wagon's Customer Relationship Management (CRM) strategy represents a comprehensive, data-driven approach to managing the entire student journey—from initial interest to alumni advocacy. As a leading coding bootcamp with a global presence, Le Wagon has developed a sophisticated CRM ecosystem that combines cutting-edge technology, AI-powered automation, and personalized nurturing to optimize conversion rates and enhance student experience.
### **Strategic Context**
Our CRM plan is built on the foundation of understanding that modern education marketing requires a multi-touch, multi-channel approach. With the competitive landscape of coding bootcamps intensifying and student expectations evolving, Le Wagon's CRM strategy focuses on three core pillars:
1. **Intelligent Lead Management**: Leveraging AI, prediction and automation to qualify, priorise, and nurture leads more effectively
1. **Personalized lead Journey**: Creating tailored experiences based on persona, intent, and engagement level
1. **Data-Driven Optimization**: Using comprehensive metrics and analytics to continuously improve conversion rates and student outcomes
### **Team Structure & Expertise**
The CRM & Ops Squad consists of four specialized professionals:
- **Strategic Leadership**: Overall CRM strategy and cross-team collaboration
- **Campaign Management**: Global campaign execution and regional coordination
- **Technical Operations**: HubSpot optimization and data management
- **Growth Innovation**: New channel development and creative acquisition/activation strategies

**Main Metrics Followed**
The CRM performance is tracked through a comprehensive set of metrics categorized by their impact on the business, campaign effectiveness, and project-specific KPIs.
- **CRM Impact over the Business:**
        - **Overall Application Conversion Rate (Lead to Apply):** Percentage of leads that complete an application.
        - **Volume of Applications with Last Direct Click Email:** Number of applications where the last interaction was an email click.
        - **Referral Program:** Proportion of total applications originating from referrals.
- **Campaign Impact:**
        - **MQL to SQL Conversion Rate:** Percentage of MQLs that become SQLs.
        - **SQL to Application Conversion Rate:** Percentage of SQLs that submit an application.
- **Campaign Efficiency:**
        - **Email Deliverability, Unsubscribe,Open rate, click rate, CTR, and Coverage Rates:** Standard email marketing performance metrics.
        - **Engagement Rates by Persona/Segment:** Email interaction rates for specific user groups.
- **Project KPIs:**
        - **Lead Scoring Effectiveness / lead priorisation:** Accuracy of the lead scoring model in predicting qualified leads.
        - **Lead Qualification Rate:** Percentage of leads meeting qualification criteria.

**Incentives: 'Tech and AI Fluency Fund'**
The 'Tech and AI Fluency Fund' is a key incentive program designed to support individuals from underrepresented communities by offering partial funding for bootcamp courses.
- **Purpose:** To provide financial assistance (up to 50% of the course fee) to attract and support diverse talent.
- **Target Groups:** The fund is available to individuals who identify as female, are from underrepresented minorities, have disabilities, are part of underrepresented communities in tech (LGBTQIA+, single parents, refugees), are career changers over 45, or are young professionals under 25.
- **Eligibility:** Applicants must be applying for a bootcamp in a European country where a Le Wagon campus is present, and they are reviewed based on their motivation and technical quiz results.

# **3. Lead Journey**
**Entry Points**
Leads enter the CRM system through various channels, which are primarily managed through forms for different offerings.
- **Syllabus Downloads:** Forms for downloading syllabi for various bootcamps and skill courses.
- **Job Reports:** Forms for accessing job reports.
- **Application Forms:** Direct application forms for courses.
- **Partnerships, Influencers, and Scholarships:** Specific contact and application forms for these programs.
- **Events:** Registration for events such as workshops and webinars.
- Ads with form
- …
**
Nurturing Workflow**
Once a lead enters the system, they are guided through a structured nurturing process designed to move them toward application and enrollment.
1. **Initial Engagement:** Leads are captured through one of the entry points and their data is collected.
1. **Qualification:** Leads are qualified based on their engagement and profile data, moving from MQL to PQL and eventually SQL.
1. **Segmentation:** Leads are segmented based on their persona and interests to deliver targeted content and campaigns.
1. **Automated Nurturing:** Leads receive automated email sequences, including post-event follow-ups and promotional content.
1. **Multi-Touch Campaigns:** the strategy is to engage leads across multiple channels, including popup/sms, to provide a more comprehensive nurturing experience.
1. **Sales Engagement:** SQLs are engaged by the sales team for direct follow-up and to move them through the sales pipeline.
1. **Conversion:** The ultimate goal of the nurturing process is to convert leads into enrolled students.
1. **Post-Conversion:** After enrollment, the focus shifts to retention and turning alumni into evangelists for the brand, who can then contribute to the referral program or tu make upsell with skill course or other new product like intro course AI etc.
