# Overview - AI Product Builder
### 🚀 **AI Product Builder Course overview**
**📌 Summary**
This program is built for technical product managers, web developers, data analysts, and entrepreneurs who want to build and ship AI-powered products at modern speed.
They learn the modern AI development stack, from low-code workflows to AI-assisted coding.
---
### 📅 **Duration & format**
- **Length:** 5 days (full-time)
- **Format:** Online
- **Schedule:** Weekdays, immersive & hands-on
---
### 🧠 **Prepwork (optional)**
- **Time:** 10 hours
- **Topics Covered:**
        - UX/UI Fundamentals
        - APIs
        - Introduction to LLMs
        - Workflow and agents with n8n
---
### 📚 **Modules at a glance**
1. **Building AI Agents & Workflows with Low Code** – Learn to build AI workflows and conversational agents in hours using low-code platforms, without writing complex code from scratch.
1. **Your App Goes Live** – Build and deploy a complete, publicly accessible application with integrated AI features using modern deployment tools.
1. **AI-Assisted & Vibe Coding** – Learn AI-powered development environments to generate, debug, and iterate on full-stack applications 10x faster.
1. **AI Product Strategy** – Learn to frame and scope AI products strategically, from compliance and architecture decisions to feature prioritization.
1. **Build Your AI Product** – Design, build, and deploy your own AI-powered application from concept to demo, ready to show employers or investors.
---
### 🛠️ **Key tools & technologies**
> *At Le Wagon, we focus on giving you the mental models and transferable skills to pick up any tool. Tools are just a means to practice the hard skills and knowledge we teach. *
- **n8n** (automation) – Open-source workflow automation tool to connect apps and build AI pipelines
- **Supabase** (database) – Open-source backend platform with a real-time database, authentication, and storage
- **Softr** (no-code app builder) – Drag-and-drop platform to build client portals, internal tools, and web apps without writing code
- **Lovable** (vibe coding) – AI-powered app builder that generates full-stack applications from a simple text prompt
- **Google Antigravity** (AI code editor) – AI-native code editor built on VS Code that autocompletes, generates, and explains code in real time
---
### 🎯 **Who it’s for**
- **Technical PM/PO or entrepreneur** with automation or low-code experience who wants to prototype AI products faster.
- **Web developer** (frontend, backend, or full-stack) looking to expand into AI product development.
- **Data analyst or data scientist** who wants to build user-facing AI products, not just models.
**Prerequisites:** No formal prerequisites are required, but this course is designed for people currently working in tech who are comfortable with technical tools, enjoy problem-solving, and have a test-and-iterate mindset.
---
### 🎓 **Career outcomes**
> *This is a 5-day intensive course. While it won't lead to a career change on its own, it will give them highly valuable, practical skills to level up in their current role or projects.*
Please note that career services are not included in this program.
