# How to use Zoom for Lectures
---
### Signing in to Zoom
Before every session, you will receive a personal Slack message with the session details (lecture, time and batch #).
Clicking on these credentials will take you to a page in Kitt that will guide you through the Zoom sign in process.

### Creating Breakout Rooms
When it’s time to create them, simply click on Breakout Rooms from the toolbar and choose how many rooms to create, depending on the number of students. Assign them manually since you will have to add the students by level, according to [[How to use Kitt|their quiz results]].

### Recovering a lecture recorded to the Cloud
If the lecture was recorded to the Cloud, you can recover it from [zoom.us](http://zoom.us/)
In order to sign in, use the **same** credentials that were [[How to use Zoom for Lectures|sent to you]] and that you used to give the lecture you recorded to the Cloud.
When you’ve found your recording, click on ... then Download 👌​
