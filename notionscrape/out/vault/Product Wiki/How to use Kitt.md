# How to use Kitt
---
Kitt is an internal tool we’ve developed to ensure a great pedagogical experience for both our teachers and students. In Kitt, you’ll be able to access and prepare your lectures, and have a birds eye view of your students, their progress and their quiz results.
## Prepare the session
Before giving a class, it’s very important you get to know the lecture and prepare the challenges/exercices that your students will be tackling for the day. Once you’ve signed into Kitt, you will land on your dashboard where you can access your bootcamp’s resources.
A key resource is the Lectures 👇​
![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)
This is page where you will be able to access the lesson’s **slides **and **video**. Getting familiar with both formats will make sure that everything goes smoothly during your Zoom session with the students, as you will be going through the slides and video.
![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)
![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)
After the lecture, students will start working on their **challenges**. Make sure to be comfortable with the exercises to be able to help the students with any issues they might have 👌 As a teacher, you can access both the Instructions and the Solution 👇​

## Review Quiz Results
Before every session, students will take a quiz, that you have to review to help you lead the session efficiently and to get a sense of the topics students struggle the most on.
As a teacher, you have access to a preview of the quiz and the answers on Kitt. By clicking on View results, you will be redirected to a dashboard with a global overview of students (or to the details of one student’s submission by clicking on their name in the index) 👇​
![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)
![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)
You should use these quiz results to create the Zoom session’s **breakout rooms**! The goal is to group students by level to foster buddying, and to help you focus on the struggling students proactively 👌​
