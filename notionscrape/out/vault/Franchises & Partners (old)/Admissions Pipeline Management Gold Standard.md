# Admissions Pipeline Management Gold Standard
> [!note] 
> A clean pipeline has many advantages! Mainly:
- 1) It gives you a **clearer view of your real opportunities**, who to push and where to allocate your time;
- 2) It supports you **better predicting how many students** will be closed in the future;
- 3) It helps** other teammates cover for your opportunities** (e.g. holidays; analysis, etc.)
In that light, this document summarizes what is consider to be our** gold standard** in **managing pipelines**, including general principles and specific actions to help guide you through each step of the way 🏋️​
## 0. Structure of the document ⚙️
---
In this document, you will find some overall considerations on how to approach and review your pipeline 🙌​
In the pipeline management detailed section you will have, **for each section of your pipeline:**
> [!note] 
> ☑️ **Must-Dos** — **checklist of must-dos** needed to ensure your pipeline is managed efficiently
---
💡 **Tip: **Pipeline management at a really high level can be quite overwhelming at first, as it can seem very mechanic and a big time spend. At first, try to keep your pipeline as shown below at the **end of your week**. Save time to review and clean your pipeline and you’ll see that once this becomes routine, you’ll do it automatically every day!
> *Disclaimer: *Whenever you read an action such as "call" or "email" you can always use the KITT reminder function to set this to another date, keeping your review faster but maintaining the peace of mind that you haven't forgotten any lead 💆‍♀️​

1. **Document! ✍️**​
        - At each stage, make sure to **document the why’s**, i.e., the reasons why applicants took the decisions they did, to make sure that in future interactions you remember the applicants’ profile and history in full — e.g.: missed or cancelled the interview because of doctor’s appointment or didn’t get back to us on why; failed to complete the assessment within deadline, etc.
---
1. **Clean! 🧹**​
        - Ensure a **clean pipeline** by **closing processes as no-go** when applicants remain unresponsive after being contacted multiple times. This way your pipeline reflects real opportunities
        - Make sure to do a **deep clean** at the end of **each quarter**, either closing processes as no-gos or moving them to the upcoming quarter.

## 3. Pipeline Management — Detailed⏳
---
### 📨 Inbox
---
✍️ **Must-Dos**
- [ ] **After 24h or 48h** → **call** to insist to book an interview and ask if they have questions 📲​
- [ ] **After 48h** → attempt second **call** to push for interview booking 📲​
- [ ] **After 5 days** → do **last chance call** and send a last email reminder
- [ ] **After 10 days** → close student as **No-go** if there was no answer. Send an email to give a status update to your candidate.
---

✅ **Golden Rules**
- **Prioritize calling** leads to allow for more personalized communication and to foster commitment on their side
- If leads don’t pick-up, **leave a voicemail** so they know who tried contacted them and why
---
❌ **Dont’s**
- Don’t have people on first contact for **more than 10 days**
### 🤙 Interview
---
✍️ **Must-Dos**
- [ ] For leads who **missed or cancelled** interviews → **call** **within 24h** to know the reason why and to try to have them book another slot 📲
- [ ] During interviews → as a general rule, set an **assessment deadline** that is **max. 5 days** 🗓️​
---
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F11875f61-3082-433d-98af-dae83ccdbe7a%2Fiw.png?table=block&id=1a68924a-f014-8068-bedd-c8f6a2e536a6&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1080&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
✅ **Golden Rules**
- If you’re not able to connect with leads via Aircall, an alternative is to **send an email** with the **KITT direct link** to rebook an interview that can be found on the Google calendar invite
- During interviews, you may use **lack of seats** / need to complete **prep-work** to push for a short assessment deadline
---
❌ **Dont’s**
- When calling leads who missed or cancelled their interview, don’t leave the call **without booking a new interview slot **(unless the candidate has lost interest in proceeding with the application)
- **Avoid** at all costs using personal devices to reach out to applicants, for the following reasons:
        - Security & Data breach
        - Professionalism & consistent branding
        - Technical support & Device management
        - Control over data & interactions
        - Efficiency, consistency & standardization of procedures
### 💻 Assessment
---
✍️ **Must-Dos**
- [ ] For candidates who are **studying for assessment** with a deadline **>10 days** → **call** half-way through to ask how they are progressing and if they have any questions 📲​
---
- [ ] For candidates who **miss their assessment deadline** → **call** **within 24h** to know the reason why and to motivate the candidate to resume track, establishing a **new deadline** 📲​
---
- [ ] For applicants who have **delivered the assessment:**
---
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F4d8aca8a-3f6d-461b-9e95-bfe787fb89a9%2Fami.png?table=block&id=1a68924a-f014-8059-bed4-f00e0ea20aab&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1080&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
✅ **Golden Rules**
- Use the post-assessment delivery **debrief calls** to cover any **potential questions** on next steps (e.g.: contract, financing, etc.)
- Regardless of waiting for any last decisions (e.g.: financing), always make sure there’s a **contract on the table**
---
❌ **Dont’s**
- Don’t leave missed deadline calls **without establishing a new deadline** for delivering assessment
### 🤝 Contract
---
✍️ **Must-Dos**
- [ ] **All applicants have a clear 5-day deadline **for signing their contract 🖊️​
- [ ] For applicants who **missed the 5-day deadline** → **call** to know why they haven’t signed, clarify any remaining questions, and close a new firm deadline for signing 📲​
---
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F26f102b1-f98e-4fd3-9a1b-4043eb144c68%2Fcont.png?table=block&id=1a68924a-f014-80b6-812f-ea2184a091a1&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1080&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
✅ **Golden Rules**
- When contacting applicants make sure to maintain **availability for questions**
- If you have a fundings team / financing-heavy admissions process: twice a week, ask the team **to review each funding/financing file **for updates** **and document these updates
---
❌ **Dont’s**
- Don’t let the **deadline for signing the contract pass** without a reminder
- Don’t let applicants **waiting for weeks or months with no update or push **on their contract. Even if they ask for extra time, most likely they are benchmarking.
### 💰 Deposit
---
✍️ **Must-Dos**
- [ ] All applicants who have **received the invoice** **> 5 days ago** → **call** to follow-up on payment 📲​
---
### 🎉 GO
---
✍️ **Must-Dos**
- [ ] Check that leads have had a touchpoint after paying the deposit in the** past 15 days **📩​
✅ **Golden Rules**
- Make sure to send the invoice to applicants** within 24h** since **contract signing**
- Use **final pressure** with the **prep-work** as a way to get the deposit to you
- If it makes sense, use may also as a **final pressure** the last seats available → “urgency” argument to the applicant
---
✅ **Golden Rules**
- Send Welcome Guides; Events News or any other idea you think is worth it for your GOs to stay up-to-date!
## 4. Steps before moving a candidate to NoGo
---
### 📨 Inbox
---
✍️ **Must-Dos**
- [ ] **After 24h or 48h** → **call** to insist to book an interview and ask if they have questions 📲​
- [ ] **After 48h** → attempt second **call** to push for interview booking 📲​
- [ ] **After 5 days** → do **last chance call** and send a last email reminder
- [ ] **After 10 days** → close student as **No-go** if there was no answer. Send an email to give a status update to your candidate (template available when you move a student to NoGo on KITT).

### 🗣 Late for the Interview/interview missed
---
- [ ] **After 5 min the interview has started **
- [ ] **If the person didn’t show up for the interview
**→ send the “interview missed email” available on KITT (it will be “unlocked” 30 min after the start of the interview).
- [ ] **After 48h → **call to see if the person is available to reschedule the interview
- [ ] **After 5 days → **try a last call to reschedule the interview. If no response, send an sms/whats’app to see if the person if still interested in joining the bootcamp and if so, if she’s available in the next few days to reschedule the interview
- [ ] **After 5 days **
### ⏳ Interview cancelled
---
**Must-Dos**
- In this case, you will receive a notification in the #city admissions channel informing you that the candidate has cancelled the interview. You will also have the reason why the candidate cancelled the interview (other training, will reschedule later,…). Depending on the reason:
- [ ] The day itself or the following day you can send the **interview cancelled email **through KITT.
- [ ] **After 4 days → **if no response, try a first call to see if the person is available in the next days to reschedule the interview
- [ ] **After 5 days **do **last chance call**
- [ ] **After 5 days **
→ If no response, close student as **No-go** if there was no answer. Send an email to give a status update to your candidate.
### 📈 Assessment
---
**Must-Dos**
- When you send the assessment, the deadline will be written in your email so the candidate will know when to complete it
- 24 hours before the assessment is due, an automatic reminder will be sent to your candidate
- [ ] If the candidate didn’t complete the assessment on time, **call** your candidate to understand the reason why it wasn’t completed
- [ ] **After 3 days** call again
- [ ] **After 4 days** send an email to see if the person is still interested in joining the bootcamp
- [ ] **After 5 days **do **last chance call**
### 📄 Contract
---
**Must-Dos**
- When sending the contract, the candidate will have 5 days to sign the contract (it is written in your contract email)
- [ ] **After 5 days**, send an email to remind the candidate to sign the contract. You will find a template here ⬇️​
- [ ] **After 3 days, **try a first call to see if your candidate has any questions about the contract and remind the deadline to sign the contract
- [ ] **After 5 days, **try a second call
- [ ] **After 5 days **do **last chance call**
### 📄 Invoice
**Must-Dos (depending on the interaction/situation of the candidate)**
- When you send an invoice, make sure there is a clear deadline to pay the deposit or the full payment (usually within 3 or 5 days after receiving the invoice). It’s always a + to ask for the proof of payment so we can speed up the process and send the prepwork!
- [ ] **After 3-5 days **if no payment try to call and understand if needs help/assistance regarding the payment
- [ ] **After 3-5 days → **
- [ ] **After 5 days →** try a last call
- [ ] **After 5 days **
