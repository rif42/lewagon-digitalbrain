# Email signature
### How to update your generic email signature in Gmail
1. Compose a new message
1. Insert signature / Manage signatures / Create new
1. Create your signature:
**First name Name
**Your role - [Le Wagon](https://www.lewagon.com/)
Rated 4.98/5 by 20k+ graduates
Switchup | Coursereport
1. Color Switchup | Coursereport in red and add the url:
Switchup: <https://www.switchup.org/bootcamps/le-wagon>
Course Report: <https://www.coursereport.com/schools/le-wagon#/reviews>
1. Insert image and upload the [[Design Guidelines|Le Wagon logo]]
