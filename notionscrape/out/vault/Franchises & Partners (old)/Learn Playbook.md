# Learn Playbook
As a **city manager on Kitt**, you automatically have access to your city's workspace on Learn, where you can **create and manage batches** for your lead acquisition workshops, online courses with schools or corporate training sessions.
> [!note] 
> To access your city's workspace on Learn, go to [kitt.lewagon.com](http://kitt.lewagon.com/) and click on the **Sign in on Learn **link in the avatar menu 👇​

# City workspace
## Adding groups
In your city's workspace, your batches are organised by **groups**. A group is just a way to gather batches together. Your groups are only visible to you and your city's coworkers, they never appear to your students / external teachers.
To create a group just click on the **New group** button, and give it a name and an organisation (your external client or Le Wagon if it's for your own acquisition purpose). You can upload a picture too (if you don't, your group will have the organisation's picture by default).
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F46d9eccb-77e5-456d-8067-d2ce1fa601db%2FScreenshot_2020-05-12_at_10.59.29.png?table=block&id=6f33b3a1-d992-46dd-b466-23b0e5d7349b&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)

# Creating a workshop
*In this section, we assume you already scheduled your event*
## **General settings**
We recommend creating a new batch for each workshop you run. Give to the batch the same **name** as your event published on Meetup / Eventbrite / Livestorm. Use short names.
You need to add a **start date** and an **end date** (it can be the same value for workshops, dates are only used for sorting purposes in your dashboard).
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F080d9178-868f-46a6-862d-fc73b11c44a7%2FScreenshot_2020-05-12_at_11.32.07.png?table=block&id=c6442a60-4fe2-4d2d-97e4-56fe6db631f6&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
The **description** is optional, you can extract some parts of the description you used to promote your event and paste them here. It only appears on [learn.lewagon.com/dashboard](http://learn.lewagon.com/dashboard), which is the students' home page.
## **Enable the workshop option**
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fc64a3455-d931-4a43-9945-c0d04a94fa1c%2FScreenshot_2020-05-12_at_11.03.52.png?table=block&id=7a42f4db-a26d-4162-ac0b-d06900c9ae31&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Checking the **workshop** checkbox in the batch's settings will automatically add a *marketing* form to the attendees onboarding journey on Learn. This form is connected to **Hubspot.** That way, all workshop attendees end up in your city's contacts on **Hubspot**. For existing contacts, the information previously submitted (on Meetup, [lewagon.com](http://lewagon.com/), or Eventbrite) is fetched and prefills the form. When they submit the form to access the content, **it will enrich the contact on Hubspot** with (i) the time of submission and (ii) the name of the attended event.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F55ea5a62-f553-4336-ab98-a46d712a56ad%2FScreenshot_2020-05-12_at_14.45.30.png?table=block&id=1605dfba-f560-4357-a57c-7e2174df9045&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)

# B2B trainings
Learn offers [[Upskilling Programs|💪Upskilling ProgramsIn Trash]] for B2B. These trainings were tailored to last 5 days, onsite. Creating a B2B batch on Learn is really close to what is described for workshops, so we will only focus on the little differences in this section.
## Announcement wall
First, in the batch's settings, don't enable the Workshop options (obviously), as it hides completely the Classmates and Buddies section from the batch page (that you want to keep for B2B training sessions).
An onsite training (even performed remotely with the appropriate tools) requires having a communication channel. If you have a dedicated workspace on Slack, you can enable the option in your batch. It does not integrate Learn with your Slack account, it just lets you add your channel's url to add a link in Learn for students to open the channel from there. Since it is hard to enforce the use of Slack on short trainings (also companies may use microsoft teams or another tool), Learn offers an alternative: **the announcement wall**.

# Online courses
Learn offers [No access](https://app.notion.com/p/e7434aac550f4467beca29915da74f61?pvs=24#e7434aac550f4467beca29915da74f61), used for B2U mostly. It is more common to create batches with **several courses** in this context, as the same class (group of students) can follow the Web Development course during the first semester, and the UX UI Course during the next semester. In that case, you can add the UX UI Course in the batch, and it will give access to the course automatically.
## Forum
In this case, we don't recommend activating the wall. We recommend setting up a **Forum** instead, which is more suited to provide online support, and can still be used for announcements. The forum can be setup in the batch's settings, **at the course's level.**
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Feaa7e410-bfa2-475c-bb24-ebc2b311e4c0%2FScreenshot_2020-05-15_at_21.20.35.png?table=block&id=d8cdc14c-1e44-41fd-adc2-c34d5b25b847&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F32e90778-3725-4517-81b9-016a00f74884%2FScreenshot_2020-05-15_at_21.28.35.png?table=block&id=05cc01e7-55ed-4007-b611-39c19949dd48&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
By default, students and teachers are notified by email each time a new question is posted on the forum. In large classes, it can be noisy for students as they don't necessarily have the same questions. In order to limit the notifications to the staff, you can uncheck the **Notify students** options in the settings, in the **Courses** section.

## Students onboarding
For B2U batches, you will most likely have to invite students with their school's email addresses. It is not recommended to use the token nor the url invitation methods in that case. Ask the school to send you a spreadsheet with the students emails (+ first names and last names if possible), and use the **Invite students from spreadsheet** onboarding method.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fc0de8446-9711-4cdb-bd62-663d3dc552d5%2FScreen_Shot_2021-03-29_at_14.58.53.png?table=block&id=7671f9b0-9b29-44cc-ac20-8f81e8f6ea72&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
They will receive a personal invitation link by email to sign up on Learn an enroll in the batch.
## Projects
In online courses, there are projects blocks which are key if you have to grade them. The projects blocks are labelled with a 🚀 in the navigation, and end with a form where students submit their work. They can submit a **url** or a **file**, depending on the assignment.
You can find all submitted projects by clicking in the corresponding link in the navbar, which appears as soon as 1 project is submitted:
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F116921ca-9cca-41e1-bce5-51536fed05c6%2FScreenshot_2020-05-19_at_12.04.05.png?table=block&id=1989302e-bfcb-4f60-a022-bda2c12e48ff&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
## Progress
Last, you can check students progress in the **Activity** page where you will see **all completed blocks** and quizzes scores. At this point, the completion is based on the following logic, for each type of block:
- A **Project** was submitted
- A **Quizz** was done
- A **Github** challenge was pushed
- (*) A **Text** was opened for a certain time proportional to its length (200 characters / minute)
- (*) A **Video** was played until the last second
- (*) A **Slide Deck** was seen until the last slide
*(*) For read-only blocks, we expect to change the logic soon and replace it with a declarative ****Mark as done**** call-to-action.*
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fef89df31-48d2-46bd-b900-da5280d51184%2FScreenshot_2020-05-19_at_12.13.13.png?table=block&id=9282393c-32c4-4c6d-afea-6ee39bfb7587&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
**Grading**
From this page, you will also be able to give a grade to each student, **that won't be visible** to her/him (internal purpose only). Just click on the Grade button to open the grading modal, and submit your grade based on the Quizzes / Project you can consult from there:
