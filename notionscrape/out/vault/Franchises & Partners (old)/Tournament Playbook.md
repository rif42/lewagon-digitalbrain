# Tournament Playbook
> [!note] 
> It’s a feature for *Events and Community Managers* to host a fun code competition on Kitt that could be used either way remotely on Zoom or on campus.
# Demo
In the following video, you will see how to **setup** and **host** a coding game on [tournament.lewagon.com](https://tournament.lewagon.com/)
> [!video] 
# Step-by-step tutorial
Start by going to [tournament.lewagon.com](http://tournament.lewagon.co/) to authenticate yourself with Kitt.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F3d4d988a-31c9-412f-8d80-68cdf730f0e4%2FScreenshot_2020-05-18_22.53.14.png?table=block&id=ba0c06a4-2abc-44c9-a88b-42490d43c7cd&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=930&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Click on New Game to create your tournament: you'll need a name, the date of the event, and you will be able to select the 5 challenges your game will feature.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fee6218c2-0ab6-4636-a80c-76f3410bb748%2FScreenshot_2020-08-31_at_17.16.39.png?table=block&id=0bbcff52-c396-46b0-8ac9-a6a9c12c314c&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=930&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
You can now click on Copy Invitation Link and share it with your students on Slack/Zoom. They will reach the registration screen and be able to choose if they want to Join as a player or do nothing and follow the event as a spectator. As a host, you'll see students registering in real time and you'll be able to close registrations when needed.
> [!note] 
> Students can choose to play in **Ruby** or **Python**, feel free to mix your batches
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fccd910d5-bd27-4456-8886-a76d40f76088%2FUntitled.png?table=block&id=28dfbec9-5c45-4ff8-9a26-0683ed8d1360&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=640&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)

![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fd6789ad5-a380-4749-acc9-f9c7c038f680%2FScreenshot_2020-05-18_23.09.01.png?table=block&id=799f594f-c8f8-452e-b581-4c538d557766&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=640&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
When starting the first round, players' screens will showcase the first coding challenge with the instructions on the left and the code editor on the right. They'll be able to run as many attempts as they can until the challenge is completed.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F45036daf-339e-47b8-9a58-6cf842c197e4%2FUntitled.png?table=block&id=5dc7ecfa-a56e-4b79-a511-39f0d4428e8a&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
During the round, the admin and spectators will see in realtime which players will have completed the challenge.
> [!note] 
> Before the end of the round, the admin can drag and drop the yellow bar between players to decide how many of them will be selected for next round. When done, the admin clicks on End to finish the round.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F722095f4-695d-46b4-8484-2fc3e5852600%2FUntitled.png?table=block&id=2cf3e262-4e3c-4259-8b5c-d8e83fa6420c&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Repeat these steps for the following rounds, and once you reach the Semi-finals, you'll be able to showcase the players' attempts in realtime to all the spectators.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F04ba69d3-f207-41f0-972d-b1bac16043f2%2FScreenshot_2020-05-02_17.36.09.png?table=block&id=ce71b88c-c4aa-4b73-8d51-08e04f63fe5e&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1220&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
At the end of the competition, the fastest to complete the final challenge wins. Time to celebrate 🎉You may think of a prize to reward the winner 🏆​
