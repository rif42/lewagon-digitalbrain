## Your role & responsibilities 🚀​
---
1. **Staff and manage the batch calendar**
        1. Reach out to existing TAs and Teachers and schedule all days before the batch start
        1. Manage the calendar in case of cancellations or when adjustments are needed
1. **Run the bootcamp**
        1. Be the point of contact for students during the batch (co-led with Bootcamp Ops)
        1. Ensure all students' computers are correctly setup on the first day
        1. Ensure and maintain a warm and welcoming environment for all students and teaching crew
        1. Oversee students' progress over the batch and react (challenge dashboard)
        1. Run the operations of non-typical days (reboot, quiz, pitch sessions)
        1. Ensure the students are ready for Demo Day
        1. Report to City Manager and feedback product challenges being faced
1. **Manage the teaching team**
        1. Be the point of contact for Teachers and wannabe TAs
        1. Give your support and onboard first time TAs
        1. Spot motivated TAs and potential Teachers and schedule 1:1s with teachers to identify up-skilling opportunities
        1. Share post batch feedback for Teachers (oral or written)
        1. Be there on first day for first-time lecturers and report to the Engineering Team / Bootcamp Ops / City Manager about the performance
        1. Participate in the Teacher Training
        1. Communicate important updates to freelance Teachers & TAs
1. **Teach**
        1. At least 40% of your time will be spent on teaching
        1. Ensure project teams are formed correctly and are guided through project weeks, acting as Project Manager [overseeing progress of all project teams]
        1. Produce & share content/documentation for students & teachers (e.g. cheatsheets, training material/resources, challenge guidance)
        1. Ad-hoc support, as the batch needs (teaching, TAing, TA support)
1. Collect Reviews
        1.
