- **Objective 1****:** Discover how effective were the new communication initiatives
- **Objective 2:**** **Identify pain for next semester’s roadmap

### Learnings & Recommendations
1. Address the bottle neck communication gap between Batch managers and global initiatives.
        1. Direct communication with Batch Managers - Open Desks?
1. Open Desks is highly effective for general communication, but needs to have more structure.
1. Our current process for exposing new initiatives is working, we need to document it and apply it to Batch Managers.
