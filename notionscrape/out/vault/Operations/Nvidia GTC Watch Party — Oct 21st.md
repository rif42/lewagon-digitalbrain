# About the project
### Context & Problem
Nvidia selected Le Wagon to host its GTC Watch Party events across Europe as part of its global GTC conference series. This is our second activation with Nvidia (after the May round table), and a strategic opportunity to deepen our partnership with one of the most influential companies in AI. For Le Wagon, being the partner Nvidia chooses to broadcast Jensen Huang's flagship keynote sends a clear market signal: **Le Wagon = the AI education reference in Europe.**
The challenge: accessing a B2B audience (founders, CTOs, AI developers, data scientists) that is notoriously hard to engage through traditional marketing. This audience does not respond to standard events — but they come for Nvidia GTC.
### The project
Le Wagon opens its campuses in **4 cities on October 21st** to co-host Nvidia's GTC Watch Party: a 2-hour broadcast of Jensen Huang's keynote, followed by networking. No speakers to source, no content to produce. One week later, a **virtual AMA with Nvidia experts** takes place online with the same audience.

# Why it's a win for Le Wagon
1. **Low-cost lead gen** — 4 cities, 4 events, venue cost = 0. No speakers to book, no content to produce. Bonus: LinkedIn earned media with zero ad spend.
1. **Brand positioning** — Being the partner Nvidia chooses to host its flagship keynote positions Le Wagon as an ecosystem player, not just a training school.
1. **A hard-to-reach audience that comes to us** — Founders, CTOs, AI developers: notoriously hard to engage. Here, they register themselves. We own the data via Luma.
1. **Partnership momentum** — 2nd activation with Nvidia after the May round table. Opens the door to a joint founders event, deeper B2B collaboration, and co-branded offers.
