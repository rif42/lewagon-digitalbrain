# How to request extra editing support
> [!note] 
> **Elena Bonhomme (graphic designer)
→ **[elena.bonhomme@lewagon.org](mailto:elena.bonhomme@lewagon.org)
### **🕰️ When to request help?**
- If your raw footage needs polishing beyond quick edits.
- For branding-heavy projects (custom overlays, animations).
- For special campaigns, ads, or www. content.
### **👋 How to request?**
1. Send email or Slack message to Elena.
1. Provide:
        - Link to raw video files.
        - Deadline.
        - Purpose of video (social ad, testimonial, etc.).
        - Any specific requests (captions, format, animations).
1. Elena will confirm feasibility + timeline.
> [!note] 
> **Pro tip:** Send requests at least **5 working days before deadline**.
