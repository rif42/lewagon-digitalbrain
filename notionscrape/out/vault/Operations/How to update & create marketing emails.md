# How to update & create marketing emails
> [!note] 
> This document intents to give standard practices for all the marketing emails sent from/to our EU regions/leads.
## 0. Context
---
Until now all the marketing emails were being sent from [contact@lewagon.org](mailto:contact@lewagon.org) or through dynamic fields as {{contact.owner}} .
This practice was making that **when a lead responded directly to a marketing email, the wrong people were receiving this email**, instead of the region sales associate. So we were not being able to track if all the leads were receiving a response and if the right owners were being notified of these emails.
A new process was created (with @Marta Santos) so that we have:
- Better segmentation
- Better management of the different regions.
## 1. What is changing?
---
For the marketing leads and managers this will not change a lot or add any extra steps when creating a marketing email 🙌 you will only change your habit of email creation ! 😀
Check how 👉 [[How to update & create marketing emails|3. How to update/create your emails now ?]]
## 2. Who will be impacted by these changes?
---
Regions below 👇🏻​
## 3. How to update/create your emails now ?
---
The CRM team already updated all the automated emails that were sent from different workflows to change the from address and in some cases the reply to address to the new listed.
> [!note] 
> For the emails where the reply to address wasn’t [contact@lewagon.org](mailto:contact@lewagon.org) we didn’t change the reply address, so do not hesitate to check your emails if changes are needed !
