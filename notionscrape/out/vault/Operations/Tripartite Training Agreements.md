# Tripartite Training Agreements
## Context
At the moment, after the assessment phase, we’re sending our applicants training agreements to be signed on DocuSign. More and more students have arrangements w/ their employer to pay the bootcamp on their behalf, and our contracts were not addressing this use case well, since the training agreement contains clauses that must be agreed on by the **student** specifically, regardless of the payer. On top of that, there was no ground for liability on the part of the company in case it did not meet its financial obligations.
## Solution
We’ve created a new contract template for this scenario, where a single contract should be signed by the three parties: **Le Wagon**, the **student**, and the **company**.
> [!note] 
> This is applicable to applicants financed by their **employer or a corporation**, not by the government or any other financing company / solution.
To make use of this new template, head over to an applicant’s profile on Kitt.
### Creating a tripartite contract
When a student lets you know that they will pay all or part of their tuition with the help of their employer, you can start by retrieving the name of the **legal representative of the Company** to be able to Add a third party signatory
In order to go through with this step, a tripartite contract template needs to be available in Kitt so it can be used later on. If there are none, you can go ahead and create one yourself by following the instructions.

The introduction of a new template means that some additional variables have been added to help you save time when issuing your contracts. It’s important to make good use of the various variables at hand in the designated areas of the contract.

### Sending the contract
When the company details are filled, you can Send contract through the ATS, and the applicant will receive a DocuSign link to sign it. Once it’s been signed, it will be available in the ATS on the applicant’s profile as usual.
You will now need to download that contract by clicking on it and opening it in a new tab, so you can then send it to the company by e-mail 👇​
