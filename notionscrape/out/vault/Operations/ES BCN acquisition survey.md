# [ES] BCN acquisition survey
# About the project
---
### Context & Problem
> [!note] 
> With growing competition in tech education, we needed to better understand **how students in Barcelona discover Le Wagon**, what drives their decision to apply, and how long that journey takes. This insight gap was preventing us from optimizing our local acquisition strategy and effectively competing with alternatives like IronHack, Codeworks, and General Assembly.
### The project
> [!note] 
> Conduct a comprehensive **survey of Barcelona bootcamp students in May 2025** to analyze their discovery journey, decision-making factors, and application timeline. The project involved survey design, data collection from 18 students, analysis of findings, and development of three distinct audience personas with actionable recommendations for refining our local acquisition strategy.
