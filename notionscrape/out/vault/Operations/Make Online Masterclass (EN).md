### Context & Problem
***Mandatory to create the idea.**** What is the challenge or pain point this project comes to solve?*
> [!note] 
> Le Wagon needs to expand its partnership with Make and reach an international English-speaking audience interested in no-code automation tools. An online event in English offers the opportunity to generate leads beyond French-speaking markets and showcase practical use cases combining Make with data skills. Goal is to promote our new AI & Data automation skill-course.
### The project
***Mandatory to move in backlog.**** What does the project look like? What is the expected outcome?*

The project includes teacher coordination (proposal already submitted to Orlane), Learn platform integration, landing page creation, pre-event communication coordination with Diana, and technical infrastructure setup (Hubspot/Livestorm - ticket #9853).
