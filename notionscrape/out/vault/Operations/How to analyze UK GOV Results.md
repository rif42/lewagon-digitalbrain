# How to analyze UK GOV Results
> [!note] 
> **Intro**
# Where you can find all data?
### Part 1: The number of lead who submitted the eligibility checker on the Landing page
To get the right number, you can check directly this list [UKGOV 25 | All Form Submission](https://app.hubspot.com/contacts/4419217/objectLists/34985/filters)
Then if you want to split by leads eligible and not eligible use these
- Eligible List : [UKGOV 25 | Eligible only leads ](https://app.hubspot.com/contacts/4419217/objectLists/34988/filters)
- Non-eligible Leads : [UKGOV 25 | Non-eligible leads](https://app.hubspot.com/contacts/4419217/objectLists/34986/filters)
Want to use date? Please follow this.
- For Eligible Leads you can :
        - Group them by editions by adding the Partnership edition on **Deals**
        - Or using the Date Creation on **Deals**
- For Non-Eligible Leads you need to :
        - Refine the form submission by date
            > [!video]
As the date of creation can not be used on contacts as some of them were created before the event and it’s not the same feature as on Deals, which are created only when the UK GOV start
### Part 2 - About the number of deals created for eligible UK GOV Leads
*Note:(reminder: the Scholargov pipeline is from eligible step to application submitted in the UK GOV use case)*
- Go on this dedicated view : <https://app.hubspot.com/contacts/4419217/objects/0-3/views/45295838/list>
    ![](https://app.notion.com/image/attachment%3A9fa97a33-2ebb-46c3-b8e5-139e2e14c93d%3Aimage.png?table=block&id=1a08924a-f014-80ca-8099-ff1480263d41&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1330&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
- Now, want in depth in data, use filters you can see and if you **click in “more**” just near to “advanced filter”, you will access to main filters to use:
        - [Partnership] Edition (ex: UK GOV | Q4 2024, UK GOV Q1 2025 etc.)
        - [Partnership] Eligibility Checker (ex: UK GOV Eligible B2C)
        - Program ( values: web, data_nalytics, data)
        - Deal stage. More details:
                    - **The Deal stages of UK GOV BID**
                                    - Eligibility Checker (ScholarGov ) = They submitted the eligibilty checker form
                                    - **Assessment Open (ScholarGov ) = The lead is eligible and received the assessment **
                                    - **Waiting CV Grade (ScholarGov )**.= They did the assessment and got a grade > 2
                                    - **Application Open (ScholarGov )**.= They got their **Motivation by Sales known **so they got called by the sales
                                    - **GOV] Go (ScholarGov )**. = The lead Applied via the dedicated apply form id 372
            ![](https://app.notion.com/image/attachment%3A44368182-775e-4b5f-97fc-e3298bdf5922%3Aimage.png?table=block&id=1a08924a-f014-80cb-a993-c672e69f1519&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1260&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
