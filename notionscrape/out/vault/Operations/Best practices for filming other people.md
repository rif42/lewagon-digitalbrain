# Best practices for filming other people
## **👥 Angles, framing, lighting, settings (interview style, testimonials).
**
### **Framing:**
- Use the **rule of thirds** → subject slightly off-center.
- For interviews: keep shoulders & head visible (medium close-up).
- Maintain eye-level perspective.
### **Angles:**
- Avoid shooting from below (unflattering).
- Slight angle = natural, friendly.
### **Lighting:**
- Place subject facing light source.
- Avoid harsh shadows across the face.
