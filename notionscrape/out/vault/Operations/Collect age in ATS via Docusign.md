# Collect age in ATS via Docusign
## Context
The OPS team needs to know if a candidate will be of legal age for the bootcamp during the interview, but without asking the applicant's age bluntly to avoid pushback.
## Solution
Break down this process into two parts.
First, ask if the user will be of legal age by the start of the bootcamp during the Interview process, a simple yes/no question.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F0aaddb86-895c-4b53-91c0-6224078c80c4%2FScreenshot_2023-01-10_at_17.00.33.png?table=block&id=ba7d17f7-1c12-4127-aa20-18768b83fe88&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Second, ask the applicant for their age when they sign the docusign contract.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F84f10dba-ae21-4c4d-ad81-f51a547b03b0%2Fdocusign_new_field.jpg?table=block&id=57257bf6-1288-4087-b077-4b1aa4f0ca84&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Both these steps should provide enough information with minimum pushback from the applicant.
> [!note] 
> Don't forget to add the $TRAINEE_AGE_INPUT age variable in your Contract Templates to activate the feature!
