# Team CRM & Ops’s OKRs
> [!note] 
> OKRs, It is a framework for defining and tracking business objectives and their outcomes.
# Objectives 🚀​
---
### Write the Objectives
Objectives are what the CRM team, or individual wants to accomplish:
- Qualitative or subjective, ambitious (should feel somewhat uncomfortable), and time-bound.
- Use expressions that convey endpoints and states (e.g. "Improve the CVR Lead to Apply by X")
- Avoid expressions that don’t push for new achievements (e.g. "keep hiring")
# Key Results 🎯​
---
### Write the Key Results
- 3-4 key results per objective.
- Key results are measurable milestones that contribute to the completion of the objective.
- Key results **should describe outcomes, not activities**.
> [!note] 
> If the KRs include words like "help" or "analyze" they’re describing activities. Instead, describe the impact of these activities: "Publish ads dashboard by March 31st.” rather than "Analyze ads impact".
### M**easure the Key Results**
Key results are measurable and should be easy to grade with a number. Google uses a grade from 0 to 1 but you can use a scale from 0 to 100% to monitor progress.
> [!note] 
> Note that the “sweet spot” for an OKR grade **is 60% – 70%.** If someone consistently fully attains their objectives, their OKRs aren’t ambitious enough. And again, **OKRs are not synonymous with employee evaluations**.
