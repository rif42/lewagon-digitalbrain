# Allow teachers to cancel / update pending invoices
## Context
Teachers previously encountered a time-consuming process when mistakes were made on uploaded invoices. Any errors in VAT rates, invoice numbers, payment references, or PDF files required contacting the Operations team to manually reject and recreate the entire invoice, creating delays and inefficiencies in the payment workflow.
## Solution
Teachers can now access a comprehensive editing and deleting interface directly from their invoice dashboard when invoices are in **sent** status, eliminating the need for Operations team intervention.
New **Edit and Delete** icons appears next to invoices with sent status 👇​
![](https://app.notion.com/image/attachment%3A33c2a9ca-e331-4744-87fc-2a3914fab3ca%3AScreenshot_2025-10-29_at_10.28.05.png?table=block&id=2998924a-f014-80aa-9c50-ebdbc45d7a07&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
> [!note] 
> The delete action is only available for invoices not linked to a purchase order.
Clicking the **edit** icon displays a modal containing all editable invoice fields:
- VAT rate
- Invoice number
- Payment reference
- PDF file
![](https://app.notion.com/image/attachment%3A4dc7e970-d579-4100-bf00-b91575d67e5e%3AScreenshot_2025-10-29_at_10.35.58.png?table=block&id=2998924a-f014-806a-9607-d1cb79f26128&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
> [!note] 
> Selected days are not among the updatable fields, in case of mistake teachers should re-create an invoice.
