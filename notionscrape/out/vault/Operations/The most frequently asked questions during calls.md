# The most frequently asked questions during calls
**Fundings / Price : **
- Fundings : what funding and how to get it - deadline? Can there be any refusals?
- What is the price of the training ?
- Who do I need to contact to make the financing process?
- Is it possible to pay in several times?
**End of training :**
- Next training dates ?
        - Is there a final assessment?
- Certification ? What happens if we miss it? is it included in the price of the training?
- Job opportunities? - How long does it take to find a job?
- How quickly do your students find a job?
- Follow-up / help to find a job post wagon ? - partner companies ?
- Can we do an internship ?
**Admissions process :**
- admissions process - video or onsite interview / how long does the admissions process take?
- How many students per program?
- pre-requisites? if I don't do Python, can I get the level by just working on the prepwork?
- how does the full time classroom experience go with covid?
- do we need to know how to code for web training?
- equipment ? computer required ?
- what is the purpose of the preparatory work?
- What questions can I be asked during the interview?
**Training : **
- How the days are run, pedagogy?
- Do we have to work evenings at home?
- difference between PT and FT ?
- Why Ruby ?
- do we make web or mobile applications?
- possible to focus on a particular subject: e.g., web: only product, data only AI?
- same program in the cities? Or, does it change from city to city?
- Web: hard to get in if you haven't done any programming? A lot of dropouts?
