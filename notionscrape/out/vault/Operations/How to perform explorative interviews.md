# How to perform explorative interviews
### Do’s and Don’ts in asking quesitons
---
1️⃣ **Rule #1: Ask open ended questions**
Always ask open ended questions to get as many data points as possible.
2️⃣ **Rule #2: Don’t ask binary questions**
**3️⃣ Rule #3: Don’t ask hypothetical questions**
4️⃣ **Rule #4: Don’t ask leading questions**
Don’t ask questions taht lead towards the truth that you want. it will give you wrong data.
5️⃣ **Rule 5: Don’t put the interviewee in akward situation**
Asking them if they like something you did might force them to lie to you. Like answering to a kid if their drawing looks good. This would give you false data.
***⚠️ General tip: ****Use this sentence to get more qualitative data points from your questions. a good interview is the the one that gets off track.*
🤨 ***“That’s interesting tell me more”***
