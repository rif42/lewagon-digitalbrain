### AI at Le Wagon
---
- **Since 2018 Le Wagon has been training students in Machine Learning and AI** with our Data Science & AI Bootcamp (advanced training).
        - With success stories from alumni, some of whom hold Data Scientist positions in the world's biggest Tech companies like Google.
- **From 2023**, Le Wagon will develop and integrate Wott, an AI assistant, into its teaching method. An AI assistant that helps and guides students, without ever giving them the solution, complementing teachers for enhanced support.
- **In 2024, Le Wagon introduced a new AI component to its historic Web Development program**, to enable students to be more productive and create web and AI applications from scratch. This update guarantees students a comprehensive, hands-on experience in web development and AI, preparing them to succeed in a world increasingly focused on this technology.
        - Use of Copilot to code more efficiently.
        - Integration of the OpenAI API to add AI functionality to web applications and implementation of RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) to improve AI accuracy.
**In short, AI at the Wagon means:**
- **A learning topic** integrated into all our curricula, either as specific components or as a raison d'être (notably in our Data Science & AI training).
- **An ****e****ducational tool **that enhances our teaching methods and enriches our students' learning experience.
- **An efficiency lever **to optimize our operations, as many companies are doing today.
Our training courses have several main objectives for their learners:
1. **Learn about technology**: Acquire technical and technological skills.
1. **Adopt AI**: Integrate and use artificial intelligence technologies.
1. **Go beyond Generative AI**: Don't limit yourself to the basic applications of generative AI, but explore more advanced and specific uses.
1. **Apply this to your career**: Apply these skills and knowledge to your professional life.
**Le Wagon aims to train professionals capable of mastering current technologies, including AI, and making advanced use of them in their careers**.
### Topics you don't want to talk about and how you respond
---
- business / sales figures?
⇒ Our main focus is on the quality of our training and the impact we have on our students and alumni. Our priority is to continue to offer high-quality programs and adapt to the changing needs of the market. We are proud of our international expansion and our ability to innovate, which is reflected in the satisfaction of our students and partners.
- next fundraising round?
⇒ Not at the moment. For now, we're focusing on innovation and improving our existing offerings, to continue to adapt to meet the changing needs of the market and our students.
- campus closures?
⇒ We are indeed seeing growing interest in our distance learning formats, offering greater flexibility and better adapting to our students' personal constraints. This trend also reflects the growth in telecommuting among employees. As a result, we are rethinking the value and use of our campuses in certain countries or regions to better meet the evolving needs of our community.
- difficulty of competitors (Wild Code School)?
⇒ The diversity of our activities, including B2B and B2U segments in addition to B2C, as well as our global footprint, make our situation different from that of some competitors. We continue to innovate and adapt to meet the changing needs of the market and our students.
- Employment rate
⇒ All our employability reports are available on our website and on the France Compétences pages of our RNCP title in the specific case of France. I invite you to consult them directly. We don't produce, nor do we communicate, an overall average, quite simply because these are very important indicators for us, which we monitor by program and by country.
