# Huntr Talent Pool
Huntr is an online platform designed to assist job seekers in managing their job search activities effectively. It serves as a job application tracking system, helping users organise and track the various stages of their job applications, interviews, and networking efforts. Huntr aims to streamline the often complex and challenging process of job hunting by providing tools for organisation and collaboration.
