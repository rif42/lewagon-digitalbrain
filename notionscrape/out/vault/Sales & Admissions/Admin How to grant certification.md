# Admin: How to grant certification?
Here is the process to follow to grant certification to a candidate through [ActiveAdmin](https://assess.lewagon.com/admin).
## Grant the missing blocks to the candidate
---
Go on <https://assess.lewagon.com/admin> and click on Granted Blocks in the top menu.
Search the candidate by id, first_name or last_name and check that they have all the block granted.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F9e93a43b-8779-4732-9def-d1bec9dfe58d%2FScreenshot_2023-04-11_at_10.02.31.png?table=block&id=4c87a737-70a9-4df1-9900-290780d92046&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
If all the block are present, but some are not granted click on edit update the validated value.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fe96038cf-2e15-44b0-861f-c48583cd817f%2FScreenshot_2023-04-11_at_10.04.49.png?table=block&id=f5cf8119-023b-430c-8d03-b06abb3902d7&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
If some blocks are missing, click on New Granted Block on the top right of the page.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F7f9e9909-a1cf-4fe1-819b-8ed990c3a6ad%2FScreenshot_2023-04-11_at_10.09.40.png?table=block&id=e002ff1e-2996-4085-98db-468bb943f043&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Select the candidate, the block and don’t forget to tick the validated checkbox to create a new granted block.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F74d7d264-474a-4159-b384-d5a0aae907bb%2FScreenshot_2023-04-11_at_10.10.41.png?table=block&id=9c27c031-c9e5-4308-a9a8-3f7bb5668e40&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)

## Certify the candidate (Bootcamps certifications only)
---
> [!note] 
> **BE CAREFUL**: this procedure only apply for RNCP exam sessions that validates all the blocks, **not for those validating a subset of blocks** (like Skill Courses exams).
Students validating only a subset of blocks **aren’t consider as certified**.
Once all the block are granted to the candidate, click on Candidates in the top menu.
Search the candidate by id, first_name or last_name and click on edit.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F05d92a8b-d222-4637-a485-3f6484e9c8ae%2FScreenshot_2023-04-11_at_10.34.03.png?table=block&id=71318ca9-72a3-45f6-9d7e-89630c4fbfec&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
Tick the certified checkbox and update the candidate to grant the certification.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fdf8b86bd-b83a-4c3f-a9d0-2a84c118f2d5%2FScreenshot_2023-04-11_at_10.35.46.png?table=block&id=dffb5427-b8f2-4bfd-9789-b5b74cd54e0a&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
## Generate the certification document
---
Once the block granted and the candidate certified, you can generate the certification document.
Click on Candidates in the top menu, select the candidates for which you want to generate or re-generate the certification document.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F19f2a976-333f-4371-bfba-3b6e0b0bb861%2FScreenshot_2023-04-11_at_10.37.15.png?table=block&id=de96fd1a-591e-437d-9007-62fc06ec4ca6&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
When all the candidate are selected, click on Generate certification document for Selected in the Bach Actions dropdown.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F0f434d08-0651-4891-bc50-fd7b4af90912%2FScreenshot_2023-04-11_at_10.38.10.png?table=block&id=271be025-6d99-4934-8937-ea1a1e9a7c32&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
