# How to Process Affiliation Referrals
## **Program Overview**
---
> [!note] 
> A commission-based affiliate marketing program that rewards partners for referring students to Le Wagon bootcamps. Partners promote Le Wagon to their communities and earn a commission per student who successfully finishes a bootcamp.

## **Who Can Be a Partner?**
---
- Tech communities, networking groups, developer communities.
- Companies seeking tech training for employees or clients.
- Public institutions aiming to promote tech skills training.
- Organizations looking to provide educational opportunities.
- Educational institutions
- Co-working spaces, incubators, accelerators
- Agencies focused on tech recruitment.
- Freelancer platforms (Upwork, Malt, Fiverr…)
- Social media influencers, bloggers, vloggers, and podcast hosts.
## **Partner Identification & Agreement**
---
Clearly outline and validate the following elements when establishing a partnership agreement:
- **Eligible Programs**
Bootcamps only. Specify eligible programs: Data Analytics, Data Engineering, Data Science & AI, Web Development.
- **Duration and renewal terms
**Set initial partnership length and conditions for renewal.
- **Marketing and promotional obligations
**Clarify any promotional expectations, channels, and frequency of communication.
- **Data and privacy compliance**
Ensure GDPR compliance and secure handling of partner data. Set Terms and Conditions.
- **Reward Structure
**Partners choose their commission and if they will provide discounts to their community. The total value is €1,000 — clearly detail rewards for partner and/or community discount. Example of splits:
    Partner CommissionCommunity Discount€1,000€0€900€100€850€150€800€200€750€250
- **Payment**: Define timelines for reward distribution: after each validated referral, monthly, quarterly annually.
