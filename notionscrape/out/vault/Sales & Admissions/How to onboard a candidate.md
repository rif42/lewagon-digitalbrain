# How to onboard a candidate?
Now that your jurors are onboard, let’s invite candidates to the certification exam.
> [!note] 
> **If you didn’t invite any jurors yet, please** [[How to onboard a juror|go back to the previous step]].
## Summary
---
- Send invitation to candidates
- What’s happening on candidates side?
- Accepting candidates
- Convocation
- Rejecting candidates
## Send invitation to candidates
---
Copy the link from the session page before pasting it into your dedicated Slack channel gathering all the candidates. Wait for them to pop in the Candidates tab.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Faf2a1686-7eeb-4ef2-9d89-56d18216c807%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_11.13.59.png?table=block&id=3dfafa6c-66d8-4211-9570-439243afa82b&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
## What’s happening on the candidate side?
---
The link will bring them to the exam sign up page. Based on your [[How to create an exam session|exam session settings]], they will be able to sign in with Kitt or/and with email.
**Signin with kitt**
---
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F6c9ebcb9-6225-4e12-81e1-5f1c0bd1e267%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_11.16.22.png?table=block&id=7430353a-5db4-49a3-9db4-c9ba83fb72c9&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
The candidates will then be redirected to the exam session page informing them of the start date of the exam or to a waiting page based on the Auto-accept candidate setting. (See bellow)
> [!note] 
> If the candidate sign up **for the first time**, he/she will be prompt to fulfil the extra informations (birthdate or birthplace) required by the RNCP before getting redirected to the exam session page.
## Accepting candidates
---
Based on your setting this step can be fully automated or required some actions of your part.
### Option 1: Auto-accept candidates
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2F1020c992-0403-4f7c-899e-c75dde974e43%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_12.06.17.png?table=block&id=cbe9d563-dbbf-48e2-bbbd-0267034035ad&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
If you choose to auto-accept candidates and enabled email notifications, each time a candidate join the session the following email will be delivered to the exam session manager.
```
Bonjour [your name],
[candidate full name] ([candidate email]) à rejoint la session [exam session name].
Vous pouvez désactiver son accès en cliquant sur l'option 'Rejeter' dans la colonneActionsdu candidat si cette personne n'est pas censée participer à cette session.
Cordialement,
L'équipe du Wagon​
```
On their side, candidates will be redirected to the exam session page displaying **only the exam start date** if your are running a sequential exam or** the complete list of challenges** if you are running a non-sequential exam.
### Option 2: Accept candidates manually
If you choose to accept candidates manually they will be redirected to a temporary waiting page once they signed up.

## **Convocation**
---

## Rejecting candidates
---
As soon as a candidate has been accepted the **reject option** is available for you to use.
![](https://app.notion.com/image/https%3A%2F%2Fs3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fsecure.notion-static.com%2Fedf3e24a-e027-4d65-a3e2-25e4631642f8%2FScreenshot_2022-01-25_at_11.19.59.png?table=block&id=fbd737b0-d6c7-461d-a273-1234543a1f97&spaceId=2b6dea13-478f-4f00-aff1-afd11ed5a03f&width=1410&userId=342d872b-594c-817d-8145-000204a2c537&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=requestProxiedImageUrl)
