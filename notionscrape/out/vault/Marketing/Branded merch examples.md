# Branded merch examples
## **General guidelines**
- Prioritize sustainable, organic, and ethically sourced goodies whenever possible
        -
- Only order the quantities you need, avoid waste or leftover stock
        -
- Always request a sample or first impression, most suppliers offer this free or at a low cost
        -
## **Merch use cases **
> [!note] 
> **Internal vs. external use
**Clearly define whether the merch is for **external use** (ex: events, fairs, partnerships) or **internal use** (ex: students, Wagoners).
