# Detailed Persona & Messaging Guide
## Career Changers
**Detailed Profile:**
- Age: 25-45
- Background: Various non-technical professions
- Motivation: Career transition into tech
- Goal: Land first tech role
**Pain Points:**
1. Need for technical skills to transition careers
1. Desire to stay relevant in AI-driven job market
1. Lack of prior coding experience
1. Need for practical, hands-on learning
1. Concerns about job prospects
**Key Messages by Stage:**
*Awareness:*
- "Transform your career in 9 weeks with both coding and AI skills"
- "No prior tech experience needed - start from scratch"
- "86% employment rate within 6 months"
*Consideration:*
- "Comprehensive program combining web development and AI"
- "Learn by building real projects from day one"
- "Access to 30,000+ alumni network"
*Decision:*
- "Strong career support with personalized coaching"
- "Portfolio of AI-powered projects to show employers"
- "Access to 1,000+ hiring partner companies"
## Tech Entrepreneurs
**Detailed Profile:**
- Age: 20-50
- Background: Business, product, or domain expertise
- Motivation: Launch tech startup
- Goal: Build MVP independently
**Pain Points:**
1. Can't build products independently
1. Dependent on technical co-founders
1. High development costs
1. Need to validate ideas quickly
1. Want to understand technical feasibility
**Key Messages by Stage:**
*Awareness:*
- "Build your AI-powered MVP in 9 weeks"
- "From idea to working product - no technical co-founder needed"
- "Join 222+ successful startup founders"
*Consideration:*
- "Learn to integrate modern AI tools and APIs"
- "Hands-on experience building real products"
- "Technical autonomy to iterate quickly"
*Decision:*
- "Complete toolkit for launching tech products"
- "Join a community of tech founders"
- "Save thousands in development costs"
## Professional Up-skillers
**Detailed Profile:**
- Age: 25-45
- Background: Marketing, Product, Sales, Operations
- Motivation: Add technical capabilities
- Goal: Enhanced current role/career
**Pain Points:**
1. Limited by basic tool usage
1. Dependent on dev teams
1. Communication gap with technical teams
1. Need to drive innovation
1. Want to stay competitive
**Key Messages by Stage:**
*Awareness:*
- "Add powerful technical capabilities to your current role"
- "Bridge the business-technical gap"
- "Future-proof your career with AI skills"
*Consideration:*
- "Build and prototype solutions independently"
- "Automate workflows and increase productivity"
- "Understand and implement AI features"
*Decision:*
- "Immediate application to your current role"
- "Lead technical initiatives confidently"
- "Join a network of tech-enabled professionals"
## Recent Graduates
**Detailed Profile:**
- Age: 21-28
- Background: Various academic fields
- Motivation: Start tech career
- Goal: Land first tech role
**Pain Points:**
1. Need practical experience
1. Want to stand out to employers
1. Looking for clear career path
1. Need industry-relevant skills
1. Want quick entry into tech
**Key Messages by Stage:**
*Awareness:*
- "Start your tech career with cutting-edge skills"
- "Stand out with both coding and AI capabilities"
- "Clear path to junior developer positions"
*Consideration:*
- "Build real projects for your portfolio"
- "Learn the exact skills companies are hiring for"
- "Join a global community of tech professionals"
*Decision:*
- "Access to 1,000+ hiring partners"
- "Career support and job preparation"
- "Industry-recognized certification"
## Sales Conversation Starters by Persona
### Career Changers
- "What inspired you to consider transitioning into tech?"
- "What type of role do you see yourself in after the bootcamp?"
- "How important is job support in your decision?"
### Tech Entrepreneurs
- "Tell me about the product you want to build"
- "What's holding you back from launching your MVP?"
- "How much are you currently spending on development?"
### Professional Up-skillers
- "What technical challenges do you face in your current role?"
- "How much time do you spend waiting for dev team support?"
- "What would you automate if you had the technical skills?"
### Recent Graduates
- "What attracts you to a career in tech?"
- "What kind of projects would you like to work on?"
- "How do you want to stand out to employers?"
