# **Q2 OVERVIEW
**
> [!note] 
> **2,352 students in Q1+Q2+Q3 (80% target)** (1573 students in H1. 92% of the target was reached.)**
****
Q1: **Strong Q1 quarter in students (99% students achieved)
**Q2: **Okay Q2 quarter (88% of students achieved) with tangible signs of acceleration.
**Q3**: Bad Q3 quarter, missing targets (73-76% of students achieved)
Overall, Q2 was a tough quarter for us as a team. We didn’t manage to deliver the big projects we needed to deliver on time due to a lack of resources and it shows in our actuals. We realized this lack of resources and delay in delivery mid-quarter and course corrected for CRM and Brand. As a team, we need to deliver our roadmap on time in Q3-Q4 and be laser-focused on what matters most - if we want to hit the targets in Q4 aka Website & CRM roadmap.
Q3 was a great quarter for lead generation (we can scale) with a steady increase Quarter over Quarter in leads. Our conversion rates in the funnel haven’t improved but once it does, we’re set for success.
**Brand-wise: **we launched our brand identity but we didn’t launch our website on time (lack of design resources + engineering solution finding). We launched the first good-looking brand campaign that was successful vs target + CPL ([destination bootcamp](https://docs.google.com/presentation/u/1/d/1M_pBs9dctEPZBdMC1k7VWtidNXJPkWcSaCwEyKf-5Wc/edit#slide=id.g134805ba58b_0_0)). We communicated Emil’s launch.
**CRM-wise: **our roadmap is delayed by a quarter but we managed to build a team around us to accelerate the delivery of our critical initiatives by September. We helped B2B get set up and Emil gets set up. Qualifying our leads and nurturing them the right way is our #1 priority as a global marketing team along with the launch of our website.
**Event-wise**: we launched [11 new workshops](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1QxgsbUG_1eAV9yfMmdGFZjinknjpLAhTBW-NRAnv38c/edit#slide=id.g136d6cc24b9_0_128). Some work better than old ones. Still need to analyze the impact of events on conversion to have the full picture and to know what to launch next.
**Paid-wise: **we restructured Paid search campaigns and launched LinkedIn + retargeting. We achieved a lot this quarter. We need to diversify our sources in Q3-Q4 by finding CPA deals.
