## 💬 Qualitative analysis
---
### 🔹 Feedback themes all programs combined
❤️ **Most liked commun themes across cities and bootcamps**
- **Community and Team spirit** - Students highly value the collaborative and social environment.
- **Learning experience** - Many appreciate the challenge and pace of the bootcamp, finding it engaging.
- **Teachers and Support** - Students appreciate the support staff
**Sample of student comments :**
> *“I loved the combination of lectures and challenges”
> ”Exceptional support from instructors and staff. The team is always approachable, responsive, and genuinely invested in helping students succeed.”
> ” I really liked the supportive atmosphere”*
❌ **Least liked commun themes across cities and bootcamps **
- **Organisation and timing issues **- Some students found parts of the bootcamp disorganised or overly fast-paced
- **Content concerns **- A few expressed concerns about course content balance, particularly around AI topics
- **Structure & Delivery** - Some lectures or teaching methods were seen as unclear or repetitive.
