# Career Services Policy
### **1. Career Paths Overview**
**1.1. **Le Wagon’s career services are divided into three distinct paths: Jobseeker, Freelancer, and Founder.
**1.2. **Trainees are welcome to join one or more paths. The selection must be made at the beginning of the training through the submission of the career survey.
**1.3.** Access to the Freelancer and Founder paths becomes available to Trainees from the date the form is submitted.
### **2. Job Seeker Path Structure**
**2.1.** The Job Seeker path is divided into three main levels:
- **Level 1: The Basics**
        - Asynchronous content.
- **Level 2: Live Sessions**
        - Live collective coaching.
- **Level 3: 1:1 Support**
        - Live individual coaching.
**2.2. **To progress to consecutive levels, Trainees must complete a set of predefined assignments. Level 1 assignments need to be added to the Trainee’s workbook in Notion, which can be found in the Level 1 asynchronous content.
**2.3.** The completion of assignments is validated by the Trainee’s assigned Student Success Manager.
- To progress from Level 1 to Level 2, Trainees must notify and send their completed workbook to their assigned Student Success Manager via Slack message.
- To progress from Level 2 to Level 3, Trainees must notify their Student Success Manager that they have attended the four minimum mandatory Live Workshops.
**2.4 **Completion of mandatory assignments is required to access the full scope of career services offered.

Le Wagon reserves the right to cancel any workshop if fewer than 10 participants have signed up. If a workshop is cancelled, all registered participants will be promptly notified and will have the option to register for a future workshop.
### **4. Trainee Responsibilities**
**4.1.** While Le Wagon staff makes every effort to support Trainee success in the job market, the responsibility for outcomes ultimately rests with the Trainee. Career support is most effective when Trainees are proactive, engaged, and consistent in their job search.
4.2 Trainees are expected to complete required assignments, attend key sessions, and actively use the Huntr platform to remain visible to Hiring Partners.

### **7. Huntr Requirements**
**7.1.** All Job Seeker path participants are required to maintain their Huntr account according to the following minimum standards to ensure continued visibility to Hiring Partners:
- **Maintain Active Status**
        - Trainees must regularly apply to jobs and/or log in to Huntr.
        - Accounts showing no application or login activity for a full calendar month will be deactivated.
- **Complete Profile Requirements**
        - Huntr profiles must be fully filled out and professionally presented in order to remain visible to Hiring Partners.
To request profile reactivation, Trainees must contact their Career Advisor and resume Huntr activity.
### **8. Privacy and Data Protection**
**8.1.** When participating in the Job Seeker Program, Trainees have the option to share their profile with Le Wagon’s Hiring Partners via the Huntr platform. Trainees who do not want their profile shared must notify their Career Advisor and Marta Bas (Head of Careers) [marta.bas@lewagon.org](mailto:marta.bas@lewagon.org) about this fact.
### **9. General Provisions**
**9.1. **This policy is subject to change at the discretion of Le Wagon to better serve the needs of its Trainees.
**9.2. **Trainees will be notified of any changes to this policy in a timely manner.
**9.3. **The above-mentioned provisions might differ if the training provided is by a franchise partner of Le Wagon.
