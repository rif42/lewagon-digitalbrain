---
1. Use this [connected Google Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1kAOJo-vAG4YFxJDhctM9PQ3xdTn0IiIzIKYhIgtJh_0/edit?gid=1278932680#gid=1278932680) to find non-respondents and their contact info

    </details>
1. Create a **team-specific tab **for organising follow-ups and reminders
1. Implement a **reminder schedule**
    Our experience shows that sending weekly reminders through various channels immediately after the initial form distribution is the **most effective strategy to achieve the 75% **response rate, even in large cities like Paris.
