# Job Description 🚀​

---

As a **Global CRM Campaign Manager**, Diana will be in charge of re-engagement, activation, and transactional campaigns to engage and convert leads into students. She will play an essential role in copywriting and act as a subject matter expert, guiding Marketing Managers across different regions (APAC, LATAM, UK, SEMEA …) on using our CRM platform effectively.

Reporting to Le Wagon’s Head of CRM, she will be mainly responsible for:

- Designing and executing multi-touch campaigns to drive engagement and conversion

- Segmenting our user base to create tailored campaigns and improve the user experience

- Analyzing and improving campaign performance metrics

- Providing training and support to the Regional Marketing team to ensure effective use of our CRM platform (Hubspot)

# Key responsibilities 🔥​

---

**Copywriting & Marketing campaigns**

- Define new activation, re-engagement & transactional strategy with multi-touch campaigns (email, sms etc.).

- Create and maintain campaign copy, ensuring all messaging is on-brand and effective.

- Identify new segments for tailored campaigns and improve the user experience.

- Identifying opportunities for automation, creating workflows, and testing campaigns to ensure their effectiveness.

- Provide guidance on messaging and segmentation to Regional Marketing Team, ensuring campaigns are well executed with personalisation and that CRM (Hubspot) is well used at their level.

**Data Analysis and Reporting**

- Monitor and analyze campaign performance, using data to inform and optimize future campaigns.

- Analyze our customer journey to identify drop-offs and suggest solutions.

**Communication and Collaboration**

- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including marketing, sales, and customer service, to ensure seamless integration of CRM strategies. Manage their requests.

- Support the Regional Marketing manager team in the execution and follow-up of their campaigns - messaging, and segmentation.

- Stay up-to-date with industry trends and best practices, continually seeking ways to improve the performance and effectiveness of our CRM efforts.
    **She will work closely with our:**

        - **Regional Marketing Managers (spread all over the world)** to help them to create the right campaigns with the right audience for getting better results and to ensure that we are aligned on the CRM strategy.

        - **Brand Team** that is in charge of all creative assets production
