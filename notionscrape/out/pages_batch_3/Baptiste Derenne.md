# Baptiste Derenne

### JOB DESCRIPTION

As a Head of CRM, you will report to the Global Head of Growth and you will have to create and structure your team.

You will be responsible for the development and management of the CRM strategy for the main channels (emailing, notifications, messaging, etc.), with a focus on user engagement, activity and conversion.

You will also be responsible for the **consistency and quality of the data** in the CRM, and all related processes to make it usable by all Le Wagon’s teams.

**CRM is a key element in our global strategy**, both for the activation of our leads on our historical B2C activity, but also to create maximum synergies between our different teams (B2B, Career Team, B2U, B2C, etc.).

**Your work will therefore have a direct and major impact on the overall performance of Le Wagon.**

### YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES 🚀**
**

### Define, develop and ensure the implementation of the CRM strategy to achieve growth objectives.

- On all the customer lifecycle: from acquisition, activation and retention (with upsell and cross-sell strategies)

- On all channels (emails, SMS,…)

- On all countries (40 campuses in more than 20 countries)

- On all Business Units: B2C (Our historical bootcamps), New Products (New bootcamps and skill courses, B2B (Le Wagon for Business).

Our main CRM tool is Hubspot, for all teams, but you will have the **freedom to add new tools to enable you to implement your vision**, activate new channels, further enrich our CRM with internal and external data!

**You will work directly with a responsive and expert engineering team who fully understand the business challenges associated with CRM.**

### Responsible for the knowledge and segmentation of our leads and students database.

- Perfect understanding of our target needs, expectations and pain points across the customer journey.

- Identify and build virtuous paths to conversion and upsell.

- Conduct a competitor watch on CRM best practices and ensure their implementation.

- Optimize current automations and design new one to boost teams efficiency and lead’s activation

- Ability to collect data:
        - GDPR Compliant

        - And to reconcile them to unify the customer vision

**You will be able to rely on the expertise and skills of our in-house data team to implement your strategy and projects.**

### Performance monitoring.

- Definition and implementation of indicators and tools to manage and optimise the performance, and to ensure a healthy and growing database.

- Definition of the level of monitoring and reporting according to the strategy and challenges.

- Analysis and recommendations to take decisions according to the level of performance (POC, test & learn, adjustment, etc.)

### Management.

- Project management:
        - Content production, translation and localization to continuously optimize our campaigns according to the lifecycle of our prospects.

        - Set new tools, implement new features working closely with our tech team and local teams.

- Team management:
        - Recruit and manage a person who will complement your skills and help you deliver your projects and achieve your goals.

        - Define the mid and long term recruitment needs for the CRM team.

### **ABOUT YOU **💪

- You are a **clear and confident communicator,** with exceptional written and verbal **English language skills**

- You have at least** 5 years' of experience** in **marketing & CRM roles, and you love data!**

- A perfect knowledge of Hubspot is a strong plus.

- You have **international experience in CRM** and management of local customizations.

- Ability to coordinate internal and external, local and international resources

- You are a creative problem solver - **highly structured, organized, and have the ability to work to tight deadlines** to provide practical solutions on multiple projects

- You have **strong project management skills** and you are data-driven & **detail-oriented**

- You are **creative, hands-on and persistent** – you try everything!

- Ability to master advanced techniques and CRM best practices
